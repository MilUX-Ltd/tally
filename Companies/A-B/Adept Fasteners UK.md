# Adept Fasteners UK

> UK arm of a US aerospace fastener distributor, supplying structural and engine fasteners, hardware and installation tooling from a stocking site in Gateshead.

## What they do

Adept Fasteners is an aerospace structural and engine fastener distributor headquartered in Valencia, California, founded in 2001. The group carries a large stocked inventory for quick delivery and offers product lines including Boeing (BAC) and Airbus (AS) standard fasteners. Its UK operation, Adept Fasteners UK, works from Team Valley Trading Estate in Gateshead and serves UK and European customers. The company does not publish a site in Wales; it is listed as a member of Defence Wales.

## Products and solutions

- **Aerospace fasteners** - structural and engine fasteners, bearings and electronic hardware from stock.
- **Tooling** - installation tooling and tool repair.
- **Supply chain services** - long-term agreements, min-max and vendor-managed inventory programmes, just-in-time supply and kitting.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Accreditations:** AS9100 and AS9120 quality system registration; ISO 9001 (as stated by the group).
- **Memberships:** Defence Wales; Aerospace Wales Forum directory listing.
