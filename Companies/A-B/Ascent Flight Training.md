# Ascent Flight Training

> Military flying training provider delivering fixed wing, rotary wing and rear crew training for the UK Military Flying Training System.

## What they do

Ascent Flight Training states that it designs, develops and delivers training, technology products and technical services, and has done so since 2008. It is responsible for delivering military fixed and rotary wing aircrew training for the UK Military Flying Training System (UKMFTS) and international partners, employing over 420 staff across six UK sites and operating a fleet of 110 aircraft and over 100 simulators. Its RAF Cranwell team delivers fixed wing elementary, multi-engine and rear crew training, and its sites at RAF Cranwell, RAF Shawbury and RAF Valley are NATO Flight Training Europe accredited. It also offers human factors, crew resource management, dangerous goods and civilian pilot training.

## Products and solutions

- **Military flying training** - live and synthetic training for fixed wing, rotary wing and rear crew under UKMFTS.
- **Mission aircrew training** - ISTAR and rear crew training on modified King Air Avengers with ground mission trainers.
- **Turnkey training solutions** - training needs analysis, courseware design, infrastructure, aircraft, simulators, IT and staffing.
- **Human factors and crew resource management training** - training for aviation and other sectors.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Accreditations:** NATO Flight Training Europe accreditation at RAF Cranwell, RAF Shawbury and RAF Valley.
- **Memberships:** Greater Lincolnshire RDSC
