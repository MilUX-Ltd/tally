# Alan Wood & Partners

> Privately owned multi-disciplinary engineering consultancy with offices across Yorkshire, Lincolnshire and the North East.

## What they do

Alan Wood & Partners describes itself as one of the largest privately owned multi-disciplinary engineering consultancies in Yorkshire and Lincolnshire, established in 1968. It has offices in Hull, which is its head office, Leeds, Lincoln, Scarborough, Sheffield, Stockton-on-Tees and York, and states it has more than 100 technical staff. The consultancy works across sectors including commercial, heritage, education, energy, health and blue light, industrial, maritime, residential, transport and rail, and utilities.

## Products and solutions

- **Civil engineering** - civil engineering design.
- **Structural engineering** - structural design and assessment.
- **Building consultancy** - building surveying and consultancy services.
- **Geotechnical and geoenvironmental engineering** - ground investigation and assessment.
- **Project management** - management of construction projects.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Accreditations:** ISO 9001; ISO 14001; Cyber Essentials; Constructionline Gold; CHAS; Achilles UVDB; RISQS; SafeContractor.
- **Memberships:** Greater Lincolnshire RDSC
