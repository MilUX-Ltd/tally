# AAK Safety AS

> Supplier of fall protection and rescue equipment, including NATO-approved and tactical equipment for the Armed Forces and special forces.

## What they do

AAK Safety supplies fall protection, rope access and rescue equipment, with courses and annual inspection services. Its Armed Forces page lists NATO-approved fall protection, tactical climbing equipment and camouflage solutions from brands such as Petzl, KONG and CMC. It has supplied the defence and rescue sectors for over 35 years.

## Products and solutions

- **Products for the Armed Forces** - NATO-approved fall protection and tactical climbing gear
- **Helideck kit** - Helicopter deck equipment
