# Arcanum Cyber Security

> Cyber security consultancy based in Bedwas, Caerphilly, providing penetration testing, certification, governance, digital forensics and operational technology security services to government, defence and industry.

## What they do

Arcanum Cyber Security (legal name Arcanum Information Security Limited) is a cyber security consultancy formed in 2008 and based at Bedwas, near Caerphilly. It states that it supplies services to UK government and supports defence, manufacturing, space and critical national infrastructure customers, with particular experience supporting defence primes and their supply chains. Its services cover security testing, certification schemes, information security governance, data privacy and operational technology security. The company describes itself as an NCSC Assured Cyber Security Consultancy.

## Products and solutions

- **Penetration testing** - IT health checks, scenario-based testing, vulnerability scanning, configuration reviews and physical security surveys.
- **Cyber Essentials and Cyber Essentials Plus** - certification services as an IASME certification body.
- **Defence Cyber Certification (DCC)** - assessment support for the MOD and IASME scheme for the defence supply chain.
- **Information security governance** - ISO 27001 certification support, Cyber Assessment Framework, NIS Regulations, PCI DSS, security accreditation and business continuity planning.
- **Operational technology cyber security** - IEC 62443 assessments, OT network architecture reviews, audits and supply chain risk management.
- **Digital forensics** - forensic investigation and forensic readiness services.
- **Data privacy** - GDPR compliance, privacy assessments and outsourced data protection officer services.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Accreditations:** NCSC Assured Cyber Security Consultancy; CREST member; IASME Gold certified and IASME certification body; Cyber Essentials and Cyber Essentials Plus; ISO 9001; CAA ASSURE cyber supplier; Defence Employer Recognition Scheme Gold award.
