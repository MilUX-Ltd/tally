# Bernard Holmes Precision

> Family-run Lincolnshire subcontract CNC machining company producing precision components from prototypes to production runs.

## What they do

Bernard Holmes Precision Limited is a subcontract precision engineering company based in Billingborough, Lincolnshire. It states that it was incorporated in 1960 after trading since shortly after the Second World War, and that its CNC machining facility, purpose-built in 1995 and extended in 2007, covers 11,500 square feet. The company offers 5-axis machining, CNC turning, multi-axis milling and palletised machining in materials including aluminium, steel, titanium, stainless and duplex steels, Inconel, tungsten, castings and plastics. It describes itself as a family-run business.

## Products and solutions

- **5-axis machining** - complex components in fewer setups.
- **CNC turning** - precision-turned parts to tight tolerances.
- **Multi-axis CNC milling** - intricate milling operations.
- **Palletised machining** - unattended production for higher volumes.
- **Prototype and batch work** - from one-off prototypes to production runs.
