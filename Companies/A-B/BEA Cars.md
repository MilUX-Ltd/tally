# BEA Cars

> Family-run Fareham car hire company providing corporate travel, airport and cruise transfers, and wedding and prom cars.

## What they do

BEA Cars Ltd describes itself as a family-run business based in Stubbington, Fareham. It provides licensed private and corporate transport for private clients, companies and VIPs, with transfers to regional and London airports, cruise terminals and south coast ports, serving Southampton, Fareham, Portsmouth and the surrounding area. It also offers wedding car and prom services.

## Products and solutions

- **Corporate travel** - executive car hire for business travellers.
- **Airport and cruise transfers** - to regional and London airports, cruise terminals and south coast ports.
- **Wedding and prom cars** - chauffeur-driven vehicles for events.
