# Broadfield

> International law firm with UK offices in London, Reading and Southampton.

## What they do

Broadfield is a law firm offering legal services across a range of sectors and jurisdictions, operating in the UK as Broadfield Law UK LLP. It lists offices in London, Reading and Southampton, and in New York, Hong Kong, Sao Paulo and Paris. Its practice areas include corporate, commercial, public procurement, regulatory, litigation and dispute resolution, and intellectual property, and its sector groups include technology and innovation, energy and infrastructure, transport, and government and public sector.

## Products and solutions

- **Corporate and M&A** - corporate, private equity, venture capital and mergers and acquisitions work.
- **Commercial and IP** - commercial contracts and intellectual property.
- **Public procurement and public law** - public procurement, public and administrative law, and parliamentary agency work.
- **Disputes and investigations** - litigation, dispute resolution, fraud and investigations, inquests and inquiries.
- **Regulatory and employment** - regulatory law and labour and employment advice.
- **Real estate and planning** - real estate, planning and infrastructure.
