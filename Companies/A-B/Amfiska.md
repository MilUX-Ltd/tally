# Amfiska

> London engineering and delivery consultancy offering architecture, solution design, technical leadership and delivery oversight.

## What they do

Amfiska describes itself as engineering and delivery specialists, using systems thinking and technical leadership to connect business goals with practical delivery. Its services cover strategic architecture, solution design, fractional leadership and delivery oversight, with work spanning research and development, requirements engineering, and technical leadership and governance. The company lists aerospace and defence among the sectors it serves and has offices in London and Madrid.
