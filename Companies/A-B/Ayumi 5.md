# Ayumi 5

> Hampshire consultancy providing data protection, compliance and project support, mainly to healthcare providers.

## What they do

Ayumi 5 describes itself as providing data protection and compliance support to GP surgeries, dental practices, pharmacies and other NHS and private healthcare providers across Hampshire and beyond. It handles subject access requests, Data Security and Protection Toolkit (DSPT) submissions and Care Quality Commission (CQC) registration and inspection preparation. It also offers project, change and operational support and management coaching to growing organisations. The business states that it is registered with the Information Commissioner's Office and works on site or remotely.

## Products and solutions

- **Subject access requests** - collation, redaction and response management for practices.
- **DSPT support** - evidence gathering and preparation of Data Security and Protection Toolkit submissions.
- **CQC support** - project management of registration applications and mock inspections.
- **Data protection health checks** - reviews, data mapping and short staff training sessions.
- **Project and change support** - project coordination, process improvement and management coaching.
