# Bandak AS

> Machining and subsea component supplier in Lunde.

## What they do

Bandak supplies machined and assembled parts, with grinding, welding, thermal coating, inspection and testing, from a 12,000 square metre site. Its customers are mainly in subsea energy, with others in renewables, defence and space, and general industry.
