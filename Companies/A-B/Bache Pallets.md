# Bache Pallets

> West Midlands designer and manufacturer of bespoke pallets, stillages and materials handling equipment, part of the Bache Group.

## What they do

Bache Pallets designs, builds and repairs bespoke pallets, stillages and materials handling equipment. It is part of the Bache Group, a family-founded manufacturing group in Lye, West Midlands, which also includes Genwork (welded wire mesh, storage, fencing and custom fabrication) and Air (acoustic barrier systems). The group states that manufacturing began in Halesowen in 1974 and lists automotive, defence, infrastructure and manufacturing among its sectors, stating that Bache Pallets supplies defence.

## Products and solutions

- **Bespoke pallets and stillages** - design and manufacture of pallets and stillages.
- **Materials handling equipment** - design, build and repair service.
- **Genwork** - welded wire mesh, expanded metal, storage and racking, fencing and custom fabrication.
- **Air** - acoustic barrier system for highways, rail and infrastructure.
