# Bournemouth and Poole College

> Further education college in Poole and Bournemouth offering college courses, a university centre, adult courses and apprenticeships.

## What they do

Bournemouth and Poole College is a further education college with campuses in Poole and Bournemouth and The Fulcrum Centre in Poole. It offers study programmes, T Levels, a university centre, adult and upskilling courses, and apprenticeships. For employers it provides apprenticeship training, work experience placements, recruitment support and upskilling courses.

## Products and solutions

- **Apprenticeships** - apprenticeship training and support for employers.
- **T Levels and work experience** - technical qualifications with employer placements.
- **University centre** - higher education courses.
- **Adult and upskilling courses** - training for adults and employees.
