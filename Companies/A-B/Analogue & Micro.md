# Analogue & Micro

> Cwmbran electronics design and manufacturing company specialising in embedded Linux systems, electronic hardware and PCB assembly for industrial, aerospace and defence customers.

## What they do

Analogue & Micro Ltd began as an electronic design consultancy in 1996 and now covers hardware development, operating systems and application software alongside electronics manufacturing at its UK site. The company designs 32-bit and 64-bit embedded devices, industrial PCs and bespoke certified products, and names aerospace, defence, oil and gas, transportation and telecommunications among the environments it designs for. Design and production work under one roof at Lakeside House, Llantarnam, Cwmbran, in South Wales, and the company states that all its products are manufactured in the UK.

## Products and solutions

- **Electronic design** - schematic and multi-layer PCB design for high-speed digital and analogue systems, power supply design, and design for EMC, environmental and safety compliance.
- **Embedded Linux and software** - bootloader, Yocto Linux toolchains and BSPs, RTOS and bare-metal development, and MilCAN systems on ARM and PowerPC platforms.
- **FPGA development** - VHDL design, synthesis and verification.
- **Embedded Linux modules** - COTS modules and development platforms based on NXP and Texas Instruments processors.
- **Electronics manufacturing** - SMT and hand assembly to IPC J-STD-001 class III, cable assembly to IPC-A-620 class III, automated optical inspection and bespoke test.
- **Life-cycle support** - product quality and obsolescence management across the product life cycle.
