# Bosch Rexroth UK

> UK arm of Bosch Rexroth supplying hydraulics, electric drives and controls, linear motion and assembly technology.

## What they do

Bosch Rexroth Limited is the Great Britain business of Bosch Rexroth, part of the Bosch group, and describes itself as a partner for hydraulics and automation. It supplies hydraulics, electric drives and controls, linear motion technology, assembly technology and mobile robotics for factory automation and mobile machinery, together with service, spare parts and training. Its mobile machinery range covers mobile hydraulics, electronics and telematics, electric drives and gearboxes. Its registered office is in St Neots, Cambridgeshire.

## Products and solutions

- **Industrial hydraulics** - pumps, valves and hydraulic systems.
- **Electric drives and controls** - drives and the ctrlX automation platform.
- **Linear motion and assembly technology** - linear motion components and assembly systems.
- **Mobile machinery** - mobile hydraulics, electronics, telematics, electric drives and gearboxes.
- **Service and training** - service, spare parts and training.
