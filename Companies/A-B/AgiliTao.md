# AgiliTao

> Alveston consultancy providing "Scrum as a Service": small teams of Agile Product Owners and Scrum Masters placed with client organisations.

## What they do

AgiliTao provides small teams of Agile specialists, Product Owners and Scrum Masters, so that clients can keep their recruitment and staff development focused on their core technical competencies. It describes its approach as bottom-up, putting experienced practitioners into these roles full time rather than asking existing staff to take them on part time. It also places individual Agile specialists with SMEs, supported by its own community of practice.

## Products and solutions

- **Teams of Agile specialists** - Product Owners and Scrum Masters working as a team within the client's programme.
- **Individual consultancy and community of practice** - single Agile specialists, backed by the wider AgiliTao community.
- **Bespoke** - engagements shaped to the client's need.
