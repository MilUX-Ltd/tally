# Browne Jacobson

> UK and Ireland law firm with an aerospace, space and defence practice and an office in Cardiff.

## What they do

Browne Jacobson is a law firm operating as Browne Jacobson LLP in the UK, with offices in Birmingham, Cardiff, Exeter, London, Manchester and Nottingham, and on the island of Ireland. It states that it has over 800 lawyers across the UK and Ireland. Its aerospace, space and defence sector team advises on transactions, procurement, export controls and disputes, and the firm states it has advised the Ministry of Defence on aerial target systems and supply chain contracts. The Cardiff office opened in 2023 and moved to One Central Square in January 2026, with a team of 40 at that time.

## Products and solutions

- **Corporate and M&A** - transactions including National Security and Investment Act 2021 notifications.
- **Sanctions and export controls** - sanctions, export control and anti-corruption advice.
- **Commercial contracting** - commercial contracts and supply chain arrangements.
- **Public procurement** - defence procurement rules, single-source contracts and defence provisions of the Procurement Act 2023.
- **Intellectual property** - protection and licensing, including dual-use technology and trade secrets.
- **Dispute resolution** - litigation, arbitration, mediation and expert determination.
- **Welsh law and devolution** - public law advice from the Cardiff office.
