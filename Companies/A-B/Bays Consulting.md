# Bays Consulting

> Guildford data science and statistics company offering predictive crowd modelling, machine learning and synthetic data generation.

## What they do

Bays Consulting Limited describes itself as a data science and analytics company that delivers explainable models and analysis to clients across industry, government and academia. Its services cover predictive crowd modelling, data science and statistics, machine learning, AI readiness audits and synthetic data generation. Its Bays Crowd tool simulates crowd movement and behaviour in response to incidents such as fights and armed attacks, to support security planning and risk mitigation. The company states that it is a Silver member of the Armed Forces Covenant.

## Products and solutions

- **Bays Crowd** - crowd simulation tool that predicts and visualises crowd movement and behaviour during disruptive incidents, with reports for security and safety planning.
- **Data science and statistics** - analysis and modelling of complex data sets.
- **Machine learning** - algorithms for automation and prediction.
- **AI readiness audits** - assessment of an organisation's preparedness for AI integration.
- **Synthetic data generation** - realistic synthetic data sets for secure testing and training.
