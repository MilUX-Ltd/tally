# Apsley Precision Engineering

> Wiltshire sub-contract precision engineering company machining components and assemblies for defence, aerospace, medical, nuclear and oil and gas customers.

## What they do

Apsley Precision Engineering Ltd is a sub-contract precision engineering company based at High Post near Salisbury. The company states it was established in 1984 as Apsley Engineering, took its current name in 2010 and operates from 12,000 sq ft premises. It specialises in close-tolerance CNC machined components and assemblies for the defence, medical, oil and gas, aerospace and scientific industries, from prototypes to volume production. The company states it supplies precision defence components, including housings, mounts and fixtures for weapons and systems platforms, to organisations across the UK defence industry.

## Products and solutions

- **CNC milling** - multi-axis machining including 5-axis.
- **CNC turning** - multi-axis turning, including turned components up to 2 metres in length.
- **Prototype, batch and volume production** - prototypes through to production runs, with tooling and fixture manufacture.
- **Inspection** - CNC CMM inspection against customer models or drawings, and automated optical inspection.
- **Stock management** - Kanban and stock management services, with project management.
