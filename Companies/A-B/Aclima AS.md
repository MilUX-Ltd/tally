# Aclima AS

> Norwegian maker of merino wool base layers for outdoor, professional and military use.

## What they do

Aclima makes wool underwear and base layers. Its PRO range is designed for professional users in defence, police, emergency and fire and rescue services. The company states its garments have been used by the Norwegian armed forces since the 1960s and in the Nordic Combat Uniform project.

## Products and solutions

- **Aclima PRO** - Wool base layers for defence, police and emergency users
