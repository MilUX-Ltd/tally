# Bluecube

> Lincoln managed IT, cyber security, Microsoft cloud and business communications provider.

## What they do

Bluecube states it has delivered technology and telecoms solutions since 2008 and provides managed IT services, cyber security, Microsoft cloud solutions and business communications to UK organisations from its Lincoln headquarters. It is a Microsoft Solutions Partner for Modern Work and supports clients in achieving Cyber Essentials and Cyber Essentials Plus certification. Its services also include hosted telephony and unified communications, business connectivity and mobile voice and data. The company trades from 103 Newport, Lincoln.

## Products and solutions

- **Managed IT services** - proactive IT support, monitoring and guidance.
- **Cyber security** - security services for people, devices and data.
- **Cyber Essentials certification** - guidance and remediation towards Cyber Essentials and Cyber Essentials Plus.
- **Microsoft solutions** - Microsoft 365 and Modern Work services.
- **Hosted telephony and unified communications** - cloud telephony and collaboration.
- **Connectivity and mobile** - business internet connectivity and multi-network mobile with device management.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Accreditations:** ISO 9001; ISO 27001; Cyber Essentials Plus; Microsoft Solutions Partner for Modern Work.
- **Memberships:** Greater Lincolnshire RDSC
