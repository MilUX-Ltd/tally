# Blakcro

> Wiltshire defence technology company designing and manufacturing military powered paraglider systems, with flight and technical training.

## What they do

Blakcro describes itself as a defence technology company delivering solutions for specialist user groups. It designs, engineers and manufactures single and dual-operator and autonomous military powered paraglider systems for independent air mobility, intended for infiltration and exfiltration where traditional aircraft are not viable. The company states it provides flight training, tactical and technical training for support teams, and service and maintenance support. It also offers regulatory consultancy and assurance services for introducing the capability.

## Products and solutions

- **P-Series** - foot-launched mil-spec powered paraglider systems, including the P1 flagship platform.
- **T-Series** - multi-role powered paraglider trikes.
- **U-Series** - autonomous systems.
- **Communication, avionics and transport** - mil-spec ancillary solutions.
- **Training** - basic, advanced, tactics, techniques and procedures, trike and technical training.
- **Service and support** - technical support and maintenance of airframes, engines, wings and reserves.
