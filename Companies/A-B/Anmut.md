# Anmut

> London consultancy helping organisations understand the economic value, risk and consequences of their data.

## What they do

Anmut describes itself as a data asset management consultancy that helps organisations understand the economic value, risk and consequences of their data through the discipline it calls Data Economics. Its published services include data valuation, data maturity and condition assessment, data strategy and data monetisation, several of which are listed on the UK Digital Marketplace (G-Cloud).
