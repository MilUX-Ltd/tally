# BWB Consulting

> Nottingham-founded engineering and environmental consultancy delivering building, infrastructure and environmental services, including for defence infrastructure.

## What they do

BWB Consulting Ltd describes itself as an integrated engineering, environmental and infrastructure consultancy, founded in Nottingham as a civil and structural engineering company. It has offices in Nottingham, Birmingham, Leeds, London and Manchester, and states it became part of the CAF Group. Its markets include custodial, defence, education, healthcare, highways and bridges, industrial and distribution, power and renewables, rail, strategic land and urban regeneration. For defence, it states it offers planning, site studies, design, project management and sustainability consulting for defence infrastructure projects.

## Products and solutions

- **Civil and structural engineering** - engineering design for buildings and infrastructure.
- **Environmental consultancy** - environmental and sustainability services.
- **Defence infrastructure** - planning, site studies, design and project management for defence facilities.
