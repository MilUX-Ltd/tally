# Bjerkvik Defence

> Oslo company building autonomous drones, ground control software and sensors for surveillance and monitoring.

## What they do

Bjerkvik Defence builds a family of autonomous drones and sensors that monitor sites continuously and report changes to an operator through one ground control picture. Its systems cover perimeter surveillance, rapid response and a concept passive seabed sensor array for detecting submarines.

## Products and solutions

- **Hubro** - Perimeter surveillance quadcopter
- **Arpia** - Rapid response VTOL fixed wing drone
- **Eyrie** - Ground control software
- **Kutling** - Concept passive underwater sensor system
