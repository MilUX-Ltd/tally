# BiD Masters

> Chichester bid and proposal consultancy offering strategic advice, bid writing, SME support and bid training.

## What they do

BiD Masters Ltd provides business development support, including opportunity identification, win strategy, bid writing and proposal development. The company states it works with large organisations pursuing major contracts and with smaller companies entering new markets, and its published client testimonials include defence and transport companies. It also offers bid and tender writing training and support for SMEs. The company publishes an Armed Forces Covenant document, an ISO 9001:2015 certificate and a JOSCAR certificate on its website.

## Products and solutions

- **Strategic advice** - opportunity identification and win strategy.
- **Bid writing** - bid and proposal writing and support.
- **SME support** - bid support services for smaller companies.
- **Bid training** - bid and tender writing training courses.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Accreditations:** ISO 9001:2015; JOSCAR certificate.
- **Memberships:** South Central RDSC
