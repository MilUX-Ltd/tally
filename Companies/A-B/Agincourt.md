# Agincourt

> Lincoln maker of immersive training, simulation and decision-support systems for defence, law enforcement and public safety, including the BattleVR platform.

## What they do

Agincourt, trading as Agincourt Consultancy Ltd, makes immersive training, simulation and decision-support systems for defence, law enforcement and public safety organisations. Its products include the BattleVR virtual and augmented reality training platform, small-arms and anti-armour simulation, counter-UAS training, battlefield simulation and exercise control, and training management tools. It also lists geospatial decision-support and military equipment image recognition products in beta. The company is based at Tower House in Lincoln.

## Products and solutions

- **BattleVR** - immersive VR and AR training platform.
- **BATTLEVEMS** - close-quarter combat training environment for shoot houses.
- **YEOMAN** - counter-UAS training.
- **Archer and ArcheonXT** - small-arms simulation and synthetic weapon and recoil hardware.
- **LancerVR and FieldVR** - anti-armour training and police process training.
- **HAWK and metaCAN** - battlefield simulation, exercise control and an integrated synthetic training platform.
- **HERALD and KEEPBOX** - lesson planning and training records, and portable range monitoring.
- **JANUS and Sentinel** - geospatial decision support and equipment image recognition (beta).
