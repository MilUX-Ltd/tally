# Aspire Management Consultancy

> Lincolnshire leadership and organisational development consultancy for business owners and senior teams.

## What they do

Aspire Management Consultancy Ltd provides leadership, team and organisational development for business owners, managing directors and senior teams in the UK and internationally. Its services include a senior team health check with a 90-day action plan, senior team performance work, executive advisory, leadership and succession support, and coaching, mentoring, facilitation and psychometrics. Its website displays a Veteran Owned mark. The company's registered office is near Lincoln.

## Products and solutions

- **Senior Team Health Check** - independent assessment of a senior team with a 90-day action plan.
- **Senior team performance** - team development for leadership teams.
- **Executive advisory** - advisory support for managing directors and executives.
- **Leadership and succession** - leadership development and succession planning.
- **Coaching and psychometrics** - coaching, mentoring, facilitation and psychometric tools including DiSC and Belbin.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Accreditations:** DiSC certified trainer; Belbin Team Roles accredited trainer (shown on its website).
- **Memberships:** Greater Lincolnshire RDSC
