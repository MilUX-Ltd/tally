# Aquarius Test Engineering

> Hampshire semiconductor test facility offering new product introduction support for fabless chip companies.

## What they do

Aquarius Test Engineering describes itself as an independent UK test facility and provider of semiconductor new product introduction (NPI) support, from design through to mass production. Its services include ATE test and product engineering, qualification support, prototype packaging, product validation and characterisation, and ESD, failure analysis and reliability consultancy, with tester access and small-batch production for fabless companies. It states that it aims to be vendor agnostic across test platforms from suppliers including Advantest, Chroma and Teradyne. The company is based at the Fareham Innovation Centre in Lee-on-the-Solent.

## Products and solutions

- **ATE test and product engineering** - test development and tester access on automatic test equipment.
- **Qualification and validation** - qualification support, product validation and characterisation.
- **Prototype packaging** - prototype packaging for semiconductor devices.
- **Reliability consultancy** - ESD, failure analysis and reliability consultancy.
- **NPI planning and training** - project and NPI planning and an NPI training programme.
