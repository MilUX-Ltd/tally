# BlinkTroll

> Maker of mobile moving target systems for military and police training.

## What they do

BlinkTroll makes programmable mobile moving target systems for military, special forces, police and sniper training. It states more than 240 systems are in use in 15 NATO countries, and offers accessories including a drone target for counter-UAS training.

## Products and solutions

- **MTS Mk V Moving Target System** - Mobile programmable target for live-fire training
- **Drone Target** - Airborne target for counter-UAS training
