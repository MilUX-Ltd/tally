# Axnes AS

> Norwegian maker of wireless intercom systems for aviation, search and rescue and military use.

## What they do

Axnes designs and manufactures wireless intercom systems used by aviation, search and rescue and military teams. It was founded in 1995, has offices in several countries and supplies major aviation manufacturers.

## Products and solutions

- **CPX Wireless Intercom** - Wireless intercom for aircraft crews
- **PNG Wireless Intercom** - Wireless intercom with handsets and base stations
