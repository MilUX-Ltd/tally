# Blueye Robotics AS

> Trondheim maker of underwater drones (ROVs) for inspection, defence and other uses.

## What they do

Blueye Robotics makes compact underwater ROVs and software for inspection, aquaculture, search and recovery and defence. Its defence page describes use by military teams for underwater situational awareness, explosive ordnance disposal and protecting critical infrastructure.

## Products and solutions

- **Blueye X7** - Underwater ROV
- **Blueye X3** - Underwater ROV
- **Blueye SDK** - Software development kit for ROV applications
