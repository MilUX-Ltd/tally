# Autrix

> Lincoln warehouse automation company providing control software, warehouse management software, robotics and implementation.

## What they do

Autrix Limited helps warehouse businesses automate their operations, combining software, robotics and process design from goods-in to dispatch. Its Autrix Control System brings robots, people and software under one control layer, and its warehouse management software provides stock visibility, workflow automation and order management. The company states it has active projects in five countries and draws on more than 30 years of hands-on warehouse automation experience. Its published case studies include an automation installation for an aerospace component distributor, using autonomous mobile robots, bespoke racking and pick-to-light workstations.

## Products and solutions

- **Autrix Control System** - a single control layer for robots, people and software in the warehouse.
- **Warehouse management software** - real-time stock visibility, workflow automation and order management.
- **Warehouse robotics automation** - autonomous mobile robots and related automation.
- **Warehouse automation consultancy** - automation planning covering return on investment and integration.
- **Warehouse design and system implementation** - process design, integration, testing and go-live.
