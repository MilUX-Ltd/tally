# AUGR

> Oslo company making a modular drone loadout system for existing drones.

## What they do

AUGR was founded in 2025 and develops a Modular Drone Loadout System that adds task-specific capability to existing drones. The team has defence, robotics and software engineering experience and states it works to defence-grade standards.

## Products and solutions

- **Modular Drone Loadout System** - Modular payload system for existing drones
