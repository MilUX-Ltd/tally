# Affinity Flying Training Services

> Aircraft provision, maintenance and continuing airworthiness company supporting the UK Military Flying Training System at RAF Cranwell, Barkston Heath and Valley.

## What they do

Affinity Flying Training Services Limited provides and maintains the fixed-wing training aircraft used in the UK Military Flying Training System under a Fixed Wing Training Package contract with Ascent that runs to 2033. It operates 42 aircraft, Grob 120TP Prefect, Beechcraft T-6C Texan and Embraer Phenom 100, from RAF Cranwell, RAF Barkston Heath and RAF Valley. The company states that the fleet is managed under joint Civil Aviation Authority and Military Aviation Authority oversight using the CAP562 Leaflet B-40 framework, with its own Continuing Airworthiness Management Organisation and Part-145 maintenance. It states it passed 100,000 flying hours in 2026.

## Products and solutions

- **Training aircraft provision** - Grob 120TP Prefect for elementary, Beechcraft T-6C Texan for basic and Embraer Phenom 100 for multi-engine pilot training.
- **Aircraft maintenance** - maintenance and support at RAF Cranwell, RAF Barkston Heath and RAF Valley.
- **Continuing airworthiness** - CAMO services under the Leaflet B-40 civil-military framework.
- **Regulatory compliance and aircraft management** - safety, compliance and fleet availability management.
