# Amethyst Risk Management

> UK cyber security consultancy serving government, defence, operators of essential services and the private sector.

## What they do

Amethyst Risk Management Limited is a UK cyber security consultancy that states it works with government, defence, operators of essential services and private-sector organisations to reduce cyber risk, meet compliance requirements and satisfy contractual security obligations. The company states its services cover cyber security certification, governance, risk and compliance, security for enterprises, programmes and projects, and security and vetting services. It references the NCSC Cyber Assessment Framework, the NIST Cybersecurity Framework, Cyber Essentials Plus, ISO/IEC 27001 and SOC 2.

## Products and solutions

- **Governance, risk and compliance** - cyber security certification and assurance.
- **Programme and project security** - cyber security support for enterprises, programmes and projects.
- **Security and vetting services** - cyber security and vetting support.
- **Sector focus** - government, defence, critical national infrastructure, enterprise and SMEs.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Accreditations:** ISO 9001, ISO 27001, Cyber Essentials Plus, NCSC Assured Consultancy, NCSC Cyber Resilience Audit.
- **Frameworks:** Cyber Security Services (CCS), G-Cloud, Digital Outcomes and Specialists.
- **Memberships:** techUK.
