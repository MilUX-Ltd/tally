# Air & Space Institute

> Newark aviation and space training campus of the Lincoln College Group and a national Defence Technical Excellence College hub.

## What they do

The Air & Space Institute (ASI) in Newark is an aviation and space training provider and part of the Lincoln College Group. It offers further and higher education courses, including HNC and HND, apprenticeships and pilot licence training, across aircraft engineering, pilot, space, cabin crew, uniformed protective services and engineering pathways. Its central Newark campus includes simulation suites and an A320neo aircraft. ASI states it is one of five national hubs for the new Defence Technical Excellence Colleges and works with the Royal Air Force to fill pilot, engineering and ground-crew roles.

## Products and solutions

- **Further and higher education** - school leaver courses and HNC and HND programmes in aviation and space.
- **Pilot training** - PPL and LAPL training and flying opportunities.
- **Apprenticeships and employer services** - apprenticeships, employer partnerships and corporate facilities.
