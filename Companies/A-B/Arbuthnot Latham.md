# Arbuthnot Latham

> London private and commercial bank and wealth manager, part of Arbuthnot Banking Group.

## What they do

Arbuthnot Latham & Co., Limited is a bank founded in 1833 that provides private banking, commercial banking and wealth management, and is part of Arbuthnot Banking Group PLC. Its head office is at Arbuthnot House, Finsbury Circus, London, with offices in Manchester, Exeter and Bristol. Its commercial banking covers accounts, deposits, lending including real estate finance and asset-based lending, access to government-backed schemes and treasury services. It is authorised by the Prudential Regulation Authority and regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority and the Prudential Regulation Authority.

## Products and solutions

- **Private banking** - relationship banking, savings and lending, including mortgages.
- **Commercial banking** - business accounts, deposits, lending, real estate finance and asset-based lending.
- **Government schemes** - access to government-backed business finance.
- **Wealth management** - wealth planning and investment management.
- **Treasury services** - foreign currency, liquidity and cash flow support.
