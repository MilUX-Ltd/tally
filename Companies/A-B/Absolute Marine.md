# Absolute Marine

> Isle of Wight builder of pilot boats, patrol boats and commercial workboats based on the Halmatic Nelson range.

## What they do

Absolute Marine Limited designs and builds pilot boats, patrol boats and workboats at its yard in Cowes, Isle of Wight, specialising in the Halmatic Nelson range. The company states it covers design, in-house engineering, composite construction including resin infusion, fit-out, certification and long-term support. Its customers include port and harbour authorities, pilotage services, commercial marine operators and government, local authority and emergency services, and it offers a multi-role patrol vessel for coastguard, police and government authorities.

## Products and solutions

- **HN35, HN40 and HN42** - pilot boats.
- **HN35-WB** - workboat.
- **HN-PATROL** - multi-role patrol vessel.
- **Build and support** - design, composite construction, fit-out, certification and through-life support.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Accreditations:** Vessels built to MCA Workboat Code 3 as standard, with RINA certification optional.
- **Memberships:** British Marine; South Central RDSC
