# Align Cargo AS

> Freight forwarder offering transport and logistics, including for government and defence clients.

## What they do

Align Cargo provides sea, air, road and rail freight, customs clearance and warehousing. It has a dedicated Government and Defence service covering transport of personnel and equipment for complex operations.
