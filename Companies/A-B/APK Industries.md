# APK Industries

> Lincoln designer and manufacturer of portable power stations for utilities, emergency services and defence.

## What they do

APK Industries designs and manufactures portable power stations, also called portable power units, which provide power where electricity access is limited, including for medical equipment and welfare appliances. The company states the units run silently as AC and DC generators with zero emissions and can be charged from sources such as wind or solar. It lists utilities, emergency services, disaster relief, and defence and security operations as markets, and states that four of the six UK distribution network operators trialled the units after a three-year development phase.

## Products and solutions

- **Portable power stations** - silent, zero-emission AC and DC portable power units.
- **Remote monitoring and onsite service** - monitoring and service support for deployed units.
