# Bravo Intelligence & Technology AS

> Develops an assurance layer for AI-enabled and autonomous systems, with defence and security use cases.

## What they do

Bravo Intelligence & Technology develops a vendor-independent assurance layer for AI-enabled and autonomous systems. Its site lists use cases in ISR and operational awareness, counter-UAS decision support, coordination of uncrewed systems and mission planning. It states the work is at an early development stage.

## Products and solutions

- **BIT Mission Assurance** - Assurance layer for AI-enabled and autonomous systems in defence and security settings.
