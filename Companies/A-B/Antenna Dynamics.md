# Antenna Dynamics

> West Sussex consultancy designing and manufacturing bespoke mechatronic systems, including target motion systems for defence test installations.

## What they do

Antenna Dynamics Ltd describes itself as a consultancy for the design and manufacture of bespoke high performance mechatronic systems for defence and automation. Its stated defence work includes dual target motion systems for RF hardware-in-the-loop (HWIL) installations, and it lists defence systems development among its services. Other projects shown on its website include an antenna platform, a satellite panel printer and a motion platform for a Formula 1 simulator. The company is based at Cowfold in West Sussex.

## Products and solutions

- **Target motion systems** - including a patented target motion system and dual target motion systems for RF HWIL installations.
- **Antenna platforms** - bespoke antenna positioning platforms.
- **Defence systems development** - design and build of mechatronic systems for defence applications.
- **Custom mechatronics and automation** - bespoke mechatronic and automation solutions, including a simulator motion platform and a satellite panel printer.
