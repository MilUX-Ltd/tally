# BEC Group

> Hampshire plastic injection moulder and toolmaker with in-house tooling, design and custom filter manufacture.

## What they do

BEC Group is a plastic injection moulding company based at Gore Road Industrial Estate in New Milton, Hampshire, with an in-house toolroom. It provides moulding, injection mould tooling, product and tool design, 3D printing and scanning, and custom filter manufacture, and supports customers reshoring production to the UK. The company states it serves marine, bio-medical, automotive, transport and FMCG sectors, and it has announced JOSCAR registered supplier status to support aerospace, defence and public sector customers. It trades through B.E.C Global Limited.

## Products and solutions

- **Injection moulding** - plastic injection, filter, overmoulding and insert moulding.
- **Tooling** - injection mould tooling, filter tooling, rapid tooling, repairs and modifications.
- **Design** - product, filter and tool design, 3D printing and scanning, and design for manufacture.
- **Custom filters** - filters with nylon, polypropylene and ABS meshes.
- **Reshoring** - transfer of mould tools and production back to the UK.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Accreditations:** ISO 9001; JOSCAR registered supplier.
- **Memberships:** South Central RDSC
