# Bacon Engineering Group

> Grimsby multi-discipline engineering company providing precision machining, fabrication and welding, marine engineering and drone and laser scanning services.

## What they do

Bacon Engineering Group is the trading name of E.Bacon & Co Limited, which states that it was formed in 1899 and operates from facilities on the South Humberside Industrial Estate in Grimsby. It provides engineering design, precision machining, structural and sheet metal fabrication, pipework, site support, installation and decommissioning, marine engineering and ship repair, and drone and laser scanning across sectors including renewables, construction, chemical processing, utilities, manufacturing, aerospace and defence. For defence it states that it has worked on components for land, sea and air platforms, with certified and coded fabrication, precision CNC machining, R&D and prototyping support, personnel security clearance and NDA-compliant processes.

## Products and solutions

- **Precision machining** - CNC machining of high-accuracy parts, including aerospace-grade tolerances.
- **Fabrication and welding** - structural, sheet metal and coded fabrication and pipework.
- **Engineering design and project management** - design, production manufacturing and project delivery.
- **Marine engineering and ship repair** - engineering support for vessels.
- **Site support** - installation, decommissioning and site support services.
- **Drone and laser scanning** - survey and scanning services.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Accreditations:** ISO 9001; BS EN ISO 3834 (welding quality); BS EN 1090 Execution Class 3; Constructionline Gold; SafeContractor.
- **Memberships:** Greater Lincolnshire RDSC
