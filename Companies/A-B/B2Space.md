# B2Space

> Newport-based high-altitude operations company using stratospheric balloons for near-space testing, high altitude pseudo-satellite missions and a balloon-launched small satellite launcher.

## What they do

B2Space provides high-altitude services based on stratospheric balloons. It offers near-space testing, in which balloons carry payloads to the stratosphere to test, qualify and validate space technology, and high altitude pseudo-satellite (HAPS) missions for earth observation, communications and surveillance tasks such as border control and maritime monitoring. It is developing a "rockoon" launch system, in which a stratospheric balloon lifts a rocket to about 35 km before launching small and micro satellites to low Earth orbit. The UK company was founded in 2016 and is registered at Maesglas Industrial Estate, Newport; it also has an office in Burgos, Spain, and has flown test balloons from sites in Wales including Llanbedr.

## Products and solutions

- **Near Space Testing** - stratospheric balloon "flying lab" for testing and qualifying space technology in near-vacuum, high-radiation conditions.
- **HAPS missions** - high altitude pseudo-satellite flights for earth observation, telecommunications and surveillance.
- **Satellite launch** - rockoon (rocket and balloon) launch concept for small and micro satellites.
- **University programme** - CubeSat programme in which student satellites are flown on stratospheric missions.
