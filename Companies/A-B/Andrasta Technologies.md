# Andrasta Technologies

> London engineering consultancy providing safety cases, sustainable product development and scientific writing for high-hazard sectors including energetic materials.

## What they do

Andrasta Technologies states that it engineers safety, sustainability and supply chain security solutions for high-hazard sectors. It develops safety cases for high-hazard facilities, aligned with UK defence standards and regulatory frameworks for energetic materials and industrial explosives operations, from hazard identification through to ALARP demonstration. The company also supports product development using circular economy principles and produces research papers, white papers and policy documents. It states that its current research explores sovereign electrochemical production systems for critical materials used by high-hazard industries.

## Products and solutions

- **Safety case development** - safety cases for high-hazard and energetic materials facilities, including hazard identification and ALARP demonstration.
- **Sustainable product development** - concept to prototype development designed around circular economy principles.
- **Scientific writing** - research papers, white papers and policy documents for engineering and scientific work.
