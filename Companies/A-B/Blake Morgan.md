# Blake Morgan

> UK law firm with six offices, including Southampton, Oxford and Reading, advising businesses, public sector bodies and individuals.

## What they do

Blake Morgan is a law firm operating as Blake Morgan LLP, with offices in Cardiff, London, Manchester, Oxford, Reading and Southampton. It states that it has more than 520 staff, excluding partners, and more than 90 partners, and that it acts for corporate, public sector, not-for-profit and government clients as well as individuals. Its registered office is at Chandler's Ford, Eastleigh, Hampshire. The firm is authorised and regulated by the Solicitors Regulation Authority.

## Products and solutions

- **Corporate and commercial law** - transactional and advisory work for businesses, including mergers and acquisitions.
- **Employment law** - employment advice and an employment forum for clients.
- **Public sector law** - advice to government and public sector organisations.
- **Private client services** - legal advice for individuals.
