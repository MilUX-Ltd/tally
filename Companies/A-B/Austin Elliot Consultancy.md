# Austin Elliot Consultancy

> Cheltenham consultancy providing technical delivery and organisational transformation, mainly to the public sector.

## What they do

Austin Elliot Consultancy describes itself as a provider of digital transformation in the public sector, tailoring technical delivery and organisational transformation to the needs of a client's people. Its services cover business and organisational transformation, digital transformation, cloud migration and support, cyber security, data management and technical capability delivery. It supplies individuals or teams, such as project managers, business change consultants and cloud developers, and can provide a fully contracted-out delivery service.

## Products and solutions

- **Business and Organisational Transformation** - technological, cultural, process and skills change.
- **Digital Transformation** - incremental, planned adoption of technology.
- **Cloud Migration and Cloud Support Services** - setting up, running and enhancing cloud services.
- **Cyber Security** - design, development and management of complex systems.
- **Data Management** - data management consultancy.
- **Technical Capability Delivery** - mixed teams delivering IT into operations.
