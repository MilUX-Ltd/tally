# Alacritas Innovations

> Hampshire maker of precision rifle shooting accessories, including rear rests and recoil control devices.

## What they do

Alacritas Innovations designs and sells precision rifle shooting accessories, describing its range as UK-made precision shooting gear. Its products include the Fortis range of rear rests and bags, the Linear Recoil Device, scope mounts and tripod accessories, and it also sells Sunwayfoto tripods. The company states it was founded by competitive riflemen and has appointed a European distributor for the EU. Its registered office is in Fareham, Hampshire.

## Products and solutions

- **Fortis rear rests** - fixed and micro-adjustable rear rests, bags and cradles for precision rifle shooting.
- **Linear Recoil Device** - a recoil management device for rifles.
- **Mounts and tripod accessories** - scope mounts and M-LOK tripod tables.
- **Tripods** - Sunwayfoto tripods.
