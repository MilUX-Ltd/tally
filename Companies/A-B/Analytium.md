# Analytium

> London data and analytics consultancy providing data strategy, AI, business intelligence and data platform services.

## What they do

Analytium Ltd is a London-based data and analytics company that states it helps organisations turn data into actionable insights. Its services cover data maturity assessment and data strategy, building and deploying AI, business intelligence, data integration, data science and data warehouse solutions, and maintaining and modernising data platforms. The company states it works with Microsoft Azure data and AI services and with SAS technologies, including SAS platform modernisation, and that it offers fixed-fee engagements and a flexible workforce service.

## Products and solutions

- **Data strategy** - data maturity assessments and data strategy consulting.
- **AI solutions** - agentic AI and large language model solutions.
- **Business intelligence** - business intelligence and visualisation, including Microsoft Power BI.
- **Data platforms** - data integration and quality, data warehouse implementation, platform assessment, maintenance and modernisation.
- **SAS modernisation** - SAS Viya, SAS language and SAS on mainframe expertise.
- **Flexible workforce** - supply of data and analytics specialists.
