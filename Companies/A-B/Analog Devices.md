# Analog Devices

> US semiconductor company with a UK headquarters in Hayes, west London, supplying signal chain, RF and sensor components to aerospace and defence among other markets.

## What they do

Analog Devices designs and makes semiconductors, including signal chain, RF and microwave, power and sensor components. On its aerospace and defence pages it describes products and subsystems for radar, electronic surveillance and countermeasures, military communications, avionics, missiles and precision munitions, and space and high-reliability applications. Its UK company, Analog Devices Limited, is registered at 15 Pressing Lane, Hayes, which trade press reported in 2019 as the company's new UK headquarters, replacing offices in Weybridge and Marlow. The UK company publishes gender pay gap reports, which apply to employers of 250 or more.

## Products and solutions

- **RF and microwave** - components and subsystems for radar, electronic warfare and communications.
- **Signal chain components** - data converters, amplifiers and related components.
- **Sensors** - MEMS accelerometers, gyroscopes and inertial measurement units.
- **Space and high-reliability products** - components for satellite and other demanding environments.
