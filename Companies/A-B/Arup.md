# Arup

> Global engineering, design and advisory consultancy for the built environment, with resilience, security and risk services including blast and protective design.

## What they do

Arup is a global consultancy that plans, designs and engineers the built environment, founded in 1946 and headquartered in London. It states that it is a trust-owned firm without external investors, reporting 18,572 members and total revenue of 2.27 billion pounds for FY 2025/26. Its resilience, security and risk practice covers safety risk, security risk and business risk, with expertise in security intelligence, blast and protective design, hostile vehicle mitigation, physical and electronic security, cyber security, and personnel and operational security. The UK operating company is Ove Arup & Partners Limited.

## Products and solutions

- **Engineering design** - structural, civil and building engineering for buildings and infrastructure.
- **Security risk** - threat and risk assessment, blast and protective design and hostile vehicle mitigation.
- **Safety risk** - technical safety and system reliability assessment and safety cases.
- **Advisory** - business risk, strategic foresight and planning advice.
