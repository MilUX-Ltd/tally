# Bonwyke

> Fareham wholesaler and installer of protective window film and glass graphics.

## What they do

Bonwyke describes itself as the largest independent wholesaler and installer of protective window film in the UK, established for over 40 years and based at Salterns Lane Industrial Estate in Fareham, Hampshire. It supplies and installs architectural, automotive, privacy, safety and security, and solar films, and prints, cuts and installs graphics for glass and walls. Its specialist range includes anti-information-leakage film for LED screens and signal-blocking films.

## Products and solutions

- **Window film** - architectural, automotive, privacy, safety and security, solar and transparent colour films, with installation.
- **Graphics** - printing, cutting and installation of imagery for glass and walls.
- **Specialist films** - AV ScreenMask anti-information-leakage film for LED screens, Signals Defense and Data Jammer films, and bird-protection film.
