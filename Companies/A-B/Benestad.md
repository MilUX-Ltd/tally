# Benestad

> Manufacturer of customised glass and ceramics-to-metal sealed products, including ignitors and penetrators for defence.

## What they do

Benestad manufactures customised glass and ceramics-to-metal sealed products. Its defence market page describes four decades supplying ignitors for ignition products and hermetic penetrators, and a vapour deposition line for specialty ignitors used in ammunition and missiles.

## Products and solutions

- **Electrical ignitors** - Glass-to-metal ignitors for ammunition and missiles
- **Hermetic penetrators** - Sealed electrical penetrators
