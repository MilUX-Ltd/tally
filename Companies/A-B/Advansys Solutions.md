# Advansys Solutions

> Yeovil systems engineering and programme management company serving the defence and aerospace sectors.

## What they do

Advansys Solutions Ltd is a privately owned company formed in 2003 that states it provides systems engineering, system development and programme management services to the defence and aerospace sectors. The company states its work includes open systems architectures for Royal Navy submarine and surface ship flotillas, and the specification and development of logistics information systems for aircraft fleet planning. It also describes a technology insertion service that replaces bespoke hardware and software with commercial off-the-shelf equivalents.

## Products and solutions

- **Systems engineering** - system development and programme management for defence and aerospace.
- **Open systems architectures** - for Royal Navy submarine and surface ship flotillas.
- **Logistics information systems** - specification and development for aircraft fleet planning.
- **Technology insertion** - commercial off-the-shelf replacement of bespoke defence hardware and software.
