# Aviation and Survival Support AS

> Supplier of aviation, maritime and land survival, life support and personal protection equipment for defence and aviation.

## What they do

The company's site is titled as equipment for defence and aviation. It supplies life support, survival and personal protection systems for air, land, maritime and subsurface operations, with engineering, customisation and lifecycle support from selection through to sustainment.
