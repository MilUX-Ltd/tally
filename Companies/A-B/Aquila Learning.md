# Aquila Learning

> Bloxham provider of learning technology and professional services for training in complex, high-risk and highly regulated occupations.

## What they do

Aquila Learning provides learning technologies, support and professional services to analyse, design, deliver and assure training for complex, high-risk and high-compliance occupations. Its ALaRMS software is built around a systems approach to training and, the company says, supports training standards used within defence, aerospace and other regulated industries. The company states that it supports organisations and militaries with training technology, training management and operational expertise.

## Products and solutions

- **ALaRMS™ (Aquila Learning and Resource Management Software)** - modular training lifecycle software with four modules: Aquila Analysis (task, competency and training options analysis), Aquila Design (instructional design, course and survey design), Aquila Delivery (course setup, scheduling, instructor and student portals and records) and Aquila Assurance (training risk, evaluation, data analytics and competency management).
- **Professional services and support** - technology support, professional services and outsourced managed services for learning technology.
