# Bredengen

> Supplies RF, fibre optic, power and Ethernet products, with a defence category covering air, ground, navy and drone solutions.

## What they do

Bredengen supplies radio frequency, fibre optic, power, Ethernet and broadcast products to several industries. It assembles pre-terminated fibre cables and custom RF cable assemblies at its Oslo facility. Its site has a defence category covering air, ground, navy and drone and UAS solutions.

## Products and solutions

- **RF cable assemblies** - Custom RF cable assemblies made in Oslo.
- **Fibre cable assemblies** - Pre-terminated fibre cables.
