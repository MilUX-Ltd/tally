# Argo MRT

> Mobile repair team specialising in composite and metallic aircraft structural repair, modification and inspection for operators, lessors and OEMs, including military platforms.

## What they do

Argo MRT provides mobile and workshop repair of composite and metallic aircraft structures, deploying engineers on assignment to support operators, OEMs, MROs and Part 145 and Part 21 organisations. It states it covers business, commercial, military and rotary-wing aircraft, with 24/7 aircraft-on-ground support. The UK company operates from Heathpark Industrial Estate in Honiton, Devon, and a sister company, Argo MRT Americas, works from Montreal. The company does not publish a site in Wales; it is listed as a member of Defence Wales.

## Products and solutions

- **Metallic and composite repairs** - structural repairs on assignment worldwide.
- **Modification and project management** - aircraft modification projects for operators and OEMs.
- **Inspection and borescope services** - asset and borescope inspections.
- **Repair and overhaul** - workshop repair and overhaul at its UK facility.
- **Line maintenance** - offered at selected locations.
