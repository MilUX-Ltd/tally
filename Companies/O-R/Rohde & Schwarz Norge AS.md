# Rohde & Schwarz Norge AS

> Norwegian subsidiary of Rohde & Schwarz supplying communications, test and measurement and security solutions, including to the Norwegian Armed Forces.

## What they do

Rohde & Schwarz Norge is the Norwegian arm of Rohde & Schwarz. Its site presents aerospace and defence solutions for air, land and sea, including naval communications, electronic warfare and cyber defence, and counter-drone systems. Its press releases describe radio systems delivered for Norwegian Armed Forces sites.
