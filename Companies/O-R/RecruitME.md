# RecruitME

> Grantham recruitment agency specialising in placing ex-military and ex-uniformed service personnel with employers across the UK.

## What they do

RecruitME describes itself as a specialist recruitment partner supporting ex-military and ex-uniformed service personnel, recruiting across office support, finance, HR, engineering, supply chain, sales, marketing, IT and communications, from entry level to senior appointments. It states that it was founded by military veterans, began trading in Grantham in 2015, and that every member of its consultancy team is part of the Armed Forces community. The company states it is veteran-owned and holds the Armed Forces Covenant Employer Recognition Scheme Gold award.

## Products and solutions

- **Ex-forces and military recruitment** - placing service leavers and veterans with civilian employers.
- **Discipline recruitment** - engineering, HR, accountancy and finance, supply chain, IT and communications, office support, sales and marketing, and blue light roles.
- **Executive search** - senior leadership appointments.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Accreditations:** Armed Forces Covenant Employer Recognition Scheme Gold award.
- **Memberships:** Greater Lincolnshire RDSC
