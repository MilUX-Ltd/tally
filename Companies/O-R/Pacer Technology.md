# Pacer Technology

> UK start-up developing thermoplastic composite materials and an automated manufacturing process.

## What they do

Pacer Technology Ltd describes itself as a UK thermoplastic composite systems and advanced manufacturing business established in 2025. It states that it is developing a proprietary thermoplastic composite material and a fully automated manufacturing process for composite structures, with production planned to start in 2027. The company describes its products as of UK origin and NDAA compliant.

## Products and solutions

- **TensoForm** - proprietary thermoplastic composite material.
- **TensaLight** - fully automated manufacturing process for thermoplastic composite structures.
