# Phixos

> Worcester engineering company providing safety-critical software, firmware and certification services to original equipment manufacturers in aerospace, space, defence and other sectors.

## What they do

Phixos states it is a strategic provider of engineering solutions to major original equipment manufacturers, focused on safety-critical and high-integrity systems, and that it has over 30 years of experience as part of a wider group. Its stated sectors are aerospace, space, defence, automotive, rail, medical, energy and life sciences. The company states it works to standards including DO-178B/C, DO-254, IEC 61508, IEC 62304, ISO 26262 and Def Stan 00-56.

## Products and solutions

- **Software engineering** - full-lifecycle real-time and embedded software.
- **Firmware engineering** - high-integrity firmware, including radiation-tolerant designs.
- **Certification** - software and firmware targeting SIL 4 and DAL A, tool qualification and re-certification.
- **Electronics design and manufacturing** - turnkey solutions with a UK-based hardware partner.
- **Project management and consultancy** - tool selection, planning, estimation, certification consultancy and quality assurance audits.
