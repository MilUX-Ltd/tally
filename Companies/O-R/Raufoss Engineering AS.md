# Raufoss Engineering AS

> Gjøvik maker of gun safes and weapon storage systems, with framework agreements with Norwegian defence bodies.

## What they do

Raufoss Engineering makes gun cabinets and weapon security systems, and takes on engineering projects in sheet metal, plastic and security products. Since 2016 it has held a framework agreement with Forsvarsbygg for fit-out of weapon stores, and in 2022 it signed an agreement with the Armed Forces logistics organisation for brackets for military vehicles.

## Products and solutions

- **Weapon store fit-out** - Modular storage system for weapon stores on military bases
- **Radio brackets** - Brackets for military vehicles
- **Gun cabinets** - Security cabinets for weapons
