# RMG Cyber Consulting

> Lincolnshire cyber security consultancy providing penetration testing, assurance and training.

## What they do

RMG Cyber Consulting Ltd is a cyber security consultancy established in 2021. Its services include web application, cloud and infrastructure penetration testing, auditing and assurance against Cyber Essentials, IASME, ISO 27001 and NCSC and NIST guidance, cyber security training, risk management, cloud security and software development. It lists private sector, public sector (including central government, nuclear, health and policing) and defence customers among its sectors. The company is based at Langworth, near Lincoln.

## Products and solutions

- **Penetration testing** - web application, cloud, infrastructure and enterprise black box testing.
- **Auditing and assurance** - Cyber Essentials and IASME assessment support, ISO 27001 and ISO 9001 implementation, and supply chain mapping.
- **Cyber security training** - training for staff and organisations.
- **Risk management and cloud security** - cyber risk management and cloud security advice.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Memberships:** Cloud Security Alliance partner; Greater Lincolnshire RDSC
