# Raplas Technologies

> UK manufacturer of industrial stereolithography (SLA) 3D printers and resins, including a platform for additive manufacturing of energetic materials.

## What they do

Raplas Technologies Ltd designs and builds large-format, production-grade stereolithography 3D printers, with its manufacturing headquarters at Windmill Park, Hayes Road, Barry, in the Vale of Glamorgan. Its machines are open to third-party resins and use software-led build automation, and the company also supplies engineering, general purpose and investment casting resins. It serves aerospace, defence, automotive, medical and dental, and education customers. Under a Ministry of Defence programme, Raplas states it is working with BAE Systems and Radnor Range to develop production units for processing energetic compositions, and in February 2025 it received Defence Technology Exploitation Programme funding for 3D printing work with BAE Systems.

## Products and solutions

- **SLA printers** - PR450, PR700, PR800/7 and PR400i industrial stereolithography systems.
- **NRG series** - additive manufacturing platform for controlled development and production of energetic materials, for suitably licensed users.
- **MDK+** - mobile development kit for resin trials.
- **Materials** - engineering, general purpose and investment casting resins.
- **Purchasing options** - financing, rental and trade-in schemes.
