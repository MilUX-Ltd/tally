# Repro Engineering

> Hampshire CNC precision engineering company machining turned and milled components for aerospace, defence, medical and commercial customers.

## What they do

Repro Engineering is a precision engineering company based in Waterlooville, Hampshire, established in 1967. The company states that it specialises in high-volume parts machining and assembly from a 12,000 sq ft site, producing turned and machined components from 1mm to 300mm diameter, and holds finished stock for many customers. It serves the aerospace, defence, fluid power, electronics, marine, medical and motorsport sectors. It is registered on the IAQG OASIS aerospace supplier database and can produce first article reports to AS9102.

## Products and solutions

- **Sliding head turning** - Swiss-type CNC turning.
- **Fixed head turning** - multi-axis CNC turning.
- **Milling** - CNC milled components.
- **Chucking and grinding** - chucking work and grinding.
- **Assembly** - component assembly and finished stock holding.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Accreditations:** AS9100 Rev D; ISO 9001:2015 (certified by NQA); registered on the IAQG OASIS database.
- **Memberships:** South Central RDSC
