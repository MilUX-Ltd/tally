# People One Fifty

> London and Isle of Wight creative production agency producing CGI, animation, film and digital content for technical organisations.

## What they do

People One Fifty describes itself as a human-led, AI-enhanced creative production team specialising in AI and live-action filmmaking, CGI, animation and digital content for organisations developing ideas, products and technologies. The company states that it assembles and manages specialist creative talent for each project through its network, The Creation Population, and takes responsibility for production from concept to delivery. It works from London and the Isle of Wight.

## Products and solutions

- **CGI and visualisation** - high-fidelity CGI and visualisation of products and technical systems.
- **Filmmaking** - AI and live-action filmmaking.
- **Animation, motion graphics and VFX** - animated and motion content.
- **AR and VR** - augmented and virtual reality content.
- **Creative strategy** - ideation, creative direction, branding and production management.
