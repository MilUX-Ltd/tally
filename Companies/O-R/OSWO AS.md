# OSWO AS

> Horten subcontractor making mechanical and electromechanical assemblies for the electronics and defence industry.

## What they do

OSWO is a subcontractor delivering CNC machining, sheet metal work, painting, welding, electromechanical assembly and construction. It supplies mechanical and electromechanically assembled products to Norwegian and international electronics and defence industry. It is certified to ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and AQAP 2110 and is audited by the Armed Forces.
