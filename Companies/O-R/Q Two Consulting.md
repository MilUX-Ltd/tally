# Q Two Consulting

> Hampshire quantity surveying and project management practice working on residential, commercial, defence and other construction projects.

## What they do

Q Two Consulting is the trading name of Q Two Consultancy Limited, a quantity surveying and project management practice. It states that it provides pre-contract and post-contract support across all stages of a construction project, with experience of traditional, design and build, partnering, JCT and NEC contracts. It lists the sectors it works in as prime residential, commercial, defence and security, student accommodation, industrial and marine, and includes a project for Thales Alenia Space among its published work.

## Products and solutions

- **Quantity surveying** - cost planning, feasibility studies, procurement and tendering, bills of quantities, valuations and change control.
- **Project management** - programming, consultant team appointments, contract administration and employer's agent services.
- **Project monitoring** - bank, fund and tenant monitoring and funder due diligence.
- **Expert witness and advisory** - claims management and advocacy in adjudication.
