# Pragmatics 3D

> Lincoln change management consultancy helping public sector organisations deliver large-scale programmes and projects.

## What they do

Pragmatics 3D Limited describes itself as a change management consultancy that helps public sector organisations deliver large-scale change on time and on budget. It organises its services into discover, develop and deliver stages, covering organisational strategy, delivery models and capabilities, and governed delivery of change. It references the MoP, P3O, MSP and PRINCE2 methodologies and states it has worked with policing, fire, prison and security organisations.

## Products and solutions

- **Discover** - defining and developing organisational strategies.
- **Develop** - building delivery models, strategies and organisational capabilities.
- **Deliver** - managing change to set timescales with transparent governance.
