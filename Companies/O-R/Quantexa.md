# Quantexa

> London decision intelligence software company connecting data for financial crime, fraud, risk and public sector use.

## What they do

Quantexa describes itself as a decision intelligence company that connects fragmented data to reveal real-world relationships for human and AI decision-making. Its platform includes data ingestion, entity resolution, graph analytics and AI capabilities, with solutions for financial crime, anti-money laundering, fraud, risk, customer intelligence, supply chain intelligence and AI sovereignty. It serves banking, insurance, telecommunications, health and social care, public sector and law enforcement customers, including border, security and customs risk detection. Its UK headquarters is at 10 York Road, London.

## Products and solutions

- **Decision Intelligence Platform** - data ingestion, entity resolution, graph analytics, Quantexa AI, Q Assist and Agent Gateway.
- **Financial crime and AML** - anti-money laundering, know your customer and fraud solutions, including Quantexa Cloud AML.
- **Public sector and law enforcement** - fraud detection across government programmes and entity resolution for investigations.
- **Supply chain intelligence** - supply chain risk and intelligence.
