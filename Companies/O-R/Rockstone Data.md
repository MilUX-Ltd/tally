# Rockstone Data

> Hampshire software research and development company building AI workflow automation, mobile apps, data platforms and proofs of concept.

## What they do

Rockstone Data Ltd describes itself as a software research and development company based in Hampshire. It states that it designs agentic AI to automate multi-step workflows, develops minimum viable products and proofs of concept that turn research and intellectual property into prototypes, and supports university teams with software processes. It also lists big time-series data capture, visualisation and analytics among its services.

## Products and solutions

- **AI transformation** - agentic AI that automates workflows and integrates with existing systems.
- **Minimum viable products** - rapid agile development of early-stage products.
- **Proof of concept** - turning research and intellectual property into commercial prototypes.
- **University collaboration** - software process and research management support for academic teams.
- **Time-series data** - data capture, visualisation and analytics.
