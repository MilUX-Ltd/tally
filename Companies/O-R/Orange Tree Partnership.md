# Orange Tree Partnership

> Hampshire consultancy providing bid and proposal support, business analysis and business workshops.

## What they do

Orange Tree Partnership Ltd is a Hampshire-based consultancy providing bid support and business workshops to businesses across the UK and Europe. Its stated services cover bid process review, proposal development, business case development workshops, business analysis, business coaching and strategic change. It lists space, construction, IT and government among its key industries. The company is based at the BASE Bordon Innovation Centre.

## Products and solutions

- **Bids and proposals** - bid process review and proposal development.
- **Business case workshops** - business case development workshops.
- **Business analysis and coaching** - business analysis, business coaching and mentoring.
- **Strategic change** - support for organisational and strategic change.
