# Oskar Pedersen AS

> Kristiansand supplier of equipment and solutions for Nordic and NATO defence forces since 1925.

## What they do

Oskar Pedersen has supplied defence equipment since 1925, working with partner manufacturers to provide solutions to Nordic and NATO armed forces. Its site lists supply of the Nordic Combat Uniform and a contract award for one-person tents.

## Products and solutions

- **Nordic Combat Uniform** - Supply of a uniform system for Nordic defence forces
- **One-person tents** - Tents supplied under a contract with partners
