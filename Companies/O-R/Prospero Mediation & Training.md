# Prospero Mediation & Training

> Nottinghamshire provider of workplace mediation, conflict coaching and conflict management training.

## What they do

Prospero Mediation & Training provides workplace conflict management, mediation and training across the UK from Retford, Nottinghamshire. Its services include interpersonal, workplace and community mediation, conflict coaching and DISC profiling, and courses on conflict management, equality, diversity and inclusion, unconscious bias, listening skills and mediation skills for professionals. The business states it is veteran-owned and shows armed forces organisations among its clients.

## Products and solutions

- **Mediation** - interpersonal, workplace and community mediation.
- **Conflict coaching** - one-to-one coaching on managing conflict.
- **DISC profiling** - behavioural profiling.
- **Training courses** - conflict management, equality, diversity and inclusion, unconscious bias, active and reflective listening, and mediation skills, with some courses CPD accredited.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Accreditations:** CPD Group accredited provider; Veteran-Owned UK certified.
- **Memberships:** Greater Lincolnshire RDSC
