# Radome.no AS

> Norwegian company designing, making, installing and servicing radomes for aviation, defence, SatCom and weather radar.

## What they do

Radome.no specialises in the design, manufacturing, installation and after-market service of radomes. Its clients are in civil aviation, defence, SatCom and weather radar. It states that each radome is tailored to the client's antenna system, supported by electromagnetic analysis and verification tools.

## Products and solutions

- **Radomes** - Protective covers for radar and satellite antenna systems
