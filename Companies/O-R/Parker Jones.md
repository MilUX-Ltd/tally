# Parker Jones

> Farnham-based consultancy and recruitment business supplying talent and project support to defence, government, cyber security and built environment organisations.

## What they do

Parker Jones Recruitment Solutions Limited is registered in Farnham, Surrey and trades with the Parker Jones Group, which the website describes as a solutions-based consultancy established in 2019 and based in the South of England. The company states that it serves defence, cyber security, built environment and services organisations, with defence and government, and IT and cyber security, among its sectors. Its offer covers project support, talent and workforce solutions, and digital threat and intelligence solutions.

## Products and solutions

- **Talent and workforce solutions** - recruitment and resourcing for defence, government and cyber security clients.
- **Project support** - support across its business areas.
- **Digital threat and intelligence solutions** - cyber-related services.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Accreditations:** Cyber Essentials
- **Memberships:** Recruitment and Employment Confederation (REC) corporate member
