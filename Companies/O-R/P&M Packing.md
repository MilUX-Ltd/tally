# P&M Packing

> Eastleigh specialist packer and transporter for export, dangerous goods, aerospace and defence items, with flight case manufacture and warehousing.

## What they do

P&M Packing designs, manufactures and delivers packing for large, fragile, valuable, confidential and dangerous items, and is IATA, IMDG and ADR approved for air, sea and road. Its 2.5 acre site in Eastleigh includes 20,000 sq ft of indoor space with areas for dangerous goods packing, export packing, carpentry, flight case manufacture and secure storage, under 24-hour surveillance. For aerospace and defence customers it produces bespoke packing for items such as radar domes, satellite handling frames and hazardous aerosols, on site or at its own facility.

## Products and solutions

- **Flight cases** - bespoke, used and hire flight cases.
- **Aerospace and defence packing** - bespoke, secure packing for delicate, valuable and dangerous items.
- **Export and international shipping packing** - including stamped, heat-treated timber.
- **Dangerous and hazardous goods packing** - including lithium battery packing.
- **Warehousing and storage** - secure storage and container rental.
