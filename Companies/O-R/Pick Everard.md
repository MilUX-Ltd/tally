# Pick Everard

> Multi-disciplinary built environment consultancy with offices across the UK and a defence estates track record, part of Artelia Group.

## What they do

Pick Everard is a multi-disciplinary consultancy delivering buildings and infrastructure projects for public and private sector clients across sectors including health, education, blue light, culture and heritage, digital infrastructure and defence. It states that its teams have over 30 years of experience in defence, with an understanding of defence estates' processes, procedures and security requirements. It lists offices in Birmingham, Bristol, Bury St Edmunds, Cardiff, Derby, Glasgow, Leeds, Leicester, London, Manchester, Nottingham and Sheffield, and gives Halford House in Leicester as its data protection address. The firm states it is part of Artelia Group.

## Products and solutions

- **Multi-disciplinary consultancy** - design, engineering and project delivery for buildings and infrastructure.
- **Defence estates** - project work within defence estate processes and security requirements.
- **Public sector programmes** - health, education, blue light and heritage projects.
- **Market insight** - published construction market reports.
