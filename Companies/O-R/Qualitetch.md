# Qualitetch

> Cambridgeshire photo chemical etching manufacturer of precision metal components, with wire erosion, stamping and laser cutting.

## What they do

Qualitetch Components Ltd is a photo chemical etching manufacturer based in March, Cambridgeshire. It states it produces precision etched metal components in materials including stainless steel, copper, brass, aluminium, nickel silver and molybdenum, and also offers EDM wire erosion, metal stamping and punching, laser cutting, forming, CNC machining, plating and assembly. It lists aerospace, defence projects, electronics, medical devices, telecommunications and renewable energy among the markets it serves, and states it holds Rolls-Royce approval for photo etching.

## Products and solutions

- **Photo chemical etching** - precision etched metal components and photo-tooling.
- **EDM wire erosion** - wire erosion machining.
- **Stamping and laser cutting** - metal stamping, punching and laser cutting.
- **Secondary operations** - forming, CNC machining, plating and finishing and assembly.
- **Fuel cell plates** - bipolar plates for hydrogen fuel cells and electrolysers.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Accreditations:** AS9100D, ISO 9001:2015 and ISO 14001:2015 (certified by NQA); JOSCAR accredited; Rolls-Royce approval for photo etching.
- **Memberships:** South Central RDSC
