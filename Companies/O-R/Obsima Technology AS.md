# Obsima Technology AS

> Norwegian supplier of tactical electronics, tactical training equipment, modular shooting ranges, simulators and support to defence and emergency services.

## What they do

Obsima Technology AS supplies tactical electronics, tactical training equipment, modular shooting ranges, foldable containers, meteorological sensors and simulators. Its website has a defence section describing components and technology for OEMs and system integrators building military systems. It also serves police, fire and special response teams, and offers service and support.

## Products and solutions

- **Tactical electronics** - Headsets, radios, antennas, video encoding and power supplies
- **Tactical training** - Targets, ballistic rubber and target materials
- **Modular shooting ranges** - Shooting range systems
- **Foldable containers** - Containers for temporary infrastructure
- **Simulators** - Training simulators
