# R-TECH Group

> Port Talbot materials testing, failure analysis and consultancy business, formerly trading as R-TECH Materials, with UKAS and Nadcap accredited laboratories.

## What they do

R-TECH Group, formerly R-TECH Materials, provides independent materials testing, analysis and consultancy from its laboratory at Kenfig Industrial Estate, Margam, Port Talbot. It is the brand for two companies: R-TECH Services Ltd, a UKAS and Nadcap accredited testing business founded in 2002, and R-TECH Consultants, which carries out materials investigations, failure analysis and plant integrity work. It specialises in steel for concrete structures and in polymers and composites. Defence is one of its listed industries, alongside aerospace, marine, power generation and construction.

## Products and solutions

- **Composites and non-metallics testing** - accredited mechanical and physical testing of polymers and composites.
- **Metallics testing** - including testing of steel for concrete structures.
- **Failure analysis and investigation** - laboratory-based root cause investigations.
- **Consultancy** - plant integrity, welding consultancy, expert witness work, field replication metallography and on-site support.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Accreditations:** UKAS accredited testing; Nadcap (non-metallic materials testing); ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and ISO 45001; Cyber Essentials.
- **Memberships:** Defence Wales; Aerospace Wales Forum directory listing.
