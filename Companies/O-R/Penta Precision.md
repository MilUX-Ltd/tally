# Penta Precision

> Portchester subcontract CNC machining company producing precision components for defence, aerospace, medical, scientific and other industries.

## What they do

Penta Precision is a UK subcontract CNC machining company based on the Murrills Estate in Portchester, Fareham. It states that it was founded by five machinists and has been established since 1998, specialising in CNC milling and turning and batch production in materials including aluminium, stainless steel, titanium, copper alloys and engineering plastics. For defence customers it states that it regularly manufactures enclosures, bracketry, bespoke heatsinks, instrumentation, electronic chassis, fittings and lock handles, with full traceability and BPSS-ready personnel. It also serves aerospace, pharmaceutical and medical, scientific, electronics, marine and research and development customers.

## Products and solutions

- **CNC milling** - 3, 4 and 5 axis milling of tight-tolerance components.
- **CNC turning** - turned components for batch and repeat production.
- **Prototype machining** - prototypes where part of a pathway to volume production.
- **Design for manufacture** - design advice to reduce machining cost and lead time.
- **Finishing processes** - chromic acid, sulphuric acid and hard anodising.
