# Precision Products (Portsmouth)

> Havant manufacturer of airshafts, safety chucks and roll handling equipment for the paper, packaging and converting industries.

## What they do

Precision Products (Portsmouth) Limited manufactures a range of roll handling machinery for the paper industry, including airshafts, mechanically expanding shafts and chucks, and safety chucks. The company states that all products are designed and manufactured in-house using CNC machine tools, and that it supplies customers worldwide. Industries it lists include packaging materials, labels, printing, converting, tapes, plastic film and paper slitting. It also offers a repair and refurbishment service for all makes of airshaft.

## Products and solutions

- **Airshafts and airchucks** - pneumatic and mechanical shafts and chucks for securing reels.
- **Safety chucks** - standard and custom safety chucks.
- **Handling equipment** - roll trolleys, shaft trolleys, roll turners and lifting mandrels.
- **Rollers** - rollers and bowed rollers for crease removal, slit separation and web spreading.
- **Spare and bespoke parts** - valves, gripping pads, hoses and inflator guns, plus airshaft repair and refurbishment.
