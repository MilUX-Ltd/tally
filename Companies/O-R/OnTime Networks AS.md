# OnTime Networks AS

> Makes rugged Ethernet switches, routers and GNSS time servers for aerospace and defence platforms.

## What they do

OnTime Networks designs rugged Ethernet and timing equipment for the aerospace and defence industries, founded in 2000. Its products carry military compliance ratings for airborne, ground and naval use. Its website cites selection of its router and time server for a mobile air surveillance radar system and of a switch for an armoured combat support vehicle.

## Products and solutions

- **Rugged Ethernet switches** - Flight-rated and ground or shipboard switches
- **Military-grade routers** - Rugged routers for defence platforms
- **GNSS time servers** - GPS and GNSS time servers and grandmaster clocks
- **Network taps** - Fully rugged network taps
- **Embedded computers** - 3U VPX and mission management computers
