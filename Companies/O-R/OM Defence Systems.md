# OM Defence Systems

> Developer of autonomous counter-UAS interceptors and a medium-lift UAV platform for infantry and field support.

## What they do

OM Defence Systems states that it builds autonomous defence systems, developed by engineers with defence and operational experience in Ukraine and Europe. Its products include the Kestrel autonomous counter-UAS interceptor system and the Shadow medium-lift UAV for logistics, ISR relay and infantry support, both with ground control software designed for jammed and GNSS-denied environments. The company states that in 2026 its systems completed SAPIENT compliance and interoperability testing with the British Army. It recruits engineering staff in Ukraine, Poland and Germany; its UK company, OMD Systems Limited, is registered in Havant.

## Products and solutions

- **Kestrel** - autonomous counter-UAS interceptor system with automatic launch, GNSS-denied navigation, EO and infrared targeting, and 5-inch and 7-inch interceptor variants.
- **Shadow** - folding multirotor medium-lift UAV carrying payloads up to 15 kg for ISR sensors, communications relay and mission equipment.
- **Ground control and C2 software** - mission planning, telemetry and payload control over a frequency-hopping RF link for degraded and jammed environments.
