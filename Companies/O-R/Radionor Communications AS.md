# Radionor Communications AS

> Trondheim company making military tactical broadband radios based on digital phased array technology.

## What they do

Radionor Communications develops digital phased array radios for military tactical broadband links. Its radios use beamforming for point-to-multipoint connections with jamming resilience, and are offered for land, sea, unmanned and infrastructure uses. It describes more than 25 years of research and development.

## Products and solutions

- **I-BEAM series** - Point-to-multipoint tactical data link radios
- **BLACKBEAM** - Phased array radio line for demanding missions
