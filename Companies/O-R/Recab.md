# Recab

> Nordic supplier of rugged embedded and industrial computers, with defence as one of its named industries.

## What they do

Recab supplies embedded systems and rugged industrial computers, including modular computers, single board computers and displays. Its site lists defence as an industry and has a defence computer product category and defence AI computers. It also offers software integration and embedded development.

## Products and solutions

- **Defence computers** - Rugged embedded computers for defence use
- **Defence AI computers** - Rugged AI computing platforms
- **VPX and VME systems** - Modular computer boards and enclosures
