# Raabakk Manufacturing

> CNC machining company in Fredrikstad making precision components and aluminium enclosures for several industries, including defence.

## What they do

Raabakk Manufacturing delivers CNC-machined components using five-axis machining, with quality control and surface finishing. It produces aluminium enclosures to protect electronics in harsh environments. Its site names defence among the industries it serves.

## Products and solutions

- **CNC machined components** - Precision milled parts
- **Aluminium enclosures** - Casings for electronics in harsh environments
