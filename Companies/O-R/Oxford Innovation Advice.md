# Oxford Innovation Advice

> Oxford-based provider of publicly funded business support programmes for SMEs, covering finance, growth, innovation and manufacturing.

## What they do

Oxford Innovation Advice designs, mobilises and delivers business support programmes for small and medium-sized enterprises across the UK, working with policymakers and funders. The company states it has supported over 34,000 SMEs and offers support in four areas: finance, growth, innovation and manufacturing. It also produces data-driven insight reports for policymakers and funders on SME policy, including work on procurement-ready manufacturing supply chains. Its registered company is Oxford Innovation Services Limited, with offices in Oxford and Melton Mowbray.

## Products and solutions

- **Finance support** - helping businesses identify and secure finance for growth.
- **Growth support** - business growth and scale-up support.
- **Innovation support** - support to bring new products and services to market.
- **Manufacturing support** - helping manufacturers develop processes and systems.
- **Insight reports** - SME data and policy reports for policymakers and funders.
