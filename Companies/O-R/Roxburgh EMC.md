# Roxburgh EMC

> Scunthorpe manufacturer of EMC filters, chokes and surge suppressors, with pre-compliance EMC testing.

## What they do

Roxburgh EMC Solutions is a division of Alpha 3 Manufacturing Limited and designs and manufactures EMC filters, chokes, surge suppressors and bespoke filter solutions from a 40,000 square foot facility in Scunthorpe. The company states that it was founded over 50 years ago and offers pre-compliance EMC testing at customer sites or in its UK laboratory. For aerospace and defence, it states that it designs bespoke EMC filters to customer parameters and standards including DEF STAN and AS9100, for applications such as aircraft electronics, missile systems, marine applications, military vehicles, avionics, radars and radios.

## Products and solutions

- **EMC filters** - mains, IEC inlet and small chassis filters, including low earth leakage options.
- **Bespoke filter design** - custom EMC filters for specific parameters and standards.
- **RFI chokes and RC networks** - interference suppression components.
- **Transient and surge protection** - transient suppressors and surge protection devices.
- **Pre-compliance EMC testing** - on-site or laboratory testing ahead of certification.
