# Quirk Solutions

> Callington consultancy working on organisational culture, leadership and change when strategy, culture and leadership misalign.

## What they do

Quirk Solutions says it partners with organisations to resolve the gridlock that emerges when strategy, culture and leadership misalign during growth, using frameworks that help people develop their own solutions. Its services cover change and transformation, culture diagnostics, cultural due diligence for mergers and acquisitions, and leadership and team development. The client logos on its website include defence and government organisations.

## Products and solutions

- **Quirk Academy** - training on organisational culture, business stress testing and growth strategy, as recorded content, workshops or in-depth application.
- **Leadership and Teams** - short leadership and team modules delivered over two to three days.
