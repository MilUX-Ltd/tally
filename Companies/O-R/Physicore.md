# Physicore

> London developer of mission orchestration software and autonomous aircraft for defence and critical infrastructure.

## What they do

Physicore develops software that brings sensors, aircraft and other machines from different manufacturers into one operational picture and coordinates the response, with authorised operators keeping control of consequential decisions. For defence it covers reconnaissance, force protection, base defence and counter-drone work; it also serves energy, critical infrastructure, maritime and public safety customers. It is designed and built in Britain, and works with manufacturers and solution partners through a partner network.

## Products and solutions

- **Meridian** - mission orchestration software: a shared operational picture across compatible third-party systems, coordinating the next action and recording the result.
- **Savant** - mission intelligence that assesses evidence and recommends the next action within operator-defined limits.
- **PX-1** - station-based autonomous aircraft under development for site security, inspection and base defence.
- **PX-7** - long-range autonomous patrol aircraft under development for borders, coastlines, power lines and pipelines.
