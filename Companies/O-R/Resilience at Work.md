# Resilience at Work

> Poole consultancy providing resilience and leadership training, offsite days, learning design and executive coaching.

## What they do

Resilience at Work was founded in 2017 to help organisations build resilient, high-performing teams and leaders. It works through training and consultancy, offsite and away days that use adventurous activities, design of bespoke learning and development programmes, and executive coaching. It states that it works with corporate and public sector organisations as well as individual leaders, and that its approach draws on occupational psychology and military leadership experience.

## Products and solutions

- **Training and consultancy** - resilience and performance training for organisations.
- **Offsite and away days** - experiential days to build trust and resilience in teams.
- **Learning and development design** - bespoke programmes for an organisation's challenges.
- **Executive coaching** - coaching packages for leaders.
