# Proofpoint

> International cybersecurity company protecting email, collaboration tools, data and AI use, with UK offices in Reading and London.

## What they do

Proofpoint is a cybersecurity company whose products cover collaboration and email security, data security, digital communications governance and security for the use of AI by people and agents. Its platform includes threat protection for email delivered by API or gateway, email fraud defence and DMARC, security awareness training, data loss prevention, insider threat management and data security posture management. It lists offices worldwide, with UK offices at Green Park in Reading and in London. In the UK it trades through Proofpoint Limited.

## Products and solutions

- **Core Email Protection** - threat protection via API or gateway.
- **Email Fraud Defence and Secure Email Relay** - brand protection, deliverability and DMARC.
- **ZenGuide** - risk-based security awareness training.
- **Data security** - enterprise and adaptive email DLP, insider threat management and data security posture management.
- **Digital Communications Governance** - capture, retention and discovery of digital communications.
- **AI security** - visibility and control of AI use by employees and agents.
- **Proofpoint Nexus** - AI and threat intelligence for threat detection and data risk assessment.
