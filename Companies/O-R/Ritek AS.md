# Ritek AS

> Mechanical workshop in Levanger for service and maintenance of heavy vehicles, with a business area for the Norwegian Armed Forces.

## What they do

Ritek AS is a mechanical workshop for service and maintenance of heavy vehicles and equipment. Its website lists three business areas: defence (defence technology and strategic projects with the Armed Forces as customer), industry and civil work. The civil side covers repair, damage repair and surface treatment of heavy vehicles.
