# PHINIA

> NYSE-listed supplier of fuel systems, electrical systems and aftermarket products, with aerospace offerings for military and civil aviation.

## What they do

PHINIA Inc. (NYSE: PHIN) describes itself as an independent provider of premium solutions and components, with brands including DELPHI, DELCO REMY and HARTRIDGE. It separated from BorgWarner and began trading on the New York Stock Exchange in 2023, and is based in Auburn Hills, Michigan. The company develops fuel systems, electrical systems and ignition systems for combustion engines, alongside hydrogen and alternative fuel technologies. For aerospace, it states that its fuel system and combustion expertise serves both military and civil aviation markets, with two aerospace production programmes launching in 2026. Its UK operating company is PHINIA Delphi UK Ltd, formerly Delphi Diesel Systems, registered at Stonehouse in Gloucestershire.

## Products and solutions

- **Fuel systems and pumps** - gasoline direct injection and diesel fuel injectors, pumps and rails.
- **Hydrogen and alternative fuel systems** - hydrogen, CNG and LNG fuel injectors.
- **Electrical systems** - starters and alternators for light and commercial vehicles.
- **Aerospace** - fuel system and combustion technology for military and civil aviation, including hydrogen and sustainable aviation fuel expertise.
- **Test equipment** - HARTRIDGE fuel injection testing technology and calibration systems.
