# PME Group

> Plymouth importer of MAN marine engines for the UK and Ireland, supplying and supporting marine power systems for commercial, leisure and government vessels.

## What they do

PME Power Systems Group Ltd, trading as PME Group, is the UK and Ireland importer for MAN marine engines. It supplies, services and supports marine engines and power systems for commercial, leisure and government customers, from specification through installation, servicing and parts. It lists defence and government marine applications among its sectors. The company is based at Langage Business Park in Plymouth.

## Products and solutions

- **MAN marine engines** - I6, V8 and V12 engine ranges.
- **Hybrid and dual-fuel systems** - hybrid drives and dual-fuel hydrogen-diesel engines.
- **Emissions systems** - SCR exhaust aftertreatment and scrubbing systems.
- **Generators and transmissions** - Nanni marine generators, ZF transmissions and control systems.
- **RePower and ReMan** - engine repowering and remanufacture programmes.
- **Aftersales support** - genuine MAN parts, servicing and a dealer network.
