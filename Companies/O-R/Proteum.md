# Proteum

> Southampton marine engine supply and support business within SC Group.

## What they do

Proteum supplies marine engines and propulsion products and provides servicing, maintenance, repair, installation, commissioning and emergency engine refits. Its stated product portfolio includes OXE Diesel outboards, FNM Marine, Bukh, Moteurs Baudouin, Masson Marine, Alamarin Jet, Konrad propulsion systems and Glendinning engine controls, supported by a network of dealers in the UK and abroad. Proteum is presented on the SC Group website alongside Supacat, SC Innovation and Blackhill, and its operating address is at Woolston, Southampton.

## Products and solutions

- **Marine engines and outboards** - distribution of OXE Diesel, FNM Marine, Bukh, Baudouin and Masson Marine products.
- **Propulsion and controls** - Alamarin Jet, Konrad propulsion systems and Glendinning engine controls.
- **Engine support** - servicing, maintenance, repairs, installation, commissioning and emergency engine refits, including overseas call-outs.
