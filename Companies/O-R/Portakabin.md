# Portakabin

> York-based designer, manufacturer and hirer of modular buildings and portable cabins, including military buildings for the defence estate.

## What they do

Portakabin designs, manufactures and supplies modular buildings and portable cabins, with hire, used sales, buy-back and aftercare services. It states it employs more than 2,200 people across 10 European countries, with its manufacturing headquarters at Huntington, York. For defence, it supplies modular military buildings to MoD estate managers, Tier 1 contractors and industry partners, including accommodation, welfare units, secure offices, command centres, medical units and workshops, with blast-resistant and ballistic options on request. It states it holds Cyber Essentials Plus and the Defence Employer Recognition Scheme Silver Award.

## Products and solutions

- **Modular buildings** - permanent-grade modular buildings.
- **Portable cabins** - cabin hire, used cabin sales and buy-back.
- **Defence buildings** - accommodation, secure offices, command centres, medical units, briefing rooms, storage and workshops.
- **Emergency response** - rapid supply of accommodation.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Accreditations:** Cyber Essentials Plus; ISO-accredited factories; British Safety Council International Safety Award (2026). Defence Employer Recognition Scheme Silver Award.
- **Memberships:** Greater Lincolnshire RDSC
