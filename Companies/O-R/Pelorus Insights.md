# Pelorus Insights

> Bath consultancy supporting portfolio, programme and project leaders with risk management, assurance and decision support.

## What they do

Pelorus Insights works with portfolio, programme and project (P3M) leaders to build capability, support delivery and improve performance. Its services cover risk management, including quantitative risk analysis, bid risk sprints and single source contracting risk advisory, alongside P3M leadership, programme recovery and assurance, and decision support such as planning and scheduling, cost management and integrated project controls. It publishes case studies on risk management in defence.

## Products and solutions

- **POPPIE** - a risk management capability improvement approach.
- **COURSE** - a decision quality approach.
- **Pelorus AI Assistant** - a chatbot giving access to the company's guidance on risk management and decision quality.
