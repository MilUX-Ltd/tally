# Pelican Insured

> Hampshire commercial insurance broker covering property, liability, professional indemnity, fleet and cyber insurance.

## What they do

Pelican Insured is a UK insurance broker for businesses; Pelican Insured and Cyber Insured are trading names of Pelican Business Insurance Services Ltd, which is authorised and regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority. It arranges property, commercial combined, liability, professional indemnity, motor fleet, cyber and contractors insurance. It lists industries including professional services, technology, construction, wholesale and manufacturing, and marine. The company is based at Swanwick Marina near Southampton.

## Products and solutions

- **Commercial insurance** - property, commercial combined, block of flats and contractors combined cover.
- **Liability insurance** - employers, public and product liability.
- **Professional indemnity** - cover for professional firms.
- **Cyber insurance** - cyber cover, also offered under the Cyber Insured name.
- **Motor fleet** - fleet insurance.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Accreditations:** BIBA Accredited Cyber Insurance Broker; authorised and regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority.
- **Memberships:** South Central RDSC
