# Resilient Leaders Elements

> Trowbridge leadership development company offering programmes, diagnostics and consultant accreditation built on its RLE Framework.

## What they do

Resilient Leaders Elements works to equip leaders, teams and organisations with clarity, confidence and resilience, and runs leadership development programmes for individuals, teams and whole organisations. Its RLE Framework has four elements: clarity of direction, resilient decision making, awareness and leadership presence. The company lists defence among its sectors, alongside education, healthcare and corporate organisations.

## Products and solutions

- **Leadership Development** - programmes for individuals, teams or organisations.
- **Organisational Diagnostic** - an assessment of leadership capability and culture.
- **Accreditation Programme** - certifies consultants to deliver the RLE Framework and programmes to their clients.
- **RLDP platform** - an online platform for programme delivery.
