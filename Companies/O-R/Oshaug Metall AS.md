# Oshaug Metall AS

> Molde foundry casting large nickel-aluminium bronze components, including for naval vessels, under the Copperstorm brand.

## What they do

Oshaug Metall casts and machines large components in nickel-aluminium bronze under the Copperstorm brand, with cast capacity up to 2.5 tonnes. Its products go to ships of several types, and its history page states work for British Navy destroyers and Norwegian Armed Forces frigates. It has operated since 1927 and completes all manufacturing steps in-house.

## Products and solutions

- **Copperstorm** - Cast and machined nickel-aluminium bronze components, such as propeller parts
