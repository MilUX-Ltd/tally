# Robot Aviation AS

> Hønefoss company that designs, builds and supports unmanned aircraft systems for defence surveillance, ISR and mission support.

## What they do

Robot Aviation designs, produces and supports modular unmanned aircraft systems for surveillance, ISR and mission support, including force protection and search and rescue. Systems are delivered with a SkyView ground control station, training and lifecycle support. The company also produces for third parties and states NATO STANAG 4586 interoperability.

## Products and solutions

- **SkyView GCS** - Single ground control station for the whole platform family
