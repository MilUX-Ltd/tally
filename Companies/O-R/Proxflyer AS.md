# Proxflyer AS

> Norwegian defence technology company developing unmanned surface and sub-surface foiling vessels.

## What they do

Proxflyer develops unmanned surface and sub-surface foiling vessels for maritime defence. Its Sea Shadow platform operates in foiling, surface, submerged and waterjet modes, and is described for surveillance, electronic warfare, covert resupply, communication relay and other mission profiles. The company is headquartered near Oslo with a US subsidiary.

## Products and solutions

- **Sea Shadow** - Unmanned surface and sub-surface foiling vessel for multi-domain missions
