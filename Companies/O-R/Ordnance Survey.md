# Ordnance Survey

> Great Britain's national mapping service, a government-owned company supplying geospatial data, APIs and maps to public and private sector customers.

## What they do

Ordnance Survey Limited is a limited company whose shares are wholly owned by the Secretary of State for Science, Innovation and Technology, and it describes itself as a public corporation, a trading body controlled by central government with day-to-day operating independence. It creates, maintains and distributes location information for Great Britain, recording around 600 million geospatial features in its National Geographic Database, and has mapped Great Britain since 1791. It states that its national security customers include defence and intelligence agencies, police and emergency services, and that it supplies data under the Public Sector Geospatial Agreement. Its head office is Explorer House in Nursling, Southampton.

## Products and solutions

- **OS data and APIs** - authoritative geospatial data products and APIs for business and the public sector.
- **National Geographic Database** - the maintained National Map of Great Britain's features.
- **Public Sector Geospatial Agreement** - premium data, APIs and guidance for public sector users.
- **OS Maps and OS Shop** - consumer mapping, paper maps and guidebooks.
- **Mapping services** - mapping for property boundaries, planning applications and elections.
