# Raptor CSL

> Bristol programme management, business change and technical consultancy working across defence, security and government.

## What they do

Raptor CSL is a programme management, business and technical consultancy founded in 2014, offering programme and project management, change management, digital transformation, systems engineering and integration, training management, acquisition support and bid support. It lists defence, cyber security, intelligence and security, emergency services, central government and industry as its market sectors. Its published case studies include research into technology options for improving the detection of concealed explosive devices for the Ministry of Defence.
