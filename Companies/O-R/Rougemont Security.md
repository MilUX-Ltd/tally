# Rougemont Security

> Basingstoke cyber security advisory firm offering risk and compliance advice and fractional CISO services to small and medium-sized businesses.

## What they do

Rougemont Security is a cyber security advisory firm that states it serves small and medium-sized businesses, identifying vulnerabilities and recommending strategies to protect digital assets and reputation. Its services cover board-level risk and compliance advice, fractional and interim Chief Information Security Officer services, consultancy and public speaking. It lists finance, manufacturing, retail, technology, aviation and government among the sectors it works with. The company states it has an office at Basing View, Basingstoke.

## Products and solutions

- **Advise** - strategic cyber risk and compliance guidance at management and board level.
- **Lead** - fractional and interim CISO services.
- **Consult** - consultancy for specific security challenges.
- **Cyber Navigator** - a cyber security solution offered by the company.
- **Security Scorecard** - an assessment of a business's cyber security weak points.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Accreditations:** Cyber Essentials (company-stated). The company also states its practitioners hold certifications from BCS, CIISec, CISM and the Cyber Leadership Institute.
- **Memberships:** South Central RDSC
