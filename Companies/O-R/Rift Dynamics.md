# Rift Dynamics

> Norwegian maker of low-cost unmanned systems for defence, including a strike drone and a target drone.

## What they do

Rift Dynamics develops unmanned systems for defence operations, designed for rapid, large-scale production in Norway. Its platforms are designed to meet Class 1A and military unmanned aircraft technical approval requirements. It lists an optional GNSS denied navigation capability.

## Products and solutions

- **Wåsp Strike** - Autonomous, low-cost strike drone
- **SKADI Target** - Target drone for air defence training
