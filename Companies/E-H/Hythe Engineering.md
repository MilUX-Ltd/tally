# Hythe Engineering

> Hampshire engineering contractor providing precision engineering, fabrication, welding and pipefitting services to defence, nuclear and renewable energy contractors.

## What they do

Hythe Engineering Limited provides precision engineering, specialist fabrication, welding and pipefitting services to defence, nuclear and renewable energy contractors. The company states that it is headquartered in Portsmouth, with workshops and support bases around the UK and a team holding full security clearance, and that it works alongside the Royal Navy and with principal contractors including BAE Systems, Rolls-Royce, Babcock and Siemens. Its services include reactive and responsive work, shipbuilding repair and refit, and bespoke or commercial off-the-shelf solutions. It states that its welding procedures follow British and ISO standards using Lloyd's rules, with Lloyd's coded welders and in-house welding inspectors.

## Products and solutions

- **Specialist welding** - coded welding including copper-nickel (CuNi) procedures.
- **Reactive and responsive work** - planned and on-call engineering support.
- **Shipbuilding repair and refit** - engineering services for naval and marine vessels.
- **Specialist fabrication** - bespoke fabrication and pipefitting.
- **First-article inspection** - validated inspection procedures for customers.
