# HITEK Electronic Materials

> North Lincolnshire supplier and fabricator of EMI shielding, thermal management, microwave absorber and adhesive materials for military, aerospace and defence electronics.

## What they do

HITEK Electronic Materials Ltd was founded in 1985 in Scunthorpe and supplies materials and expertise for protecting electronics, with more than 40 years in manufacturing and distribution. The company states its primary focus is the military, aerospace and defence sectors and that it is qualified in accordance with MIL-DTL-83528 QPL. It distributes products from brands including Parker Chomerics, Henkel, Permabond, ABS Technics and Shieldex, and offers fabrication, form-in-place gasketing, shielded enclosures and bespoke design. In March 2026 it became part of Aeromed Group, a US aerospace and defence supply chain company.

## Products and solutions

- **EMI shielding** - conductive gaskets, coatings and shielding materials.
- **Adhesives and encapsulants** - conductive and structural adhesives and potting compounds.
- **Thermal management** - thermally conductive interface materials.
- **Microwave absorbers** - broadband foam and other absorber materials.
- **Anti-corrosion** - anti-corrosion products and onsite support.
- **Fabrication services** - fabrication, form-in-place gasketing, shielded enclosures, sampling and down packing.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Accreditations:** AS9100D; BS EN ISO 9001:2015; ISO 14001; ISO 45001; Cyber Essentials; JOSCAR registered; MIL-DTL-83528 QPL qualification; SC21 recognition.
