# Frontier Resourcing

> Bristol recruitment firm supplying contract, permanent and project resource in IT, digital and engineering.

## What they do

Frontier Resourcing describes its work as partnering clients with the best talent, through contract resource, permanent hires, executive search and project solutions. It recruits in digital and IT and in engineering, and says it has supplied niche technology resources for the public and private sectors. Its project solutions offer outcome-focused delivery across IT, digital and engineering markets.
