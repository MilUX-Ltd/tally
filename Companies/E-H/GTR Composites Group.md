# GTR Composites Group

> West Sussex composite design and manufacturing group serving motorsport, automotive, aerospace and defence customers.

## What they do

GTR Composites Group is the trading identity of Global Technologies Racing Limited, a composite design and manufacturing business based at Denmans Lane, Fontwell, West Sussex. The company states that its heritage goes back to G-Force, established in 1991, which became Global Technologies Racing in 2003 with a motorsport focus, and that it now operates nine facilities on four sites. For defence it manufactures protected vehicle crew pods, lightweight vehicle armour panels, uncrewed systems components, radomes and enclosures, specialist seating and aerospace and naval structures. It states that its defence work has included composite aircraft components for the AW101 Merlin helicopter, components for Martin-Baker ejection seats and composite protected crew pods for the MOD's Foxhound vehicle.

## Products and solutions

- **Protection systems** - composite protected vehicle crew pods and ballistic composite armour panels.
- **Aerospace structures** - lightweight composite structures and components, and ejection seat components.
- **Radomes and enclosures** - electromagnetically transparent composite enclosures.
- **Uncrewed systems components** - composite parts for uncrewed systems.
- **Manufacturing services** - design and feasibility, patternmaking, prototyping, autoclave curing, hot pressing, kit cutting, machining, painting, assembly, inspection and testing.
