# Gurit UK

> Isle of Wight site of the Gurit group, manufacturing composite materials including epoxy resins, adhesives and prepregs for wind, marine and industrial markets.

## What they do

Gurit (UK) Limited is the UK company of Gurit, a group that manufactures and supplies advanced composite materials, including structural core materials, prepregs, epoxy resins and adhesives, glass profiles, and associated engineering and manufacturing services. The UK operation is based at St Cross Business Park in Newport, Isle of Wight, where the group's StrucPro epoxy resin and adhesive range was developed. For defence, Gurit states that its structural foam core and prepregs are used in applications including subsea drones, fast craft, radomes, UAVs and UUVs and launch systems, and that it supplies materials to commercial and military vessel builders.

## Products and solutions

- **Prepregs** - pre-impregnated composite materials for marine, industrial and defence applications.
- **Epoxy resins and adhesives** - including the StrucPro, Spabond and Ampreg ranges.
- **Core materials** - Corecell structural foam cores, including S-foam for subsea applications and M-foam for impact resistance.
- **Engineering services** - structural engineering and composite design support.
