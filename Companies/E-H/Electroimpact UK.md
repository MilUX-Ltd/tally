# Electroimpact UK

> UK arm of Electroimpact, designing, installing and supporting automated aircraft assembly equipment and tooling from Hawarden, Flintshire.

## What they do

Electroimpact UK Limited is the UK company of Electroimpact, a US-headquartered designer and manufacturer of automated aircraft assembly equipment whose customers include major airframe manufacturers. The UK company is based at Electroimpact Technology Park, Manor Lane, Hawarden, on Deeside in Flintshire. It provides warranty and maintenance support for the installed base of Electroimpact equipment in the UK, including riveting machines, fixtures and handheld riveting tools, with 24-hour maintenance cover. Its engineering work covers mechanical design, CNC software, machine build and installation, and aircraft tooling fixture installation.

## Products and solutions

- **Automated assembly equipment** - design, build, installation and debugging of automated aircraft assembly machines and CNC machinery.
- **Aircraft tooling** - fixture installation, precision machine bed installation and alignment, and fixture certification.
- **Metrology** - laser tracker metrology for machine and tooling set-up.
- **Maintenance and support** - warranty, 24-hour maintenance and programming support for installed Electroimpact equipment.
