# Hand and Millar Management Consulting

> Marshfield management consultancy supporting strategy, delivery and control of transformation programmes.

## What they do

Hand and Millar Management Consulting helps organisations with strategy (business approval and buy-in for digital transformation), delivery (working inside client transformation teams) and control (structure and rigour for projects and programmes). The firm says it supports complex defence programmes with strategic planning, governance and risk management, and also works in the public sector and in sport. Its published case studies cover portfolio risk management, programme management, delivery risk committees and business change readiness.
