# HETA

> Not-for-profit engineering training provider in the Humber delivering engineering apprenticeships, adult upskill courses and Higher Nationals.

## What they do

HETA (Humberside Engineering Training Association) describes itself as a not-for-profit charity Group Training Association that delivers apprenticeships and standard and bespoke upskill training courses. It was established in 1967 by a group of local companies and has training centres in Hull, Stallingborough and Scunthorpe. Its apprenticeship programmes cover electrical, mechanical, electrical installation, fabrication and welding, multiskilled and process operations disciplines, and it works with employers ranging from sole traders to multinationals.

## Products and solutions

- **Engineering apprenticeships** - electrical, mechanical, electrical installation, fabrication and welding, multiskilled and process operations.
- **Adult upskill courses** - standard and bespoke engineering training for employers.
- **Higher Nationals** - higher engineering qualifications.
- **Apprentice recruitment** - support for employers recruiting apprentices.
