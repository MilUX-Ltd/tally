# Fjord Defence

> Norwegian developer and manufacturer of weapon mounts, tripods and pedestals for ground, vehicle and maritime platforms.

## What they do

Fjord Defence develops and manufactures weapon mounts, tripods, pedestals and related systems for ground, vehicle and maritime use. It states that it develops and manufactures its systems in Norway. Maritime mounts are made in stainless steel for boats and naval platforms.

## Products and solutions

- **HMG Swing Arm** - Compact pivoting mount for heavy and medium machine gun stations
- **Dual Mount HMG/MK19** - Dual weapon mount
- **LMGT Tripod** - Tripod system for light machine guns
- **Maritime Mounts and Pedestals** - Stainless steel systems for patrol boats and naval platforms
- **Ground Mounts and Tripods** - Lightweight mounts with soft mount technology
