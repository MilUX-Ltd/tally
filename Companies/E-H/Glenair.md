# Glenair

> Maker of interconnect products such as connectors and cable assemblies for demanding environments including military.

## What they do

Glenair supplies interconnect technology to protect sensitive electronics in demanding environments. Its Nordic site lists military and aviation connectors, space and maritime connectors, backshells, hoses and cable assemblies.

## Products and solutions

- **Military and aviation connectors** - Connectors for defence and aerospace
- **Cable assemblies** - Custom cable assemblies
- **Protective hoses** - Metal and polymer conduit
