# EnableWorks

> Operational improvement consultancy for established UK founder-led manufacturing and services businesses.

## What they do

EnableWorks works with established UK founders of manufacturing and services businesses with turnover of about one to five million pounds, helping them remove operational bottlenecks, build team capability and protect margins. It offers a short operational diagnostic audit, hands-on operational transformation, and fractional operational leadership on a monthly basis. The company states its approach draws on operational experience across military service, commercial business transformation and trustee governance. EnableWorks Services Ltd was incorporated in 2026 with its registered office in Petersfield.

## Products and solutions

- **RevU Operational Diagnostic** - a two to three week audit of fulfilment workflows, team structures and bottlenecks, with a 90-day roadmap.
- **Core Operational Transformation** - hands-on work on system simplification, role accountability and team capability.
- **Fractional Operational Leadership** - embedded operational leadership on a flexible monthly basis.
