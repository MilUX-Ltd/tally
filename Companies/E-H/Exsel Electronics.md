# Exsel Electronics

> Welshpool software-centric systems integrator focused on information and cyber security, part of the Exsel Group.

## What they do

Exsel Electronics Limited is a software-centric systems integrator with a specialised focus on information and cyber security. The company states its core expertise is multi-domain communications, data and system management, and that it has worked with the UK Ministry of Defence on its data centric security system. It states it is part of the Exsel Group, a defence technology solutions company, and that its approach aligns with the UK National Cyber Security Strategy.

## Products and solutions

- **Need2Know (N2K)** - applies security classification to individual paragraphs, pictures or words within a single encrypted source file, with versions generated for each audience, for national security, military, medical and commercial users.
- **Data centric security (Bastion DCS)** - a data security model that encrypts data in use, in transit and at rest, with encrypted communities of interest controlling information flow.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Other:** The Exsel Group states it holds the Gold Award in the Ministry of Defence Defence Employer Recognition Scheme and is a signatory to the Armed Forces Covenant.
