# Geoptic

> Hampshire university spin-out providing passive muon imaging systems and services to reveal subsurface and structural density changes.

## What they do

Geoptic Infrastructure Investigations Limited describes itself as a UK university spin-out from three universities. It provides passive muon imaging systems and services that measure density changes in and around assets, for infrastructure, energy, nuclear, defence and research clients. Applications it lists include tunnels and buried infrastructure, viaducts and dense structures, and subsurface voids and geology. It has its main office in South Warnborough, Hampshire, with satellite offices in South Yorkshire and at NETPark, Sedgefield.

## Products and solutions

- **Muon imaging systems** - detectors deployed around or within assets to measure density.
- **MuonOS** - cloud platform for survey design, field data, density visualisation and engineering reporting.
- **Imaging surveys** - investigation of tunnels, buried infrastructure, viaducts and subsurface voids.
