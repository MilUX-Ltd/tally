# Eelume AS

> Developer of autonomous underwater vehicles, with defence missions in mine countermeasures, ISR and special operations.

## What they do

Eelume makes autonomous underwater vehicles. Its All-Terrain AUV combines wide-area sonar mapping, close-range optical imaging and precise navigation in one system. Its defence and security page lists mine countermeasures, intelligence, surveillance and reconnaissance, and special operations.

## Products and solutions

- **All-Terrain AUV** - Autonomous underwater vehicle combining sonar, optical imaging and navigation
- **Eelume S series** - Vehicle for rapid deployment and long-duration operations
