# Fortis DPC

> Surrey compliance consultancy and certification body for data protection, ISO standards, Cyber Essentials and Defence Cyber Certification.

## What they do

Fortis DPC Ltd is a UK compliance consultancy offering data protection and GDPR services, ISO standards support, cyber security, PCI DSS, DORA and AI standards services. It is a certification body for Cyber Essentials, IASME Cyber Assurance and Cyber Baseline, and for Defence Cyber Certification at Levels 0 and 1. The company states it has helped over 450 clients in the UK, Europe, South Africa and the USA. It is based at Downside, near Cobham, Surrey.

## Products and solutions

- **Data protection** - GDPR audits, outsourced DPO, DSAR support, DPIA support, training and an EU representative service.
- **ISO standards** - support for ISO 9001, 14001, 27001, 27701, 45001, 22301 and 42001.
- **Cyber Essentials certification** - Cyber Essentials and IASME certification as a certification body.
- **Defence Cyber Certification** - certification body for DCC Level 0 and Level 1.
- **Cyber risk services** - vulnerability scanning, cyber risk management and dark web monitoring.
- **PCI DSS and DORA** - payment card and financial operational resilience compliance.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Accreditations:** Cyber Essentials and Cyber Essentials Plus certified; Cyber Essentials, IASME Cyber Assurance and Cyber Baseline certification body; Defence Cyber Certification certification body.
- **Memberships:** UK Cyber Security Council (corporate member); South Central RDSC
