# Evoke Management

> UK provider of part-time finance and commercial directors and growth, succession and exit advice for SMEs.

## What they do

Evoke Management provides experienced directors to growing small and medium-sized businesses on a part-time basis, together with advice on growth strategy, succession planning, exit strategy and business valuation. The company states it works as an integral part of its clients' teams, offering external perspective and constructive input on issues vital to business success. It also advises on employee ownership trusts. Evoke Management Limited is registered in London.

## Products and solutions

- **Part-time finance directors** - finance director and CFO support on a part-time basis.
- **Part-time commercial directors** - commercial leadership on a part-time basis.
- **Business growth strategies** - growth planning for SMEs.
- **Succession and exit planning** - business succession and exit strategies.
- **Employee ownership trusts** - advice on transition to employee ownership.
- **Business valuation** - valuation services for business owners.
