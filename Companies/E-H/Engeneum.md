# Engeneum

> Reading-based provider of cloud document management, data capture and data preparation services.

## What they do

Engeneum states that it protects and manages digital assets, providing secure sharing and business workflows through a cloud-based digital asset management platform. Its services cover data capture, document conversion, data management and data distribution, including tools to harmonise, validate and normalise legacy data for use with AI. The company states that client data is held in a Microsoft Azure private cloud. Engeneum traces its history to a document management business formed in 1990 and adopted the Engeneum name in 2016.

## Products and solutions

- **Data capture** - document conversion, email and document classification, forms processing and supplier invoice capture.
- **Data management** - search, business process automation, compliance and digital records, and identity document storage.
- **Data distribution** - online payslips, HR and employee portals, finance document portals and e-invoice delivery.
- **AI data preparation** - tools to harmonise, validate and normalise data before AI use.
