# Halodyne AS

> Oslo start-up developing an autonomous counter-drone turret and sensor module for vehicles, unmanned ground vehicles and infrastructure.

## What they do

Halodyne develops autonomous counter-UAS systems to defend positions and vehicles against small drones, including FPV drones. Its HRS-2 is a bolt-on turret with onboard detection, identification and engagement, and can take cues from external radar, RF or acoustic sensors. The site lists membership of Norwegian and Nordic defence industry groups and approved bidder status with the NATO NCIA.

## Products and solutions

- **HRS-2** - Autonomous bolt-on counter-drone turret with onboard sensors and effector.
- **HRS-2R** - HRS-2 configuration guided by external radar or other detectors.
- **HALO-AI** - Sensor and AI module for third-party turrets and combat modules.
