# ESI Technology

> Wrexham manufacturer of pressure transducers and transmitters using silicon-on-sapphire sensor technology, for aerospace, defence, subsea, hydrogen and industrial applications.

## What they do

ESI Technology designs and manufactures pressure transducers and pressure transmitters at Wrexham Technology Park in North Wales. Founded in 1984, it states that its early designs were for demanding military and aerospace applications, and its range now measures from a few inches of water up to 5,000 bar. The company has been part of the German SUCO group since 2009. Its products are used in aerospace testing and ground support equipment, subsea, marine, hazardous-area and hydrogen applications.

## Products and solutions

- **General purpose and high pressure transmitters** - including the GS4200 and HP1000 ranges.
- **High precision transducers** - including the HI2000 range.
- **Downhole pressure transmitters** - HI5000 range.
- **Digital and USB pressure transmitters** - GD4200-USB with ESI-USB software.
- **Hazardous area and hydrogen ranges** - intrinsically safe and hydrogen compatible transmitters and transducers.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Accreditations:** AS9100D; ATEX and IECEx (intrinsically safe products); DNV approval for marine use.
- **Memberships:** Defence Wales; Aerospace Wales Forum directory listing.
