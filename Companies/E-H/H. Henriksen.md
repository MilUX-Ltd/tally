# H. Henriksen

> Designs and produces solutions for the defence and maritime industry, including mine countermeasures tooling.

## What they do

H. Henriksen designs and produces solutions for the defence and maritime industry. Its range includes mine countermeasure and maritime unmanned system products, hooks and a loadbar.

## Products and solutions

- **MCM** - Mine countermeasures
- **MUS** - Maritime unmanned systems
- **HOOKS** - Lifting and release hooks
- **SEA RESQ** - Rescue product
- **LOADBAR** - Loadbar product
