# Evodia

> Farnham recruitment and resourcing company serving the defence, aerospace, security and space sectors.

## What they do

Evodia Limited is a Surrey-based recruitment and resource solutions company. The company states it has provided resource augmentation, contingent labour and outcome-based delivery services to the defence, national security and advanced engineering sectors for nearly 30 years. It states it places security-cleared professionals in technical, engineering, digital and project delivery roles, on permanent, contract, interim and project-based terms. The company also has an office in Gloucester.

## Products and solutions

- **Aviation, aerospace and space** - recruitment across military and civil aviation, aerospace and space.
- **Submarines and ships** - recruitment for maritime programmes.
- **Land equipment and systems** - recruitment for land-based platforms and systems.
- **Safety engineering** - safety engineering roles in defence, aerospace, rail, automotive and nuclear.
- **Cyber security and digital** - cyber security and digital transformation roles.
- **Project controls, PMO and P3M** - programme and project control roles.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Accreditations:** JOSCAR registration; ISO 9001:2015 (UKAS certified); Cyber Essentials Plus.
- **Other:** Corporate member of the Recruitment and Employment Confederation; FCSA Recruiter Partner; signatory to the Armed Forces Covenant.
