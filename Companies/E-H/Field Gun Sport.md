# Field Gun Sport

> Southsea company promoting the team sport of Pro Field Gun and offering team building, business planning and ceremonial gun salute events.

## What they do

Field Gun Sport Ltd states that its aim is to establish field gun as a recognised international sport, as the successor to the Royal Navy's Command Field Gun competition, which ended in 1999. Alongside the sport it offers corporate team building packages for groups of up to 48, business planning days based on the military decision-making process and tactical exercises, and ceremonial gun salutes for weddings and other events. It also invites corporate sponsorship and investment.

## Products and solutions

- **Team building** - tailored packages from half a day to a week for single delegates up to groups of 48.
- **Business and project planning** - outdoor planning exercises drawing on military planning methods.
- **Ceremonial salutes** - gun salutes by a battery of three guns for weddings and events.
