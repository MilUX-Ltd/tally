# Glamox

> Lighting manufacturer with a naval and coast guard range alongside civil marine lighting.

## What they do

Glamox makes lighting for ships and other applications. Its navy and coast guard segment covers interior, open deck and navigation lighting, including night-vision compatible and military-tested systems.

## Products and solutions

- **Advanced Naval Lighting Systems** - Naval lighting and control
- **MIL-tested lights** - Lighting tested for naval shock, vibration and EMC
- **Navigation lights** - Night-vision compatible navigation lights
