# Flowlens

> Belfast software company providing cloud-based MRP and manufacturing execution software for small and medium equipment, device and machinery manufacturers.

## What they do

Flowlens Ltd develops a cloud-based ERP and MRP platform designed for small and medium manufacturers that build products to order, including equipment, devices and machinery with multi-level bills of materials. The company states that its software covers stock, sales, production, sub-contracting, purchasing, quality tracking and service management, with integrations to Xero, QuickBooks and Sage 50. It lists customers in sectors including measurement and test devices, sensors and detectors, drones and uncrewed aircraft systems, rail and maritime transport equipment, and subsea equipment. The company states it was established in 2006 and is based in Belfast.

## Products and solutions

- **Flowlens MRP and MES** - cloud software for production planning, shopfloor job tracking, bills of materials and costing.
- **Stock and purchasing** - real-time demand, purchase-to-order and supplier performance tracking.
- **Service management** - equipment service and cost tracking.
- **Accounting integrations** - native Xero and QuickBooks integration, with Sage 50 as an option.
- **Implementation services** - setup, training, data import, business mapping and manufacturing analytics.
