# Ferno Norden Military Systems AS

> Norwegian supplier of tactical combat casualty care and medical evacuation equipment to armed forces.

## What they do

Ferno Norden Military Systems specialises in equipment for tactical combat casualty care and medical evacuation for Nordic and European defence customers. Its site cites a framework agreement with the Norwegian Armed Forces for blood transport coolers. It is based in Skoppum.

## Products and solutions

- **TCCC equipment** - Tactical combat casualty care kit
- **FlexiPOD** - Rapidly deployable field clinic system
- **Blood Transport Equipment System** - Cooled blood transport for the field
- **MedEvac vehicles** - Converted civilian cargo vans
- **Project Heimdall** - Medical and evacuation equipment for forward-deployed medics
