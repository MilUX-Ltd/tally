# Fresh Eyes Consulting

> UK consultancy offering programme health checks, requirements management, systems engineering capacity and bid support.

## What they do

Fresh Eyes Consulting is a trading style of BMC Global Ltd, a management consultancy registered in England and Wales. The company states that it helps clients achieve project and business goals through programme and project health checks, delivery and execution support, project rescue, requirements management, systems engineering capacity and bid and proposal support. It also offers supply chain support such as independent programme health checks and sub-contractor liaison, and a monitoring and assurance service for investors. It states that it works on retainer, master service agreement, statement of work, time and materials or success fee terms.

## Products and solutions

- **Programme and project assurance** - programme and project health checks, delivery and execution support and project rescue.
- **Engineering support** - requirements management, design to value, systems engineering (requirements and ITEA) capacity and bid and proposal support.
- **Strategy and culture** - vision, mission and strategy workshops, culture optimisation and scenario and risk planning.
- **Supply chain support** - independent programme health checks, sub-contractor liaison and statement of work development.
- **Investor assurance** - monitoring and assurance of EBITDA-critical projects with quarterly maturity reports.
