# Glenside

> Reading-based family business providing commercial interiors, specialist fit-out and workplace consultancy for technical and secure environments.

## What they do

Glenside provides consultancy, design, technical advisory and fit-out delivery for offices, laboratories and specialist commercial spaces. The company states it was established in 1973 as a family-run business and has delivered more than 400 projects. It works across nine sectors, including advanced manufacturing, aerospace and space technology, defence and secure facilities, electronics and semiconductors, life sciences and pharmaceuticals. For defence and security organisations, it states it fits out and refurbishes specialist, controlled and operational environments.

## Products and solutions

- **Consultancy and advisory** - project scoping, feasibility, and early budget and programme guidance.
- **Collaboration and design** - space planning, layout development and design coordination.
- **Project delivery and fit-out** - managed fit-out delivery for offices, laboratories and specialist spaces.
- **Commissioning and handover** - completion checks and handover.
- **Ongoing support** - continued advice as workspace needs change.
