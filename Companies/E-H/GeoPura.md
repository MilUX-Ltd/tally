# GeoPura

> UK hydrogen power provider supplying green hydrogen and zero-emission Hydrogen Power Units on an energy-as-a-service basis.

## What they do

GeoPura Ltd describes itself as a UK hydrogen power provider that produces and supplies green hydrogen and offers Hydrogen Power Units (HPUs) on an energy-as-a-service model. It states its HPUs use Ballard fuel cells and are manufactured in the UK with Siemens Energy. The company lists construction, events, film and television, data centres, utilities, maritime and EV charging among its sectors, and names defence among the sectors its technology serves, stating it has powered EV infrastructure for the Ministry of Defence. It is a recipient of the King's Award for Enterprise in Innovation.

## Products and solutions

- **HPU1 and HPU2** - hydrogen-powered generators for industrial loads.
- **HPU-M** - mobile hydrogen power unit with integrated storage.
- **HPU Agile-H** - lightweight towable 80 kW hydrogen power unit.
- **Zero-emission fuels** - low-carbon hydrogen supply, generation and refuelling.
