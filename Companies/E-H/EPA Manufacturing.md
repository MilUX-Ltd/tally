# EPA Manufacturing

> Lincolnshire manufacturer of small arms ammunition, explosive ordnance disposal products and counter-drone solutions, part of EP Group.

## What they do

EPA Manufacturing is a UK defence company within EP Group Ltd that delivers bespoke defence projects from concept to completion. The company states that it specialises in small arms ammunition, explosive ordnance disposal and related defence products for the UK Ministry of Defence, CAST, and military and police units, and that it is licensed for energetics and pyrotechnics and Home Office approved for weapons and ammunition. It operates from a purpose-built facility near Wickenby, Lincolnshire, with internal and external test ranges for calibres up to 30mm. In March 2026 CBC Global Ammunition acquired a controlling shareholding in EP Group.

## Products and solutions

- **Small arms ammunition** - specialist projectiles including barrier, glass penetrating, armour piercing, frangible and close quarter battle types.
- **Counter-IED and EOD** - explosive ordnance disposal and counter-IED products.
- **Pyrotechnic cartridges** - cartridges for aircraft ejection seats.
- **Counter-drone** - counter-UAS research, development and manufacture through a dedicated division.
- **Testing facilities** - instrumented internal and external ranges for calibres up to 30mm, including high explosives.
