# EfficiencyIT

> Dorking specialist in data centre infrastructure, prefabricated modular data centres and cloud communications.

## What they do

EfficiencyIT Limited states that it was established in 2016 and provides consultancy, technology and services for data centres, critical IT infrastructure and communications environments. It designs, builds, installs and maintains infrastructure including UPS systems, power distribution, cooling, monitoring and data centre infrastructure management software, and states that its prefabricated modular data centres are built in the UK and can be deployed in under three months. It also supplies unified communications and cloud contact centre services. The company states that it holds a Royal Warrant as Supplier of IT Infrastructure and Services to His Majesty the King, and it has published material on resilient data centre infrastructure for defence operations.

## Products and solutions

- **Prefabricated and modular data centres** - UK-built modular data centres for rapid deployment.
- **Power and cooling** - single-phase and three-phase UPS, power distribution units and racks, server room cooling and liquid cooling.
- **Data centre infrastructure management** - DCIM and data centre optimisation software, and security and environmental monitoring.
- **Data centre services** - consultancy, design and build, and maintenance and support.
- **Cloud communications** - unified communications and cloud contact centres.
