# Fosstech

> Norwegian developer and manufacturer of electromechanics, potting and encapsulation, and cable and enclosure assemblies for demanding environments including defence.

## What they do

Fosstech designs and manufactures electromechanical solutions, including potting and encapsulation of electronics, cables, batteries and enclosures. It serves subsea, maritime, aquaculture, defence and industrial customers. Its site describes electromechanical enclosures and power supply units developed for advanced defence systems as a strategic partner of Kongsberg Defence & Aerospace, and manufacture of transducers.

## Products and solutions

- **Potting and encapsulation** - Protection of electronics, cables, batteries and components for harsh environments.
- **Cable and enclosure assembly** - Assembly and testing of enclosures, cable harnesses and electromechanical systems.
- **Development** - Design of mechanical and electromechanical solutions optimised for production.
