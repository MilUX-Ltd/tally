# HPI

> Independent French designer and manufacturer of custom hydraulic pumps and power packs, including aerospace pumps for civil and military aircraft.

## What they do

HPI SAS describes itself as an independent French company that has designed and manufactured custom hydraulic components since 1966. Design, prototyping, manufacturing, assembly and quality control are carried out at its production site in Chennevières-sur-Marne, and it states it has 180 staff. Its products serve agriculture, transport, material handling, medical technology and aerospace, and the company states its pump systems are used in civil and military aeronautics, including helicopters, fighter aircraft and other military vehicles. It sells through distributors and an online shop.

## Products and solutions

- **Aerospace range** - compact safety pumps and auxiliary electric pumps for landing gear, flight controls, wheel brakes, rotor brakes and winches.
- **Silent pumps** - low-noise gear pumps for noise-sensitive applications such as material handling.
- **Power packs and pumps** - hydraulic pumps and micro and mini power packs.
- **Services and support** - hydraulic engineering services alongside its products.
