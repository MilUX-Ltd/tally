# Erinacus Consulting

> Hampshire consultancy providing contracting strategy and contract management services, mainly to SMEs.

## What they do

Erinacus Consulting Limited was founded in November 2024 and provides contracting strategy and management services. The company states that it is aimed primarily at the SME market and supports clients across the contracting lifecycle, from reviewing business templates such as NDAs and standard terms to approval and handover processes, contract performance tracking and change management. It also supports sales teams with contract strategy and negotiations, and handles contract changes, disputes and supplier engagements. Published client testimonials include a business operating in the defence and energy sectors.

## Products and solutions

- **Contract templates review** - review and improvement of NDAs, standard terms and proposals.
- **Contract management processes** - approval, handover, performance tracking and change management systems.
- **Negotiation support** - contract strategy and negotiation support for sales teams.
- **Commercial coaching** - summaries, trackers and coaching for client teams.
