# EmLogic AS

> Norwegian embedded systems design centre offering FPGA, embedded software, hardware and DSP design, with experience in space, maritime and defence.

## What they do

EmLogic is an embedded systems design centre in Norway. It provides FPGA design, embedded software, hardware design, digital signal processing and edge AI, from specification through to verification and test. Its own site lists defence among the other domains where its engineers have experience, alongside space, maritime, health and energy.
