# GKN Aerospace Norway

> Aerospace engine component maker at Kongsberg, including parts for the F135 engine of the F-35.

## What they do

GKN Aerospace Norway manufactures engine components at Kongsberg, which is the group centre of excellence for shafts. It produces diffuser cases, low pressure turbine cases and shafts for the F135 engine. The site also makes parts for other civil and military engines.

## Products and solutions

- **F135 engine components** - Diffuser cases, turbine cases and shafts
