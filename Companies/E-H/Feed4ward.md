# Feed4ward

> Portsmouth industrial automation company designing PLC, SCADA and control panel systems for pharmaceutical, industrial, defence and maritime customers.

## What they do

Feed4ward Control Ltd designs, writes, tests and implements bespoke automation software control systems, and builds control panels in its own workshop in Portsmouth. The company is a certified Siemens Solution Partner and specialises in highly validated systems, including GAMP 5 compliant solutions for pharmaceutical manufacturing. For defence and maritime programmes it states that it delivers Siemens PLC, SCADA and control panel systems, including safety-rated PLC design, that its staff hold BPSS clearance, and that it is currently engaged with MOD prime contractors on Siemens automation programmes. It states it has been delivering these systems since 2002.

## Products and solutions

- **Automation software** - PLC and HMI software design and programming using Siemens TIA Portal.
- **SCADA systems** - SCADA and DCS design and integration, including WinCC.
- **Control panels** - in-house panel build, with supply of PLC, HMI, SCADA and control hardware.
- **Failsafe and functional safety systems** - safety-rated PLC design to BS EN 61508.
- **Technical support** - ongoing support and site attendance worldwide.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Accreditations:** ISO 9001:2015; JOSCAR registered; Siemens Solution Partner.
- **Memberships:** South Central RDSC
