# GemaSecure

> Basingstoke developer of voice and data processing and encryption technologies running on FPGA-based hardware.

## What they do

GemaSecure Ltd describes itself as a specialist developer of advanced voice and data technologies that run on its own solid state, FPGA-based hardware devices. It states that its products provide fast voice and data processing, including search and retrieval, together with data encryption and protection against cyber attack. The company is based at Belvedere House, Basing View, Basingstoke.

## Products and solutions

- **Data processing** - high-speed voice and data processing, search and retrieval on FPGA hardware.
- **Data encryption and protection** - encryption and cyber protection built on its hardware devices.
