# EY

> UK member firm of the EY global professional services organisation, providing assurance, consulting, strategy and transactions, tax and law services.

## What they do

EY in the UK operates as Ernst & Young LLP, a member firm of the global EY organisation. It provides assurance, consulting, EY-Parthenon strategy and transactions, tax, law and managed services. It runs a government and public sector practice and an aerospace, defence and mobility industry group. EY reported UK fee income of 3.86 billion pounds for the financial year to 3 July 2026, and its London office is at 1 More London Place.

## Products and solutions

- **Assurance** - audit and assurance services.
- **Consulting** - business, technology and public sector consulting.
- **EY-Parthenon** - strategy and transactions advice.
- **Tax and law** - tax and legal services.
- **Managed services** - outsourced and managed services.
