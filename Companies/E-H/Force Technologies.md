# Force Technologies

> Wiltshire provider of obsolete semiconductor replacement, testing and long-term storage for aerospace, defence, rail and industrial programmes.

## What they do

Force Technologies Ltd provides solutions for obsolete semiconductors and states it has been trusted by defence primes and aerospace OEMs since 1986. It manufactures, assembles and packages form, fit and function replacement devices, including to MIL-STD-883, and offers custom semiconductor design. The company also provides upscreening, device characterisation, anti-counterfeit testing in line with AS6171, die extraction and long-term component storage. It operates from Collingbourne Ducis near Marlborough in Wiltshire.

## Products and solutions

- **Replacement semiconductors** - manufacture, assembly and packaging of form, fit and function replacements, including MIL-STD-883 and DESC form devices.
- **Custom semiconductor design** - design of replacement integrated circuits.
- **Testing and screening** - upscreening, device characterisation, AS6171 anti-counterfeit authentication and destructive physical analysis.
- **Die extraction** - recovery and reassembly of die from obsolete devices.
- **Long-term storage** - component storage under its Titan service.
- **Obsolete component supply** - supply of obsolete semiconductors including EEPROM, UVEPROM and SRAM.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Accreditations:** AS9100 Rev D; ISO 9001. Armed Forces Covenant signatory.
- **Memberships:** Make UK; Wessex Aerospace and Electronics Forum; International Institute of Obsolescence Management; South Central RDSC
