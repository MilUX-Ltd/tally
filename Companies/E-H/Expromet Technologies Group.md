# Expromet Technologies Group

> UK group of precision casting, machining and fabrication businesses supplying engineered metal components to aerospace, defence and industrial customers.

## What they do

Expromet Technologies Group Limited is a UK group of precision casting and machining businesses, headquartered at Mullacott Cross Trading Estate, Ilfracombe, Devon. It combines UK manufacturing with an international supply chain and serves aerospace, defence, energy, marine, medical, transportation and industrial customers. Its businesses include Investacast (investment castings, Devon), Haworth Castings (sand and gravity die castings, Hampshire), Metaltech Precision (CNC machining, Somerset), NC Precision and Tiverton Fabrications. The group's website lists no site in Wales.

## Products and solutions

- **Investment castings** - through Investacast, with a manufacturing base in Devon and sourcing partners in Asia and Europe.
- **Sand and gravity die castings** - through Haworth Castings in Hampshire.
- **Precision machining** - CNC turning and milling, including Hastelloy, Inconel, Monel and titanium, through Metaltech Precision.
- **Fabrication and welding** - vessels, vacuum chambers, cylinders and tubes through Tiverton Fabrications.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Accreditations:** AS9100; JOSCAR registration; EN 15085 certified welding for rail structures.
- **Memberships:** Defence Wales
