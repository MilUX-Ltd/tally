# Growth Lending

> London-based specialist SME lender offering growth, acquisition, healthcare and defence, security and resilience loans.

## What they do

Growth Lending describes itself as a specialist SME lender that has supported high-growth businesses since 2013, stating that it has backed more than 200 UK businesses and deployed more than £1bn of funding. Its Defence, Security and Resilience Loans provide non-dilutive funding to businesses in the defence supply chain, lending against purchase orders and Tier 1 or Ministry of Defence contracts. The company states these facilities are aimed at defence, security and national security SMEs in the UK, Europe or Canada with a confirmed purchase order and a track record with Tier 1 contractors or the Ministry of Defence. Its principal place of business is in London.

## Products and solutions

- **Growth loans** - funding from £2m to £10m to support business growth.
- **Acquisition finance** - capital to support mergers and acquisitions.
- **Healthcare finance** - funding for the private healthcare sector.
- **Defence, Security and Resilience Loans** - up to £15m of structured debt, combining a 24-month revolving facility for purchase order fulfilment with a 36-month term loan.
