# Hempel Special Metals

> UK company of the Swiss-based Hempel Special Metals group, distributing and processing titanium, nickel alloys and stainless steels for aerospace, defence, medical and industrial customers.

## What they do

Hempel Special Metals Ltd is the UK member of the Hempel Special Metals group, an owner-managed group headquartered in Switzerland that distributes and processes semi-finished high-performance metals, in particular stainless steels, nickel alloys and titanium. The UK company is registered in Dewsbury, West Yorkshire, and traded as Airco Metals Limited until 2007. The group lists aerospace and defence among the industries it serves, supplying materials to aerospace specifications. No Welsh site is published on its website.

## Products and solutions

- **Aerospace and defence materials** - titanium, nickel-based alloys (such as 625, 718 and Waspaloy), aerospace-grade stainless steels and other alloys supplied to AMS and other specifications.
- **Semi-finished products** - bar, sheet, plate, wire and other forms in stainless steels, nickel alloys and titanium.
- **Material processing** - cutting and processing services from the group's service centres.
- **ECONOXX** - the group's trading platform for surplus metal stock.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships
