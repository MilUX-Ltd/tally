# Hellios Information

> Operator of community-based supplier assurance schemes, including JOSCAR for the defence, aerospace and security sector.

## What they do

Hellios operates community-based platforms that collect, validate and share supplier data on behalf of buying organisations, simplifying compliance and reducing supply chain risk. Its JOSCAR (Joint Supply Chain Accreditation Register) scheme is the supplier assurance system used across the defence, aerospace and security sector. Suppliers submit a single registration to provide pre-qualified data to over 29 buying members, including the Ministry of Defence and major primes. It also runs schemes for financial services (FSQS) and energy (ESSCAR), plus sustainability and carbon reporting tools.

## Products and solutions

- **JOSCAR** - supplier assurance and pre-qualification for defence, aerospace and security; the shared supplier qualification register has over 5,000 published suppliers providing validated compliance and capability data through a single submission.
- **JOSCAR Zero** - carbon and sustainability reporting for the JOSCAR community.
- **Pooled audit and SME portal** - shared assurance services for suppliers.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Frameworks:** JOSCAR (operator)
- **Accreditations:** ISO 9001, ISO/IEC 27001, Cyber Essentials Plus.
- **Memberships:** ADS Group
