# HiPer Business Solutions

> East Midlands operational improvement consultancy for growing service businesses.

## What they do

HiPer Business Solutions is the trading name of High Performance Business Solutions Ltd. It works with leaders and teams of growing service businesses to identify what causes work to stall and to put practical operational changes in place. Its offers include an operational blueprint, systems and implementation projects, fractional operations and technical director support, and HiPer Command, a tool for tracking agreed actions, owners and progress. It states that it works remotely across the UK and runs on-site sessions in the East Midlands, including Newark, Nottingham and Lincoln.

## Products and solutions

- **Operational Blueprint** - a diagnostic and plan for operational improvement.
- **Systems and implementation** - implementation projects for systems and processes.
- **Fractional operations director** - part-time operations and technical director support.
- **HiPer Command** - a tool for keeping agreed actions, owners and progress in view.
