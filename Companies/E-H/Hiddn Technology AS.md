# Hiddn Technology AS

> Norwegian maker of hardware-based encryption for data at rest, aimed at defence, law enforcement and government users.

## What they do

Hiddn Technology designs and manufactures hardware-based encryption products that protect data at rest. The products protect information on personnel devices and sensor platforms if a device is lost or captured. Its site names defence personnel, government agencies and military alliances as users.

## Products and solutions

- **Hardware-encrypted storage** - Encrypted storage for personnel devices and sensor platforms.
- **Two-factor authentication** - Hardware-based two-factor authentication.
