# Green Ammo

> Develops reusable electronic training systems that convert service weapons for military training.

## What they do

Green Ammo converts existing service weapons into training systems using reusable electronic technology. The aim is to widen training opportunities while reducing ammunition and logistics demand.

## Products and solutions

- **E-Blanks** - Electronic blank training systems for service weapons
