# Evrmind

> Developer of hardware-enforced safety and assurance co-processors for AI and autonomous systems.

## What they do

Evrmind builds hardware-enforced safety and security for autonomous AI systems. Its co-processor sits on separate silicon between an autonomy stack and its actuators, verifies every proposed action against a formal rule set, physically blocks anything outside it, and produces a cryptographic record of each decision. Because enforcement runs in hardware rather than software, the company states it holds when the control link is lost or the software stack is compromised. The enforcement block is stated as validated at TRL 6 on FPGA silicon with 80 nanosecond measured interdiction, and the company reports 26 patent families filed at the UK Intellectual Property Office.

## Products and solutions

- **EvrChip** - Hardware safety co-processor providing gate-level interdiction of autonomous system actions, demonstrated on Xilinx Zynq 7020 FPGA.
- **EvrGuard** - Cloud API offering deterministic mathematical verification of AI actions as a service.
