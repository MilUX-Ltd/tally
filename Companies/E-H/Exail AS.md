# Exail AS

> Norwegian office of Exail, supporting its defence and civil customers in Scandinavia with navigation and maritime technologies.

## What they do

Exail AS is the Norwegian business office of the Exail group, opened in the Oslo area in 2023 to support growth in Scandinavia. The group's site states contracts in both civil and defence markets in the region. These include inertial navigation systems for Norway's U212 submarines and coast guard vessels, and for Finnish and Swedish naval programmes.

## Products and solutions

- **Inertial navigation systems** - Navigation systems supplied for submarines, corvettes and coast guard vessels.
