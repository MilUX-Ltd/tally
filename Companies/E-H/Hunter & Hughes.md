# Hunter & Hughes

> Plymouth consultancy providing senior leaders and specialists for government and defence programmes, capability and people development, and cyber training and exercises.

## What they do

Hunter & Hughes provides senior leadership and experienced specialists to government and defence organisations, helping them deliver complex programmes, develop capability and people, and design and lead cyber training and exercises. It works in four disciplines: project management, capability development, people development (training management, training needs analysis and competence assurance) and cyber training, covering the scoping, design, delivery and recovery of collective exercises. For defence clients it lists programme and capability delivery, portfolio management, training and workforce development, and collective exercising and operational readiness.
