# Experis UK

> ManpowerGroup's UK IT resourcing and solutions brand, with a defence and national security recruitment practice.

## What they do

Experis UK is an IT resourcing and solutions business and a brand of ManpowerGroup. It provides professional resourcing, IT consulting, managed services and project services, with practice areas in business transformation, cloud and platform, cyber security, digital workspace and enterprise applications. Its defence and national security practice recruits software, cloud, cyber, AI and data, systems integration and programme roles across air, land, sea, space, cyber and intelligence, and supports candidates through SC, DV and eDV vetting. The registered entity is Experis Limited.

## Products and solutions

- **Professional resourcing** - permanent and contract technology recruitment.
- **Defence and national security** - specialist recruitment for cleared and clearable roles.
- **IT consulting and managed services** - consulting, managed and project services.
- **Experis Academy** - technology skills programme.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Accreditations:** ISO 9001. Approved supplier to the UK Government through Government Procurement Service; Disability Confident.
- **Memberships:** techUK; Recruitment and Employment Confederation; Association of Professional Staffing Companies; South Central RDSC (listed as Experis ETS)
