# Foss Fiber Optics AS

> Supplier of fibre optic infrastructure and ruggedised optical products, with production in Norway and Slovakia.

## What they do

Foss Fiber Optics supplies fibre optic systems and structured cabling, with production in Norway and Slovakia. It develops custom systems for demanding environments in the defence and industrial sectors. Its ProBeam system was originally developed for military applications.

## Products and solutions

- **ProBeam** - Ruggedised fibre optic connection system
- **Fibre optic systems** - Racks, panels, cables, connectors and WDM equipment
- **Optical slip rings** - Industrial optical rotary connections
