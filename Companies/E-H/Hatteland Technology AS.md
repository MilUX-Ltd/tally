# Hatteland Technology AS

> Designs and makes industrial displays and computers, including a defence range for naval and defence use.

## What they do

Hatteland Technology designs and manufactures HATTELAND products and distributes industrial equipment. Its defence range includes mil-qualified monitors, panel computers and accessories for naval and defence markets.

## Products and solutions

- **HATTELAND Defence monitors** - Mil-qualified monitors
- **Panel computers** - Mil-qualified panel computers
