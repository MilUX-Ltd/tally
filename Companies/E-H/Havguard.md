# Havguard

> Defence technology company developing underwater awareness and communication systems.

## What they do

Havguard develops distributed intelligence systems built for defence. It lists naval forces, critical infrastructure and defence primes as customers, with a focus on real-time underwater awareness.

## Products and solutions

- **OrcaNet** - Distributed underwater system
- **OrcaPath** - Underwater system
- **OrcaField** - Underwater system
