# ECHA Microbiology

> Cardiff industrial microbiology company supplying test kits, laboratory analysis, consultancy and training for microbial contamination and corrosion in fuels, oils and fluid systems.

## What they do

Echa Microbiology Ltd, trading as ECHA Microbiology, helps organisations detect and manage microbial contamination and microbially influenced corrosion, mainly in fuel and oil systems in aviation, marine and ground transport, and in energy and industrial sectors. It began as an independent consultancy in 1983 and adopted its current name in 1988. The company manufactures field test kits, runs an industrial microbiology laboratory, and provides consultancy, site surveys and training from Willowbrook Technology Park, St Mellons, Cardiff.

## Products and solutions

- **MicrobMonitor2** - field test kit for estimating viable bacteria, yeast and mould in fuels and oils.
- **Test kits, accessories and sampling tools** - kits and equipment for on-site microbial testing.
- **Biocides** - products for treating microbial contamination.
- **Laboratory services** - routine analysis and investigative studies in its industrial microbiology laboratory.
- **Consultancy and site surveys** - audits, surveys and contamination investigations.
- **Training** - microbiology training courses for industry.
