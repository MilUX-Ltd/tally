# Hive Mind Medical

> Nottingham tactical medical consultancy giving practitioner-led clinical input to defence, medical technology, simulation and training work.

## What they do

Hive Mind Medical is a tactical medical consultancy built on frontline clinical practice in austere and hostile environments. It supports defence and security organisations and teams building medical devices, simulation hardware, clinical software and training, checking clinical workflows against real operational pressure.

## Products and solutions

- **Medical Consultancy** - clinical input for teams building medical devices, simulation hardware and clinical software.
- **Defence Support** - operational and austere environment medical expertise for defence and security organisations: capability advice, clinical workflow, and equipment and training requirements.
- **Training and Simulation** - clinical and trauma training for mixed-ability groups, with simulation matched to the situation it prepares people for.
- **Innovation** - problem solving and project delivery for new approaches to medical capability, technology and training.
