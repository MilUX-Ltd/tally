# Evocatus Consulting

> Dorset wargaming consultancy designing bespoke wargames and scenario-based exercises for decision-making, resilience and training.

## What they do

Evocatus Consulting designs and runs bespoke wargames and scenario-based exercises, from 90-minute tabletop games to multi-day simulations, to test plans, train teams and support decisions. The company grew out of large-scale exercise design for the British Army and works in support of defence and academia as well as public and commercial clients. It is based at Dorset Innovation Park and has traded since 2016.

## Products and solutions

- **Wargames and scenario exercises** - bespoke tabletop and multi-day exercises built around the client's own problem, ending in a structured debrief.
- **Decision support and red-teaming** - practising decisions under uncertainty and testing plans before they meet reality.
- **Resilience and continuity** - exercises that test plans and people for disruption, including cyber resilience.
- **Training solutions** - experiential learning programmes for decision-making and organisational learning.
- **Strategic foresight** - games that help organisations think through future outcomes.
