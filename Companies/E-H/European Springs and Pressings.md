# European Springs and Pressings

> Beckenham manufacturer of custom and stock springs, wireforms and pressings, part of the Lesjöfors group.

## What they do

European Springs and Pressings Ltd manufactures springs, wire forms, pressings and stampings at its facility in Beckenham, Kent, and states it has been operating for over 70 years. It serves sectors including automotive, telecommunications, aerospace and defence, hydraulics, nuclear, medical and lighting. For defence, the company states it makes springs and pressings for applications such as airborne smart decoys, electrical connectors, rifles and the Eurofighter. It is part of Lesjöfors, a spring manufacturing group owned by Beijer Components, a subsidiary of the Stockholm-listed Beijer Alma.

## Products and solutions

- **Made-to-order springs** - compression, tension, torsion, garter, wave, die, disc, constant torque and other spring types.
- **Custom wireforms** - bespoke wire forming.
- **Pressings and stampings** - precision stamping in steel, beryllium copper, copper, nickel and aluminium alloys.
- **Stock springs** - catalogue range of stock springs and pressings.
- **Engineering services** - CAD design, prototyping, tool making and tool transfer.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Accreditations:** ISO 9001 (BSI); ISO 14001.
- **Memberships:** South Central RDSC; Institute of Spring Technology; Made in Britain
