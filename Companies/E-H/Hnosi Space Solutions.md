# Hnosi Space Solutions

> Advisory and specialist talent solutions company for the space, aerospace and defence sectors.

## What they do

Hnosi Space Solutions Ltd is a company that states it combines strategic advisory with specialist talent solutions for space, aerospace and defence organisations. It states it embeds with client teams to provide advisory support, talent mapping and low-volume, consultative recruitment. The company states it supports space programmes (spacecraft, satellite communications and payload systems), aircraft, rotorcraft and UAV programmes, and defence programmes in secure, clearance-aligned environments. It states it operates across the UK, Europe, North America, Asia-Pacific, the Middle East and Australia.

## Products and solutions

- **Strategic advisory** - insight and scenario planning to align workforce strategies with emerging technologies and mission demands.
- **Talent intelligence and mapping** - market analysis, talent mapping and benchmarking.
- **Targeted talent placement** - introductions to niche experts and senior candidates.
