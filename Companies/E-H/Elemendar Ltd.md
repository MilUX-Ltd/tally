# Elemendar Ltd

> AI tools that turn raw cyber threat intelligence into structured, actionable output for analysts.

## What they do

Elemendar builds AI-driven tools for cyber threat intelligence (CTI) analysis and threat-based risk management, intended to replace slower manual analyst workflows. The company was founded in 2017 at the UK's first GCHQ/NCSC Cyber Accelerator and is based in London. It has since taken part in further accelerator programmes including NVIDIA Inception, an IBM accelerator, and Google for Startups.

## Products and solutions

- **READ** - automated threat analysis: an AI tool that processes raw cyber threat intelligence into structured, actionable output.
- **Compass** - threat-based risk management: maps threats to business risk, identifies control gaps, and helps prioritise security investment and compliance alignment.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Programmes:** GCHQ/NCSC Cyber Accelerator (2017, first cohort); NVIDIA Inception; IBM accelerator; Google for Startups.
- **Awards:** Innovate UK Grant Award (2023); finalist, EIT Digital Challenge (2022); winner, I2C/OMV Challenge; finalist, NTT Data Open Innovation Contest.
