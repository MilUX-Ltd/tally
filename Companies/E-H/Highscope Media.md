# Highscope Media

> Hampshire aerial photography and video company.

## What they do

Highscope Media Limited is a Hampshire media company providing aerial photography and aerial video, and combined packages of edited photography and video. It states it holds operational authorisation from the UK Civil Aviation Authority and the US Federal Aviation Administration and is fully insured. The company gives its location as Southampton.

## Products and solutions

- **Aerial photography** - stills captured on GPS-enabled cameras.
- **Aerial video** - aerial motion footage.
- **Complete package** - professionally edited aerial photography and video.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Accreditations:** CAA (UK) and FAA (USA) operational authorisation.
- **Memberships:** South Central RDSC
