# Force Effect

> Hampshire RF engineering company supplying sensor and effector technology, engineering services and products to defence and national security customers.

## What they do

Force Effect Limited states that it works to deliver advanced RF sensor and effector technology to partners in defence and national security. The company describes three services: acting as a UK partner for a curated set of US and European OEMs supplying RF, CEMA and ISR sub-systems; providing project, systems engineering and software expertise to governments, defence primes and investors; and designing operational technology for EW, CEMA and ISR requirements on a contract research and development basis. It also develops its own products for direction finding, signal classification, frequency extension and GNSS-denied training. Its contact page gives its headquarters at Sherfield English near Romsey, Hampshire.

## Products and solutions

- **Tech bridge** - UK partner for overseas RF, CEMA and ISR technology, including acquisition support, export control navigation, local integration, training and through-life support.
- **Deep expertise** - project and engineering support for adoption of RF sensor and effector technology.
- **Special projects** - contract R&D for EW, CEMA and ISR challenges.
- **AXION** - a portable radio direction finding system for spectrum monitoring, force protection, counter-UAS and tactical SIGINT (preliminary design).
- **RAO** - a four-channel, phase-coherent 30 to 6000 MHz direction finding antenna (preliminary design).
- **HEISENBERG** - a signal detection and modulation classification plugin for 3dB Labs SCEPTRE.
- **MESON** - an up-converter extending 6 GHz transceivers into the 6 to 8 GHz band.
- **PARALLAX** - GNSS jamming and spoofing training that injects interference inline without radiating.
