# HiDE Industry

> Norwegian developer of modular unmanned aircraft for defence and civil roles, built and flight tested at Eggemoen.

## What they do

HiDE Industry designs and builds autonomous unmanned aircraft in Norway for European and NATO defence and for dual use. Its modular airframe carries mission-configurable sensors and payloads for roles from surveillance and mapping to test and training. The aircraft are flight tested year round in Northern Norway on an open autonomy stack developed alongside Auterion.

## Products and solutions

- **Modular unmanned aircraft** - Configurable airframe with mission-specific sensors, payloads and communications.
