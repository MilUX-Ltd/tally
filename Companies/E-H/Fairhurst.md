# Fairhurst

> UK multidisciplinary engineering consultancy providing civil, structural, transport, geo-environmental, water and planning services.

## What they do

Fairhurst, operating as Fairhurst Group LLP, describes itself as a UK multidisciplinary engineering consultancy that has provided services for over 120 years. It has evolved from a civil and structural design practice to cover geo-environmental, traffic and transportation, rail, water, planning, landscape, health and safety and mining legacy services, and has an energy transmission team. It operates offices across Scotland and England, including Glasgow, Edinburgh, Aberdeen, Dundee, Newcastle, Leeds, London, Bristol, Taunton and Watford.

## Products and solutions

- **Civil and structural engineering** - design for buildings and infrastructure.
- **Roads and bridges** - highway and bridge engineering.
- **Geotechnical and environmental** - ground investigation and geo-environmental services.
- **Traffic and transport planning** - transportation and planning services.
- **Water services** - water engineering and environment.
- **Landscape and urban design** - landscape and planning services.
- **Energy** - engineering for electricity transmission, distribution and generation.
- **Mining legacy** - mining legacy specialists through JWH Ross.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Accreditations:** ISO 9001:2015 and ISO 14001:2015 quality and environmental management system; ISO 45001:2018 occupational health and safety management system.
- **Memberships:** South Central RDSC
