# Ex-Eltronics (UK)

> Alton-based UK arm of the US Ex-El Group, supplying interconnect, componentry and electro-mechanical products to aerospace and defence customers.

## What they do

Ex-Eltronics (UK) Limited is the UK division of Ex-El Group, whose corporate headquarters is in Plainview, New York, alongside its sister maintenance, repair and operations provider Midway Industrial Electronics. The group states it provides key technologies and procurement services to the aerospace and defence industries, including global sourcing, procurement, kitting and supply chain management for system and subsystem OEMs. Its product range covers connectors and cable assemblies, components and electro-mechanical sub-systems, with value-added services and technical support. The UK business operates from Omega Park in Alton, Hampshire.

## Products and solutions

- **Interconnect products** - filtered connectors, MIL-spec circular, avionic rack and panel, marine and naval, data-bus and soldier-worn connectors, backshells and conduit systems.
- **Componentry** - transient voltage suppressors, transformers, inductors and obsolete semiconductor solutions.
- **Electro-mechanical products** - sub-systems and control panels, illuminated switches and panels, power converters, ground test equipment and pilot and tactical helmet communications.
- **Services** - global procurement, technical support and value-added services.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships
