# Electus Recruitment Solutions

> Specialist recruiter of permanent and contract staff, including security-cleared professionals, for defence, aerospace, infrastructure and energy.

## What they do

Electus Recruitment Solutions provides contract and permanent recruitment to the defence, government, aerospace, infrastructure, and power and energy sectors. The company states that it has more than two decades of experience supplying security-cleared talent into UK defence, security and critical national infrastructure, with networks of candidates holding DV, SC, CTC and NATO-level clearances. Its consultants cover domains including land, maritime, air, weapons, nuclear, ISTAR, electronics, communications, avionics and cyber security. It reports recruitment support for more than 300 companies.

## Products and solutions

- **Permanent recruitment** - specialist hiring across engineering, technical and commercial disciplines.
- **Contract recruitment** - contractor supply and contractor services.
- **Security-cleared recruitment** - SC and DV cleared candidates for sensitive programmes.
- **Specialisms** - autonomous systems and UAS, systems engineering, safety and risk, cyber security and AI, software engineering, supportability engineering and project services.
- **Executive search** - C-suite searches.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Accreditations:** UKAS-accredited ISO 9001 certification held since 2005.
- **Memberships:** APSCo; South Central RDSC
