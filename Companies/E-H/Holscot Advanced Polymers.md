# Holscot Advanced Polymers

> Grantham manufacturer of fluoropolymer tubing, film, heat shrink sleeving and formed components.

## What they do

Holscot Advanced Polymers Ltd describes itself as a fluoropolymer specialist established in 1970, manufacturing directly from FEP, PFA and ETFE granules. Its head office and main manufacturing site is in Grantham, Lincolnshire, with a Northern Division in Leven, Fife, and a European base in the Netherlands. It produces extruded tubing up to 400mm diameter, film and sheet, heat shrink sleeving, and welded and vacuum-formed components such as bladders, diaphragms and containment products. It lists aerospace, chemical, lighting, architectural, medical and green technology among its markets, and space propellant containment among its products.

## Products and solutions

- **Fluoropolymer extrusions** - FEP, PFA and ETFE tubing from 4mm to 400mm diameter, and hose liners.
- **Film and sheet** - FEP, PFA and ETFE film, precision cut sheet and self-adhesive tape.
- **Heat shrink sleeving** - FEP and PFA sleeving, roll covers and aero-engine fan case sleeves.
- **Containment products** - bladders, diaphragms, bags for hazardous materials and space propellant containment.
- **Flange shields** - clear protective shields against chemical spillage.
- **ETFE panels** - architectural ETFE panels.
