# FundingRound

> Newark independent business and commercial finance broker, authorised and regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority.

## What they do

Funding Round is the trading name of FundingRound Ltd, an independent business and commercial finance broker that states it works with an unrestricted number of lenders and does not lend its own funds. It arranges asset finance, commercial loans and invoice finance, property finance including commercial mortgages, bridging and development finance, and finance for health professionals buying practices. It also offers guidance on grants and finance for SMEs and start-ups. The company states it is authorised and regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority and is based at the Navigation Business Centre in Newark.

## Products and solutions

- **Asset finance** - funding for business asset purchases.
- **Commercial finance** - commercial loans, cash advances and invoice financing.
- **Property finance** - commercial mortgages, bridging loans and development finance.
- **Business support** - guidance on grants and finance for SMEs and start-ups.
- **Finance for health professionals** - funding to buy healthcare, dental, veterinary or care home practices.
