# G W Martin

> Family-run Hampshire precision engineering company making CNC-machined components and assemblies for defence, aerospace, marine and other sectors.

## What they do

G W Martin describes itself as a family-run UK specialist in precision engineering and manufacturing, operating from a 25,000 sq ft facility in Eastleigh, Hampshire, and machining since 1959. The company states it produces over a million components a year and works with defence companies on medium to high-volume orders delivered on scheduled monthly call-offs. It also serves aerospace, marine, medical, commercial and automotive customers. The company states that around 30 per cent of its energy comes from its own solar installation and that it is working towards AS9100 accreditation.

## Products and solutions

- **CNC machining** - CNC turning and milling, including multi-axis machining.
- **Design for manufacture** - design support for manufacturability.
- **Assembly** - assembled parts and sub-assemblies.
- **Stockholding** - stock management and scheduled call-off supply.
- **Sub-contract treatments** - managed finishing and treatment processes.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Accreditations:** ISO 9001:2015; ISO 14001:2015.
- **Memberships:** Make UK; Make UK Defence; South Central RDSC
