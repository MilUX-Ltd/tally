# Highforce Advisory

> Tamworth defence and technology advisory firm, formerly Hardfork Ltd, covering procurement, bids and UAS and counter-UAS.

## What they do

Highforce Advisory Limited, registered as Hardfork Ltd until March 2026, is a defence and technology advisory firm providing counsel to defence primes, technology suppliers and investors across UK and allied markets. It states it advises on MOD and allied procurement frameworks, UAS and counter-UAS capability and procurement, and defence commercial structuring. It also runs DroneMatch, described as a brokerage for uncrewed air system components and solutions.

## Products and solutions

- **Procurement and bid support** - defence procurement and bid support.
- **Business development** - go-to-market strategy for defence markets.
- **Commercial and contract advisory** - commercial structuring and contract advice.
- **UAS and counter-UAS advisory** - capability and procurement advice.
- **Market intelligence** - opportunity tracking.
- **Due diligence** - for investors and acquirers.
- **DroneMatch** - brokerage for uncrewed air system components and solutions.
