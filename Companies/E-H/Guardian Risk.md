# Guardian Risk

> Lincoln-registered consultancy providing business risk assessment, corporate investigations and digital forensics for UK SME and mid-market organisations.

## What they do

Guardian Risk Ltd describes itself as a specialist consultancy protecting organisations from complex business risks, serving UK SME and mid-market organisations and supporting directors, trustees and legal counsel. The company states it carries out corporate investigations, incident response, digital forensics and open-source intelligence work, with evidence handled to withstand legal and regulatory scrutiny. It states that it does not offer generic cyber security products and focuses on investigation and compliance assurance.

## Products and solutions

- **Business risk assessment** - operational vulnerability assessment and resilience planning.
- **Corporate investigations** - investigations into fraud, misconduct and operational anomalies, and fact-finding for employee disputes.
- **Incident response** - containment and investigation of security or data incidents.
- **Digital forensics** - acquisition and analysis of data from mobile devices and computers.
- **Open-source intelligence** - OSINT gathering to identify risks or corroborate findings.
- **Data protection guidance** - support on data protection obligations and regulatory reporting.
