# Faldingworth Defence

> Lincolnshire site providing licensed explosives and ammunition storage, testing, an indoor range and related services for military and police customers.

## What they do

Faldingworth Defence Ltd states that it provides storage and testing services for explosives and ammunition from a 750-acre facility at Faldingworth in Lincolnshire, which it describes as a top tier COMAH site with HSE-licensed hazardous storage. The company states that it operates a 225 metre indoor shooting range and serves police and Ministry of Defence customers. Its published services include explosive and weapon system testing, product drop testing, an external range, military and police training, ammunition disposal by incineration, transport and data centre space.

## Products and solutions

- **Licensed storage** - HSE-licensed storage for explosives, ammunition and other hazardous goods, with 24-hour security.
- **Explosive testing** - ammunition and weapon system testing, underground mine tests and product drop testing.
- **Indoor 225 metre range** - indoor range facility.
- **Training** - military and police training programmes.
- **Disposal** - ammunition incineration.
- **Transport** - transport services.
