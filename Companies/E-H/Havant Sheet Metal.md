# Havant Sheet Metal

> Havant precision sheet metal fabrication company working in stainless steel, mild steel and aluminium for defence and other sectors.

## What they do

Havant Sheet Metal is a precision metal fabrication company in Havant, Hampshire, which states it has specialised in sheet metal components and fabrications since 1939. Its capabilities include CNC punching, fibre laser cutting, folding, welding, tubular fabrication, machining, press tooling and finishing, with powder coating, wet spray painting and stove enamelling. It lists defence, aerospace, marine, electronics and telecommunications among its sectors, and reports that it supplies fabricated parts used on military vehicles.

## Products and solutions

- **Sheet metal fabrication** - CNC punching, fibre laser cutting, folding, sawing and welding, including stud welding.
- **Machining and tooling** - machining, press tooling, jigs and fixtures.
- **Finishing and coating** - graining, polishing, powder coating, wet spray and stove enamelling.
- **Design and planning** - design support and subcontract services.
