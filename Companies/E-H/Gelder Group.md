# Gelder Group

> Lincolnshire construction group building, maintaining, repairing and renovating commercial and residential buildings.

## What they do

Gelder Group is a construction company established in 1988 and based at Sturton by Stow near Lincoln. It operates commercial building, facilities repair and maintenance, insurance repair and renovation, and residential house building (Gelder Living) divisions. It lists education, health, retail, insurance, leisure, residential and commercial building among its sectors, and states a commitment to training and apprenticeships.

## Products and solutions

- **Commercial** - building, extending and maintaining commercial buildings.
- **Facilities, repair and maintenance** - long-term service partnerships.
- **Insurance repair and renovation** - repairs after subsidence, water, fire, flood, storm and malicious damage.
- **Gelder Living** - residential house building.
