# ENGNDATA

> Gosport engineering consultancy combining mechanical engineering with data analytics, machine learning and custom software for industrial, aerospace and automotive businesses.

## What they do

Engineering and Data Limited, trading as ENGNDATA, provides engineering development and data services to industrial, aerospace and automotive businesses. The company states that it offers design and development, test and qualification plans, value analysis and value engineering, and programme management, alongside machine learning, analytics dashboards and custom software. It states a focus on local LLM deployment and secure cloud architectures to keep client designs private. It offers project consultancy, programme support during peak workloads and strategic advisory.

## Products and solutions

- **Engineering development** - design and development, test and qualification plans, innovative materials and VA/VE plans.
- **Programme management** - programme support for engineering projects.
- **Machine learning** - predictive modelling pipelines.
- **Advanced analytics** - interactive client data dashboards.
- **Custom software** - bespoke software and AI roadmap support.
