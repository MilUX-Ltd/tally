# GM Defense UK

> UK entity of GM Defense, the defence subsidiary of General Motors, leading its engagement with the Ministry of Defence on light military vehicles.

## What they do

GM Defense UK was launched in January 2026 by GM Defense, the military mobility business of General Motors, to create a dedicated UK presence. GM Defense states that the UK entity operates from GM's sites in Leamington Spa and Silverstone, leads all UK Ministry of Defence engagement and anchors GM's wider NATO and European defence activity. GM Defense is the vehicle manufacturer in Team LionStrike with NP Aerospace and BAE Systems, which is competing for the British Army's Light Mobility Vehicle programme with vehicles based on commercial Chevrolet and GM Defense platforms.

## Products and solutions

- **Tactical Mobility Vehicle** - a version of the GM Defense ISV-U offered for the Light Mobility Vehicle requirement.
- **General Purpose Vehicle** - an adapted Chevrolet Silverado light-duty truck integrated in the UK.
- **Battlefield Ambulance** - a variant on the Chevrolet Silverado heavy-duty platform, designed and integrated with NP Aerospace.
- **General Support Utility Platform** - a utility vehicle shown at DSEI.
- **Through-life support** - UK support backed by GM's global parts distribution network.
