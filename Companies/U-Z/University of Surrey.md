# University of Surrey

> Guildford university with defence-relevant research in space, cyber security, telecommunications and AI, and a founding member of the Defence Universities Alliance.

## What they do

The University of Surrey is a university based in Guildford. It names space, cyber security, telecommunications and artificial intelligence among its strengths relevant to defence. Its Surrey Centre for Cyber Security has been recognised by the National Cyber Security Centre as an Academic Centre of Excellence in Cyber Security Research since 2014, and leads the university's gold-level Academic Centre of Excellence in Cyber Security Education. In July 2026 the university was named one of 35 founding members of the Ministry of Defence-led Defence Universities Alliance.

## Products and solutions

- **Surrey Centre for Cyber Security** - research in cryptography, distributed systems, formal verification, networked systems, and security and privacy.
- **Space research** - space research through the university, named among its defence-relevant strengths.
- **Telecommunications research** - next-generation mobile communications research.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Accreditations:** NCSC Academic Centre of Excellence in Cyber Security Research; NCSC Academic Centre of Excellence in Cyber Security Education (gold).
- **Memberships:** Defence Universities Alliance; South Central RDSC
