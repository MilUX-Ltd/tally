# Wardyn Technologies

> British threat detection company fusing multi-sensor data with AI for drone detection and insider-threat monitoring.

## What they do

Wardyn Technologies describes itself as a threat detection and intelligence company focused on drones and insider threats, serving defence, critical infrastructure, security and commercial customers. Its products share a fusion engine that combines data from many sensors and systems into one picture and uses AI to identify what matters, running locally without cloud dependency. The company states it supports the NATO and UK MOD SAPIENT and ASTERIX standards and designs and assembles its systems in Great Britain. It lists naval bases and defence sites among its operating environments.

## Products and solutions

- **SENTRair C2** - multi-sensor fusion command and control for drone detection, combining radar, RF, Remote ID, ADS-B, optical, acoustic and thermal sensors.
- **Forseti** - guard force intelligence linking access control, cameras and building systems to flag unusual patterns.
- **Centrifuse** - the fusion engine underlying both products.
- **Hired-in drone detection** - an off-grid detection tower available on hire.
