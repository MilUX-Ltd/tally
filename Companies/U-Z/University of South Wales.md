# University of South Wales

> University with campuses in Pontypridd, Cardiff and Newport, home of the National Cyber Security Academy and active in defence and security partnerships in South Wales.

## What they do

The University of South Wales (USW) was formed in 2013 and has campuses at Pontypridd (Treforest), Cardiff and Newport. Its National Cyber Security Academy, launched in 2016 with the Welsh Government at the Newport City Campus, teaches cyber security through projects set by industry partners, and the university is recognised by the National Cyber Security Centre as an Academic Centre of Excellence in Cyber Security Education at Gold level. USW was the first UK higher education institution to sign an Armed Forces Covenant of its kind in 2013, joined the Wales Defence and Security Cluster in June 2026, and is a founding partner in the Marshall Land Research Alliance on defence technology and advanced manufacturing.

## Products and solutions

- **National Cyber Security Academy** - applied cyber security education delivered with industry partners at the Newport City Campus.
- **Cyber security degrees** - including BSc (Hons) Applied Cyber Security and MSc Cyber Security Risk and Resilience.
- **Computing, cyber security, mathematics and informatics research** - university research and innovation group.
- **Criminology, policing and security research** - including the International Centre for Policing and Security.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Accreditations:** NCSC Academic Centre of Excellence in Cyber Security Education (Gold).
- **Memberships:** Defence Wales; Wales Defence and Security Cluster; Fortinet Training Institute Academic Partner Programme; Armed Forces Covenant signatory.
