# UK Docks Marine Services

> Family-owned ship repair group with dry docks and repair berths in the North East and southern England, supporting commercial and defence fleets.

## What they do

UK Docks Marine Services is a family-owned ship repair business, founded in 1992, with its headquarters on Tyneside. It states it offers dry docks, afloat repair berths and specialist ship repair facilities at locations including Tyneside, Teesside and Gosport, providing vessel maintenance, repair and refit, in-service support, upgrades, conversions and ship life extension programmes. The company lists the Ministry of Defence and the Royal Navy among its clients and offers defence, commercial and sovereign security fleet support. Its Teesside yard operates four dry docks taking vessels up to 175 metres, and its Gosport facility at Endeavour Quay serves vessels up to 40 metres.

## Products and solutions

- **Defence fleet support** - repair, refit and in-service support for defence and sovereign security vessels.
- **Commercial fleet support** - dry docking, afloat repairs and mobilisations for commercial shipping.
- **Dry docks and repair berths** - four dry docks and dedicated repair berths on the River Tees.
- **Boatyard services** - refit, repair and new build for steel, aluminium and GRP vessels on the Tyne and at Gosport.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Accreditations:** ISO 9001, ISO 14001, ISO 45001 and ISO 27001 (UK Docks North); ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and ISO 27001 (UK Docks South).
- **Memberships:** South Central RDSC
