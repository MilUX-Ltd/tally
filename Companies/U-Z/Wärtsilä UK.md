# Wärtsilä UK

> UK arm of Finnish marine and energy technology group Wärtsilä, providing propulsion, shaft line, electrical and automation products and services.

## What they do

Wärtsilä in the UK is part of Wärtsilä Corporation, headquartered in Helsinki, which supplies technologies and lifecycle solutions for the marine and energy markets. The company states that its UK business spans 13 locations with over 700 professionals and focuses on products, solutions and services for shipping and power generation. UK sites published on its website include a head office in Fareham, Wärtsilä Defence Solutions Ltd in Havant, propulsion services in Glasgow, electrical and automation services in Montrose, and engine services in Aberdeen. Its UK services include propulsion services, propeller repairs, seals and bearings, and electrical and automation support, and the group offers shaft line solutions.

## Products and solutions

- **Propulsion services** - fitting shop, machine shop, propeller repairs, spin test facility and exchange thrusters.
- **Shaft line solutions** - seals, bearings and shaft line products and services.
- **Electrical and automation services** - lifecycle support for marine electrical systems.
- **Engine services** - marine engine service support.
