# University of Lincoln

> University in Lincoln with defence and security research partnerships, armed forces education programmes and defence-focused artificial intelligence research.

## What they do

The University of Lincoln states that, as an anchor institution in a region with a strong military heritage, it engages with the defence and security community through collaborative research, skills and innovation. It works with supply chain organisations including SRC UK, BAE Systems and Leonardo, and is part of the Capita-led consortium delivering training and education to the Royal Navy under Project Selborne. Its Maritime Studies Centre is part of Britannia Royal Naval College, and Lincoln Business School offers work-based distance learning programmes for serving, reservist and veteran members of the armed forces. The university holds the Armed Forces Covenant Employer Recognition Scheme Gold Award and is a member of the Ministry of Defence-led Defence Universities Alliance.

## Products and solutions

- **Defence and Security Research Network** - engagement with the armed forces and industry partners on shared problems.
- **Centre for Defence and Security Artificial Intelligence** - a centre established with QinetiQ.
- **Armed forces education** - work-based distance learning programmes for serving, reservist and veteran personnel.
- **Maritime Studies Centre** - based at Britannia Royal Naval College.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships
