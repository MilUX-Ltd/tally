# Wall Colmonoy

> Pontardawe materials engineering company making surfacing and brazing alloys, metal powders, precision castings and machined components for aerospace, defence, energy and industrial markets.

## What they do

Wall Colmonoy Limited is the UK company of the Wall Colmonoy group, a privately held materials engineering business headquartered in Michigan, USA. The UK operation, on the Alloy Industrial Estate in Pontardawe near Swansea, makes nickel and cobalt surfacing and brazing alloys, atomised metal powders for surfacing, brazing and additive manufacturing, precision castings and machined components. It serves aerospace, defence, automotive, energy, glass, oil and gas, steel and nuclear customers, and states that about 90 per cent of its output is exported to more than 50 countries. The company has operated at Pontardawe since 1969 and employs about 200 people there.

## Products and solutions

- **Surfacing alloys** - Colmonoy nickel-based and Wallex cobalt-based hardfacing alloys.
- **Brazing filler metals** - Nicrobraz, Niferobraz and CuBraz alloys.
- **Metal powders** - water and gas atomised powders, including powders for laser cladding and additive manufacturing.
- **Precision castings** - investment, centrifugal, sand and vacuum castings, including HIP.
- **Precision machining** - CNC milling, turning, grinding, EDM, honing and lapping.
- **Thermal processing and coatings** - vacuum brazing, heat treatment and HVOF thermal spray coatings.
