# University of Chichester

> West Sussex university with campuses in Chichester and Bognor Regis, including an engineering and digital technology park.

## What they do

The University of Chichester is a university based at Bishop Otter Campus in Chichester, with a second campus at Bognor Regis. Its Tech Park at the Bognor Regis campus is the purpose-built home of its Engineering and Creative Industries departments, with engineering workshops, electronics and mechanics laboratories, 3D printing and computer-aided design teaching. The university lists engineering and computing among its subjects and offers apprenticeships and executive short courses alongside undergraduate and postgraduate study.

## Products and solutions

- **Engineering education** - engineering courses taught at the Tech Park.
- **Tech Park facilities** - engineering workshops, electronics and mechanics laboratories, ideas labs and co-working spaces.
- **Business collaboration** - access to expertise, partnerships and executive short courses for employers.
