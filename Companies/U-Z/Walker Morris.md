# Walker Morris

> Leeds law firm with a defence sector practice covering procurement, transactions, export controls and national security.

## What they do

Walker Morris LLP is a law firm headquartered in Leeds, which states it has specialists in more than 40 areas of law. Its defence sector practice advises on complex procurement, major programmes and long-term support contracts, supply chains, export controls, sanctions and national security requirements, and on M&A, investment and foreign investment approvals in defence. The firm states it advised on acquisitions involving defence and security technology companies and on a National Security and Investment Act notification for a UK defence communications supplier.

## Products and solutions

- **Procurement and commercial** - defence procurement, major programmes, long-term support contracts and supply chains.
- **Regulatory** - export controls, sanctions and national security requirements.
- **Corporate and transactions** - M&A, investment, foreign investment approvals and technology transfer.
- **Technology and data** - IP, data rights, AI governance and cyber incident response.
- **Employment and estates** - security-cleared workforces, TUPE and defence estate projects.
