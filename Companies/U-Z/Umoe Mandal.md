# Umoe Mandal

> Norwegian builder of composite naval vessels and defence composite structures, including Skjold class hulls and mine countermeasure vessels.

## What they do

Umoe Mandal develops and delivers vessels, composite structures and components for naval, industrial and commercial applications. Its products include Type 26 mast structures, the Skjold class, gun cupolas and weapon system structures, mine countermeasure vessels and Wave Craft hull technology. It covers design, production, testing and lifecycle support.

## Products and solutions

- **Type 26 mast structures** - Composite mast structures for naval ships
- **Skjold class** - High-speed composite naval vessels
- **Gun cupolas and weapon system structures** - Composite structures for weapon systems
- **Mine Countermeasure Vessels** - Composite mine countermeasure ships
- **Wave Craft hull technology** - Hull technology for fast craft
