# Westcontrol

> Electronics development and manufacturing supplier, with clients including defence.

## What they do

Westcontrol develops, manufactures and assembles electronics, from mass-produced units to products for extreme conditions. Its site lists clients in oil and energy, defence, maritime, health and agriculture, and states EN 9100 certification for aviation and defence work. Services include prototyping, machining, cable assembly and logistics.

## Products and solutions

- **Electronic manufacturing** - Turnkey electronics production
- **Machining** - In-house machining
- **Cable** - Cable assembly
