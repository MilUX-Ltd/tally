# UGV Defence

> Develops and delivers hybrid-electric autonomous tracked vehicle platforms for defence forces.

## What they do

UGV Defence develops hybrid-electric autonomous tracked platforms for defence forces, carrying payload, power and remote operations. The FLEX range covers payloads of 6, 12 and 16 tonnes, with parallel and serial hybrid drivetrains. The site describes logistics, weapon platform and mobile power uses and a unified control interface for route and vehicle management.

## Products and solutions

- **FLEX L12-L** - Parallel hybrid tracked platform, up to 12 tonne payload
- **FLEX L12-P** - Serial hybrid platform with larger battery and onboard power
- **FLEX L6 and L16** - Lighter and heavier platform sizes, listed as later releases
