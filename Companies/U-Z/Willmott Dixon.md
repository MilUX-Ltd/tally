# Willmott Dixon

> Privately owned UK construction, fit-out and development group headquartered in Letchworth Garden City.

## What they do

Willmott Dixon describes itself as a privately owned contracting and interior fit-out group founded in 1852, with its head office in Letchworth Garden City, Hertfordshire. Its activities include construction of public and commercial projects, residential construction, interior fit-out, development and regeneration through Willmott Dixon Developments, and property services delivered by Fortem. The company states a goal of becoming a net zero carbon company by 2030. Willmott Dixon Construction Limited is its main contracting company on the Companies House register.

## Products and solutions

- **Construction** - public and commercial building projects.
- **Residential construction** - housing across tenure types.
- **Interior fit-out** - bespoke interiors.
- **Development** - development and regeneration through Willmott Dixon Developments.
- **Property services** - property services delivered by Fortem.
