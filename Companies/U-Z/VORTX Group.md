# VORTX Group

> Southampton cyber risk and capability advisory business, including Defence Cyber Certification readiness support for defence suppliers.

## What they do

VORTX Group Ltd describes itself as a cyber risk and capability partner that helps organisations gain visibility of cyber exposure, operational pressure and capability gaps. Its services include a cyber and capability review, supplier assurance programmes, incident and continuity planning, capability statements and certification readiness. For defence suppliers it offers readiness support for Cyber Essentials, Cyber Essentials Plus and Defence Cyber Certification (DCC), and states that it is not itself a DCC certification body. The company was incorporated in May 2026 and its registered office is in Southampton.

## Products and solutions

- **Cyber and capability review** - a structured review of cyber risk, ownership and evidence, with a 30/60/90-day improvement roadmap.
- **Certification readiness** - readiness support for Cyber Essentials, Cyber Essentials Plus and Defence Cyber Certification.
- **Supplier assurance** - supply chain mapping and recurring supplier assurance reviews.
- **Incident and continuity planning** - incident plans, continuity notes and walk-through exercises.
- **Capability statements** - a guided builder for customer-ready capability statements.
