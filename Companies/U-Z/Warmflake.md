# Warmflake

> Develops medical technology to prevent pre-hospital hypothermia in demanding conditions, aimed at military casualty care.

## What they do

Warmflake develops active warming solutions that keep patients warm from the point of injury through evacuation until treatment. The site frames this around maintaining the body temperature of bleeding soldiers. The main product is the Warmflake Patient Warmer.

## Products and solutions

- **Warmflake Patient Warmer** - Active patient warming system
