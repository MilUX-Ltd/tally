# Water Linked AS

> Develops underwater sensors for vehicles and divers, including DVL, 3D sonar, underwater GPS and acoustic modems, with a defence industry offer.

## What they do

Water Linked, founded in 2013, creates underwater navigation and communication sensors. Its products serve underwater vehicles and divers across industries that include defence, public authorities and search and rescue.

## Products and solutions

- **DVL** - Doppler velocity logs
- **Sonar 3D-15** - 3D sonar
- **UGPS G2** - Underwater GPS
- **Modem M16** - Acoustic modem
