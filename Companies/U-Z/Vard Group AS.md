# Vard Group AS

> Shipbuilder designing and building specialised vessels, including naval and coast guard vessels, with repair and conversion services.

## What they do

Vard designs and builds ships for energy, seafood, security and cruise markets, and offers ship repair and conversion. Its security segment covers naval and coast guard vessels, and it runs a dedicated governmental defence programme. It also supplies HVAC-R, interiors, handling systems, piping and power and automation systems.

## Products and solutions

- **Naval and coast guard vessels** - Security segment newbuilds
- **Ship design** - Vessel design services
- **Ship repair and conversion** - Repair and conversion yard services
- **Vard Electro** - Power and automation systems
