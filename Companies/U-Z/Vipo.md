# Vipo

> Polymer materials company supplying corrosion, fire protection and insulation solutions, including to navy, submarine and aircraft programmes.

## What they do

Vipo develops and manufactures elastomer-based corrosion, fire protection and thermal insulation solutions. Its defence page says it supplies navy programmes, submarines, aircraft systems, missile systems and land-based transport, and is a certified supplier to national defence programmes in several NATO countries.

## Products and solutions

- **Vikotherm Insulation and Coating** - Thermal insulation and coating
- **Firestop Passive Fire Protection** - Passive fire protection
- **Engineered Rubber Solutions** - Engineered rubber parts
- **BV206 Tracks** - Tracks for the BV206 vehicle
