# University of Southampton

> Research university in Southampton with defence and security research groups, defence doctoral training and defence-related teaching.

## What they do

The University of Southampton is a Russell Group research university based in Southampton. Its research groups include a Centre for Defence and Security Research, a Cyber Security Academy, and an EPSRC and MOD Centre for Doctoral Training in Complex Integrated Systems for Defence and Security. In December 2025 it joined the UK-Australia Advanced Skills Alliance supporting security and defence research, and in June 2026 it received more than 6.6 million pounds under a Ministry of Defence and Department for Education programme to expand computing, cyber and robotics teaching.

## Products and solutions

- **Centre for Defence and Security Research** - university research group for defence and security.
- **Cyber Security Academy** - cyber security research and education.
- **Doctoral training** - EPSRC and MOD Centre for Doctoral Training in Complex Integrated Systems for Defence and Security.
