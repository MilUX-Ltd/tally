# Zeabuz

> Maritime autonomy software for commercial and naval vessels, including autonomous navigation and collision avoidance.

## What they do

Zeabuz equips vessels with autonomy software for navigation, docking, collision avoidance and remote control. It serves urban and road ferries, workboats, aquaculture, and a defence and security segment. Its platform is described as dual-use across commercial and naval domains.

## Products and solutions

- **ZeaMate** - Self-sailing autonomy system for vessels
- **ZeaControl** - Motion control system
- **ZeaWatch** - Remote supervision
