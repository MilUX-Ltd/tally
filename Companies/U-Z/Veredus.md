# Veredus

> London executive search and interim management firm serving government, defence, transport and education.

## What they do

Veredus provides interim management, executive search and selection, leadership and talent assessment, and fractional leadership, for organisations in complex and regulated environments. It works across government, defence, transport and education. In defence it describes placing executive and interim leaders with clients across the Ministry of Defence, defence agencies and innovation centres, including appointments at Defence Equipment and Support.

## Products and solutions

- **Interim Management** - executive-level interim professionals.
- **Executive Search and Selection** - research-led search for senior leaders.
- **Leadership and Talent Assessment** - assessment of leadership capability, succession readiness and fit.
- **Fractional Leadership** - experienced executives on a part-time basis.
