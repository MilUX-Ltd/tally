# Vestdavit

> Designs and supplies davit systems for launch and recovery of manned and unmanned craft, including for military customers.

## What they do

Vestdavit's davits are now presented by Fairbanks Morse Defense. The range covers standard and bespoke davits that meet SOLAS requirements and military customer demands. They are designed for launching and recovering manned and unmanned craft in difficult sea conditions.

## Products and solutions

- **Single point davits** - Standard and bespoke davits
- **Dual point davits** - Davits for launch and recovery
- **Telescopic davits** - Telescopic davit range
- **All electric range davits** - Electrically driven davits
