# Xperate

> Winchester-based software development company building integrations and bespoke software for the legal sector.

## What they do

Xperate Limited describes itself as an international software development company specialising in the legal sector. It builds integrations between case management, finance and third-party systems, bespoke legal software, workflow automation, practice and case management systems, and data migrations. The company states it started in 2021 and has offices in Winchester, Pune, Toronto and Melbourne. It is listed as a partner of several legal technology platforms.

## Products and solutions

- **Integrations** - connecting case management, finance and third-party systems.
- **Bespoke software** - custom portals, onboarding platforms and legal-specific tools.
- **Workflow automation** - mapping and implementing automated workflows.
- **PCMS development** - building, customising and integrating practice and case management systems.
- **Data migration** - end-to-end data migration.
- **Development as a Service** - ongoing outsourced development capacity.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Accreditations:** ISO 27001:2022; Cyber Essentials Plus.
- **Memberships:** South Central RDSC
