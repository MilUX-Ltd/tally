# Whiteflare

> Wiltshire recruitment business placing engineering, project and safety professionals with UK defence organisations.

## What they do

Whiteflare Ltd states it works with the UK's defence organisations to place skilled candidates on projects across the sector, covering land, air, maritime, space and cyber. Its focus is engineering, project management and safety management roles, many based at Abbey Wood and MOD Bristol. The company also states it supports service leavers with career transition from up to 12 months before leaving the military, and it is a signatory of the Armed Forces Covenant.

## Products and solutions

- **Defence recruitment** - engineering, trials, safety and munitions roles for defence employers.
- **Candidate support** - CV tailoring, interview preparation and career guidance.
- **Service leaver transition** - support for personnel leaving the armed forces.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Accreditations:** Cyber Essentials
- **Other:** Armed Forces Covenant signatory (January 2018)
