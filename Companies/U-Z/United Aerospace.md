# United Aerospace

> Pembroke Dock designer, manufacturer and finisher of composite components, tooling and assemblies for aerospace and other sectors.

## What they do

United Aerospace Limited designs, manufactures and finishes carbon and glass fibre composite components and assemblies from a purpose-built factory on Ferry Lane, Pembroke Dock, Pembrokeshire. It works from build-to-print through to prototype development and production, and states it has delivered over twenty civil airline premium interior contracts as well as medical, energy and automotive work. The company states on its LinkedIn page that it has supplied airframe body shells for Banshee target drones, first to Meggitt and later to QinetiQ. It has installed a CMS Kreator large-format additive manufacturing machine, which it describes as the first in the UK.

## Products and solutions

- **Composite components** - pre-preg moulding, RTM, resin infusion, wet lay-up and vacuum forming.
- **Design** - CAD design in CATIA V5 and SolidWorks, and CAD/CAM in Alphacam.
- **Tooling and assembly** - design and manufacture of tooling and assemblies.
- **Finishing** - trimming, sanding, paint, fabric and film covering.
- **Large-format additive manufacturing** - LFAM using a CMS Kreator machine centre.
- **Inspection** - first article dimensional reports using a FARO arm.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships
