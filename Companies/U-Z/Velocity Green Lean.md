# Velocity Green Lean

> Altrincham-based Lean improvement consultancy providing interim operations leadership, improvement projects and Lean training.

## What they do

Velocity Green Lean is a business improvement consultancy that provides interim and contract operations leadership, focused transformation projects and Lean training. It states that it works across manufacturing, engineering, supply chain and service operations, including with Airbus suppliers, and draws on a network of specialists for larger programmes. The company is based in Altrincham, Cheshire, and does not publish a site in Wales; it is listed as a member of Defence Wales.

## Products and solutions

- **Interim and contract leadership** - operations leadership to stabilise performance and restore priorities.
- **Transformation and improvement projects** - KPI-led projects covering safety, quality, delivery, cost and morale.
- **Training and capability building** - Lean foundations, leader routines and problem solving.
