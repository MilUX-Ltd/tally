# Westcon Yards

> Shipyard for repair, maintenance, conversion and newbuilds, with naval service work on Norwegian Armed Forces frigates and coast guard vessels.

## What they do

Westcon Yards is a full-service yard for repair, maintenance, conversion and newbuilding of vessels and rigs. It holds maintenance responsibility for a significant part of the Armed Forces fleet, including coast guard vessels and frigates, and aims to help develop standardised vessel solutions.

## Products and solutions

- **Naval Service** - Maintenance and upgrades of naval vessels
