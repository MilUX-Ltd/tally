# Våpensmia

> Fine mechanical manufacturer producing reflex sights for defence weapons alongside firearms service and hunting and shooting equipment.

## What they do

Våpensmia is a fine mechanical manufacturer operating since 1976 with CNC machinery and AQAP 2110 certification. It develops and produces reflex sights for 7.62 GPMG, 40 mm AGL, .50 calibre and M72 weapons for the defence and security sector. It also sells firearms, optics, reloading equipment and metals.

## Products and solutions

- **Reflex sights** - Sights for GPMG, 40 mm AGL, .50 calibre and M72
