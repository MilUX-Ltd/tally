# University of Portsmouth

> Portsmouth university providing defence-focused education and research in cyber security, AI, robotics, aerospace engineering and advanced manufacturing.

## What they do

The University of Portsmouth is a university based in Portsmouth with a London campus. It states that since 2015 it has delivered defence-focused education and professional development to more than 4,400 MOD-related learners, working with the Royal Navy, the Defence Medical Service and MOD establishments, and that it provides specialist military education through contracts at RAF Cranwell and RAF Halton. Its research includes the Centre of Excellence in Defence, Risk and Resilience and cyber security and forensics work, and it lists cyber security, robotics, AI, aerospace engineering and advanced manufacturing as defence focus areas. It is a founding member of the UK Defence Universities Alliance.

## Products and solutions

- **Centre of Excellence in Defence, Risk and Resilience** - research centre working with defence partners.
- **Cyber security and forensics research** - research combining computer science and behavioural science, including AI for intrusion detection and malware classification.
- **Centre for Cybercrime and Economic Crime** - research centre on cybercrime and economic crime.
- **Defence education** - degree and professional education for MOD-related learners, including RAF Cranwell and RAF Halton contracts.
