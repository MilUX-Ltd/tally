# Womble Bond Dickinson

> Transatlantic law firm with a Global Defence and Security Solutions team and UK offices including Southampton.

## What they do

Womble Bond Dickinson (UK) LLP is the UK member firm of the Womble Bond Dickinson network, which states that it has over 1,300 lawyers and technical specialists across 42 offices in the UK and US. Its UK offices are in Bristol, Edinburgh, Leeds, London, Newcastle, Plymouth, Southampton and Teesside. The firm's Global Defence and Security Solutions team advises defence and security businesses on international trade, government contracts and procurement, intellectual property, cyber security and digital and technology matters, and states that it maintains government clearances for lawyers who need access to sensitive government information.

## Products and solutions

- **International trade** - advice on export controls and international trade regulation for defence businesses.
- **Government contracts and procurement** - support with defence procurement and government contracting.
- **Intellectual property** - protection and exploitation of intellectual property.
- **Cyber security** - legal advice on cyber security obligations and incidents.
- **Digital and technology** - legal support for technology and digital projects.
