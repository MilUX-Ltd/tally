# YMD Boon

> Multi-disciplinary construction consultancy providing architecture, building surveying and health and safety services from offices in the Midlands and Lincoln.

## What they do

YMD Boon Limited describes itself as a multi-disciplinary construction consultancy offering architecture, building surveying, and health and safety services, with offices in Market Harborough, Nottingham, Lincoln and Birmingham. Its sectors include schools, further and higher education, blue light emergency services, heritage and conservation, commercial and industrial, leisure, residential, and justice, security and defence. For justice, security and defence work it states that its team holds security clearances, has experience of MOD and MOJ technical requirements, uses MOD-approved drones and holds Cyber Essentials Plus, enabling work on high-risk and Critical National Infrastructure sites.

## Products and solutions

- **Architecture** - design services across education, public and commercial sectors.
- **Building surveying** - condition surveys, refurbishment and remodelling projects, with an in-house Clerk of Works.
- **Health and safety** - Principal Designer, Building Regulations and CDM services.
- **Funding support** - support with the Condition Improvement Fund for schools.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Accreditations:** Cyber Essentials Plus.
- **Memberships:** Greater Lincolnshire RDSC
