# Verve Aero

> Pontypool-based designer and manufacturer of composite airframes and components for uncrewed aircraft and drones.

## What they do

Verve Aero designs, develops and manufactures off-the-shelf and bespoke fully composite airframes and components for remotely piloted aircraft, all produced in the UK. It works with commercial UAV operators and military suppliers, and offers design services including CAD/CAM, 3D printing and pattern making, together with development and research and development work. The company operates from Mamhilad Park Estate, Pontypool, in Torfaen, and has access to a test site for medium-sized drones and UAVs. It was incorporated in 2023.

## Products and solutions

- **Composite UAV airframes** - off-the-shelf and bespoke fully composite airframes for fixed-wing UAVs and drones.
- **Composite components** - parts manufactured for UAV operators.
- **Design and development** - CAD/CAM, 3D printing, pattern making and R&D for uncrewed aircraft structures.
- **Testing** - test site for medium-sized drones and UAVs.
