# 3T Additive Manufacturing

> Berkshire supplier of finished metal components and sub-assemblies made by metal additive manufacturing, serving aerospace, space, defence, energy and industrial customers.

## What they do

3T Additive Manufacturing Ltd describes itself as a UK-based supplier of finished metal components and sub-assemblies produced by metal additive manufacturing, combining printing, CNC machining, heat treatment, testing and inspection in one facility at Thatcham. The company states it acquired its first metal printer in 2007, restructured in 2021 to focus on series production, and was acquired in October 2025 by SBO AG, a Vienna-listed precision manufacturing group, as the basis of that group's Additive Manufacturing Division. It lists aerospace, space and defence among the sectors it serves and states it is an ASTM additive manufacturing Centre of Excellence in the UK.

## Products and solutions

- **Design for manufacture** - design optimisation advice balancing cost, quality and producibility.
- **Development and qualification** - early development through to qualification using established material and process parameters.
- **Series production** - production launch through an APQP-based new product introduction gate process.
- **Metal additive manufacturing** - EOS multi-laser and single-laser powder bed printers, each dedicated to an alloy family.
- **CNC machining** - five-axis machining integrated with offline CAM simulation.
