# 4Secure

> Earls Barton provider of cross domain solutions, data diodes and secure data transfer for government, defence and critical national infrastructure.

## What they do

4Secure provides Cross Domain Solutions that let government, defence, critical national infrastructure and commercial organisations move data between networks of different security classifications, alongside hardware data diodes for one-way transfer and consultancy on accreditation and certification. The company says it has worked in cyber security since 2003 and specialises in secure data transfer and operational technology security. For defence it describes Cross Domain Solutions for intelligence sharing and secure voice and video communication, built and supported in the UK.

## Products and solutions

- **TrustedFilter®** - the company's own software that inspects and verifies data at the content level, deployable on premise or in the cloud, with SECUREimpex (secure data import) and SECUREcommand (bi-directional applications over unidirectional appliances) modules.
- **Data Diodes** - hardware-enforced one-way data transfer.
- **Consultancy** - advice on best practice, accreditation and certification.
