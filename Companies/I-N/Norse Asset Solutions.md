# Norse Asset Solutions

> Norse Asset Solutions offers inspection services, sensors and unmanned vehicles for civil and defence customers.

## What they do

Norse Asset Solutions provides asset inspection, defence solutions and unmanned vehicle support. It supplies sensors and unmanned air, ground and surface vehicles for civil and defence sectors, with custom engineering.

## Products and solutions

- **Unmanned vehicles** - Airborne, ground and surface platforms
- **Sensors** - Gas, radiation and laser scanning sensors
