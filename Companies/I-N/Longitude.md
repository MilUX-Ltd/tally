# Longitude

> Independent marine design and engineering consultancy for energy, offshore and shipping, part of ABL Group.

## What they do

Longitude states that it has delivered specialist marine design, engineering and analysis services since 2006, with roots in structural engineering and naval architecture. Its work covers facilities and subsea, ship design, offshore wind and marine operations across renewables, maritime and oil and gas, and it reports a team of more than 200 design and engineering professionals worldwide. Its ship design portfolio, which includes the designs of OSD-IMT acquired in 2021, covers vessel types including small patrol, rescue and rigid inflatable boats. Longitude is part of ABL Group; its UK company is Longitude Consulting Engineers Limited.

## Products and solutions

- **Ship design** - newbuild design, conversion, upgrade and retrofit, small craft, research and offshore vessels, and clean shipping.
- **Through-life support** - engineering and design support to vessels from design through construction and operation.
- **Facilities and subsea** - field development, brownfield modifications, asset integrity and life extension.
- **Marine operations** - mobilisation, transportation and installation engineering, moorings and metocean.
- **Offshore wind** - turbine and substation foundation design and submarine power cable engineering.
