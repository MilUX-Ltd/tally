# Nine23

> Southampton high-assurance managed service provider delivering secure private cloud, collaboration and security advisory services to defence and government.

## What they do

Nine23 Ltd describes itself as a UK sovereign, high-assurance managed service provider delivering cyber secure digital services to Defence, HM Government, national security and their supply chains. It states that it was founded in 2012 by a British Army veteran and became part of Austability Group Ltd in 2026. Its services cover information security advisory and assurance, private and hybrid cloud, secure collaboration and integrated service management, built around its Platform FLEX private cloud hosted in UK data centres. For defence, the company states that Platform FLEX adheres to the Code of Connection for MOD networks and supports services at OFFICIAL-SENSITIVE and above OFFICIAL.

## Products and solutions

- **Platform FLEX** - private and hybrid cloud hosted in secure UK data centres, with FLEX Sentinel, FLEX Pulse and FLEX Pathway services.
- **LynX** - secure collaborative working, including LynX Mesh and LynX FileShare.
- **Information security advisory and assurance** - Secure by Design adoption, security and cyber strategy, assurance as a service, and threat, vulnerability and risk assessment.
- **Working above OFFICIAL** - services for organisations handling protectively marked data.
- **Integrated service management** - managed service delivery for regulated environments.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Accreditations:** Cyber Essentials Plus; ISO 27001; ISO 9001; ISO 14001; ISO/IEC 20000-1; Defence Cyber Certification (as stated by the company).
- **Memberships:** South Central RDSC
