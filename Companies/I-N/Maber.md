# Maber

> Nottingham architectural practice with offices in Birmingham, Leicester and London, whose work includes MOD cadet training facilities.

## What they do

Maber is an architectural practice headquartered at St Mary's Hall in Nottingham, with offices in Birmingham, Leicester and London. Its listed projects span museums, industrial facilities, urban regeneration, healthcare, education, corporate headquarters and biodiversity masterplanning. Its defence-related work includes the Beckingham Cadet Training Centre, which received an award at the Ministry of Defence Sanctuary Awards in 2026.

## Products and solutions

- **Architecture** - architectural design across commercial, industrial, healthcare, education and cultural sectors.
- **Masterplanning** - urban regeneration and biodiversity masterplanning.
- **Sustainability** - sustainable building design.
