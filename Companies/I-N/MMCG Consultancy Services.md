# MMCG Consultancy Services

> Formby consultancy providing digital transformation delivery, programme management and business change.

## What they do

MMCG describes itself as a mission-focused consultancy and digital transformation delivery partner, providing delivery leadership from strategic direction through to implementation and assurance. Its services are portfolio, programme and project management, business change, business analysis and research, strategy and transformation, and training and capability development. It says it has deep roots in the Ministry of Defence and public service, and works across defence, law enforcement, the NHS and government.
