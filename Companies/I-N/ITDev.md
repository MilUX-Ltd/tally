# ITDev

> Southampton software and electronics design consultancy providing managed technology design services and FPGA IP.

## What they do

ITDev provides fully managed technology design services from its offices on the Southampton Science Park. The company states that it supplies software and electronic engineering services to customers in the UK, Europe, the USA and worldwide, covering technology consultancy, product development and design services, and that it develops FPGA intellectual property for media-over-IP. It lists projects in telecommunications, scientific and research, energy, aerospace and marine, automotive and semiconductor markets. Formed in 2000 and incorporated as I.T. Dev Limited in 2003, it states it is wholly owned by its directors.

## Products and solutions

- **Technology consultancy** - advice on technology choices, design architecture and approach.
- **Product development** - support through product development, including innovation days and continuous integration services.
- **Design services** - software, electronics and systems development, including ASIC, FPGA and PCB test and evaluation.
- **FPGA IP** - IPMX media-over-IP IP cores, 3D LUT and SDR/HDR conversion.
