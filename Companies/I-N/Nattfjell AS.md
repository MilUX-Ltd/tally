# Nattfjell AS

> Nattfjell, in Alta, makes passive modular drone-protection nets for secure corridors and infrastructure.

## What they do

Nattfjell AS makes modular, passive protection against small FPV drones, produced on a Norwegian industrial line. Its systems cover protected road corridors, thermal signature screening and whole-site cover for critical infrastructure.

## Products and solutions

- **NattSC** - Passive net corridor modules that stop small FPV drones
- **NattTherm** - Screening that reduces thermal signature
- **NattShield** - Net cover over whole infrastructure sites
