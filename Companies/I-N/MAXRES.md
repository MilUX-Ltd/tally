# MAXRES

> Bristol developer of secure AI tools that turn source material into courses, lessons and assessments for defence and regulated industries.

## What they do

MAXRES provides AI courseware development that turns source material into structured courses, lessons and assessments, with subject matter experts reviewing and approving the output. For defence, the company says it develops DSAT-aligned courseware against JSP 822 for delivery in connected or air-gapped environments, running on the customer's own hardware. It also offers sovereign and edge AI hardware and immersive media production.

## Products and solutions

- **Architect** - AI curriculum design from training needs analyses, scoping documents and standards.
- **Construct** - AI courseware production of lessons, assessments and SCORM or xAPI packages, with sign-off and audit trail.
- **Morpheus** - immersive media toolset for VR, 3D, 360° video and AI-assisted media.
- **Sovereign AI Hardware** - sovereign and edge AI, XR and deployable devices, including MSDS hardware for delivering training content into disconnected environments.
