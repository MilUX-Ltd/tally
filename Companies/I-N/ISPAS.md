# ISPAS

> Norwegian radar and RF company making radar emitters, radar cross section measurement systems and oil spill detection radar.

## What they do

ISPAS develops radar and RF equipment. Its Nordic Training Emitter is an X-band emitter that simulates real radar systems for sensor verification and mission-critical training of military aircraft. It also makes radar cross section measurement systems and an oil spill detection radar used on offshore platforms.

## Products and solutions

- **Nordic Training Emitter (NTE)** - X-band RF emitter that simulates radar systems for training and testing.
- **RCS measurement systems** - Radar cross section measurement equipment and support.
- **OSD radar** - Ku-band radar for detecting oil on the sea surface.
