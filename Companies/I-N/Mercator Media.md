# Mercator Media

> Fareham events and media company for the maritime industry and organiser of the Seawork commercial marine exhibition.

## What they do

Mercator Media Ltd describes itself as an events and media company dedicated to the maritime industry, established in 1989. It organises exhibitions, conferences and webinars and publishes B2B media covering commercial marine and workboats, ports and terminals, shipping and technology, and commercial fishing and aquaculture. Its events include the Seawork exhibition in Southampton, described as the European commercial marine and workboat exhibition, and Speed@Seawork. The company is based at Spinnaker House in Fareham, Hampshire.

## Products and solutions

- **Seawork** - European commercial marine and workboat exhibition in Southampton, with conferences, vessels afloat and awards.
- **Speed@Seawork** - on-water event in Cowes.
- **Conferences** - including World Ports Conference, Propulsion and Future Fuels, Coastlink and GreenPort Congress.
- **Media brands** - Maritime Journal, GreenPort, Maritime Contracts Journal, World Fishing and Aquaculture, Port Strategy and The Motorship.
