# MilDef Ltd

> UK subsidiary of the Swedish MilDef Group, supplying rugged IT hardware and systems integration for defence customers from Bro Tathan, St Athan.

## What they do

MilDef Ltd is the UK company of MilDef Group AB, a Swedish supplier of rugged electronics, tactical IT and related software and services for defence and security customers. MilDef states that it was established in the UK in 2011 through the acquisition of Terralogic Ltd, and the UK company operates from Building 232 on Dragon Way, Bro Tathan West, St Athan, in the Vale of Glamorgan. The group's range covers rugged computers and servers, laptops, tablets and handhelds, displays, network equipment and power products built to military standards, together with systems integration and support. A second UK company in the group, MilDef UK Ltd (formerly Westek Technology Ltd), operates from Melksham, Wiltshire.

## Products and solutions

- **Rugged computing** - computers, servers, laptops, tablets and handhelds for vehicle, command post and dismounted use.
- **Displays and peripherals** - rugged displays, panel PCs, KVM consoles and peripherals.
- **Network and power products** - rugged Ethernet switches, routers, firewalls, media converters and power distribution units.
- **Solutions integration** - integration of hardware and software into customer systems, with through-life support.
