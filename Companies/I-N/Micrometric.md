# Micrometric

> Lincoln laser processing specialist and contract manufacturer of fine parts and precision components.

## What they do

Micrometric Ltd was founded in 1981 in Lincoln and specialises in the laser processing of metals. It provides contract and subcontract manufacturing of precision components, from prototypes to volume production, using laser cutting, welding, drilling and etching, tube cutting and precision machining. It serves customers in aerospace, automotive, energy, medical devices, oil and gas, instrumentation, electronics and general engineering. The company states that it holds AS 9100 and ISO 9001 accreditation.

## Products and solutions

- **Laser cutting** - fine cutting of metals and other materials.
- **Laser welding** - precision welding of components.
- **Laser drilling and etching** - fine holes, marking and etching.
- **Tube cutting** - laser cutting of tubes.
- **Precision machining** - component machining.
- **Reverse engineering** - reproduction of existing parts.
