# Mission Insight

> Gloucester data company building enterprise data architectures and turning raw data into near real-time insight.

## What they do

Mission Insight describes itself as experts in building enterprise-level data architectures, specialising in end-to-end data operations that turn raw data into real-time, actionable insights. Its services are data engineering to bring disparate datasets into unified data models, bespoke software engineering, and advanced analytics using AI and machine learning to support operational decisions and visualise data in near real time. It cites military operations and cyber security among the settings where timely decisions matter.
