# Norwegian Special Mission AS

> Nannestad company making airborne mission systems for surveillance, flight inspection and remote sensing for civilian and military customers.

## What they do

Norwegian Special Mission builds mission systems for special mission aircraft. Its product range covers airborne surveillance, flight inspection, remote sensing and airborne laboratories, and it also offers mission systems for remotely piloted aircraft. It serves civilian and military customers worldwide and is part of the Sundt Group.

## Products and solutions

- **SeaHunter 8000** - Mission management system for airborne surveillance
- **UNIFIS 3000** - Flight calibration and validation system for navigation and communication aids
- **SkyHawk 7000** - Mission system for remote sensing and airborne laboratories
