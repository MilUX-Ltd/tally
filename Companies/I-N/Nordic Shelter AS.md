# Nordic Shelter AS

> Designer and maker of mobile, self-sustained shelters for military, emergency response and industrial clients.

## What they do

Nordic Shelter designs and manufactures mobile, self-sustained shelters and describes itself as a provider of military and industrial shelters. It offers project management, system engineering, design, welding, assembly, in-house sandwich panel production and coating, including camouflage coatings. It has operations in Norway, Sweden and Estonia.

## Products and solutions

- **Expandable shelters** - Shelters that expand on site.
- **Lightweight shelters** - Lighter mobile shelters.
- **Hooklift and special shelters** - Shelters for hooklift carriers.
- **Mobile hospitals** - Deployable medical facilities.
- **Flatrack platforms** - Flatrack carriers.
