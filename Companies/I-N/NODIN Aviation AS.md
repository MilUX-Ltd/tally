# NODIN Aviation AS

> Develops and delivers systems for medical evacuation by land, sea and air, including a casualty evacuation stretcher kit.

## What they do

NODIN develops and delivers tailored systems and solutions for medical evacuations, covering CASEVAC, MEDEVAC, TACEVAC and STRATEVAC across land, sea and air. Its main product is a shock and vibration damped stretcher suspension system installable in vehicles, boats, helicopters and aircraft. The site names military and defence organisations among its customers.

## Products and solutions

- **NODIN CASEVAC kit** - Shock and vibration damped stretcher suspension system for one to three patients.
