# Nortrak

> Lincoln technology company supplying GPS trackers, IoT sensors and cloud telematics software for tracking and monitoring assets, vehicles and infrastructure.

## What they do

Nortrak Ltd describes itself as a UK technology company helping organisations connect, monitor and manage physical assets, equipment and infrastructure. It combines GPS tracking, IoT sensors, wireless communications and cloud-based software, and integrates the resulting data with business systems such as ERP and asset management platforms. The company states it specialises in battery-powered GPS trackers and serves sectors including logistics and supply chain, construction and plant hire, rail and infrastructure, fleet and trailer tracking, and security and evidential tracking. It operates from Lincoln and has signed the Armed Forces Covenant.

## Products and solutions

- **GPS trackers** - battery-powered and 4G asset and vehicle trackers.
- **Telematics platform** - cloud software and apps for tracking, alerts and analytics.
- **Sensors and alerts** - temperature, shock, humidity and movement monitoring.
- **TrailerSentry** - trailer and towed-asset tracking.
- **Sign-Bot** - road sign status monitoring.
- **Data integration** - API integration with existing business systems.
