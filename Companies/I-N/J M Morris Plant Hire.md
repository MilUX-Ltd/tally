# J M Morris Plant Hire

> Warwickshire plant hire company providing road sweepers, vacuum excavators and dust suppression.

## What they do

J M Morris Plant Hire provides plant hire across the Midlands and Warwickshire, including road sweeping, beam sweeping, vacuum excavation and dust suppression. It also hires a tipper lorry and a 360 excavator. The company states it offers a 24/7 service with trained operators and in-house engineering and maintenance, on contract or one-off hire. Its website gives its location as Rugby, Warwickshire.

## Products and solutions

- **Road sweeper hire** - road sweeper lorries.
- **Beam sweepers** - surface finishing using rotor jets.
- **Vacuum excavation** - vacuum excavator hire.
- **Dust suppression** - dust suppression services.
- **Tipper and excavator hire** - tipper lorry and 360 excavator.
