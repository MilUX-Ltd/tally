# Matre

> Norwegian maker of fire fighting systems, foam proportioners, nozzles and pumps for maritime, offshore and naval vessels.

## What they do

Matre supplies products, systems and services to the maritime and offshore industry, including fire fighting, calibration equipment, pumps and machining. It has a naval segment that provides modular fire protection systems for naval vessels, with reference vessels in coast guard and mine countermeasure classes.

## Products and solutions

- **Deck integrated fire fighting systems (DIFFS)** - Fire fighting systems for helicopter decks and vessel decks.
- **Foam proportioners** - Balanced pressure, bladder tank, in-line and inductor units.
- **Nozzles** - Water curtain, pop-up and deluge nozzles.
