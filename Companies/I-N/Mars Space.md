# Mars Space

> Southampton developer of electric propulsion systems and plasma technologies for spacecraft, owned by Aerospazio Tecnologie.

## What they do

Mars Space Ltd states that it designs, develops and delivers electric propulsion systems and plasma technologies for spacecraft. Founded in 2007, it was acquired in 2019 by Aerospazio Tecnologie Srl, an Italian test services company, and relocated in 2022 to its current headquarters at Adanac Park near Southampton. It operates an electric propulsion laboratory with vacuum chambers, an ISO-class cleanroom, in-house simulation and high-tolerance machining. The website shows ESA, the UK Space Agency and QinetiQ among its partners.

## Products and solutions

- **Gridded ion thrusters** - RIT3.5, T5, T6, T7 and GIE150 thrusters.
- **Hollow cathodes** - HC4 hollow cathode and DN3.4 dry cathode.
- **Pulsed plasma thruster** - low-power PPT for small spacecraft.
- **Test and manufacture** - electric propulsion laboratory testing, cleanroom manufacture and precision machining.
