# Metrology Engineering Services

> Welsh aerospace engineering company providing 3D laser scanning, reverse engineering, structural damage assessment and MRO technical support.

## What they do

Metrology Engineering Services Ltd (MES) provides laser scanning, reverse engineering, damage assessment and structural reporting, on site and off site, using its own data acquisition methods and software tools. Its core team has a background in aircraft repair engineering, and it offers MRO technical support including round-the-clock support for aircraft on ground situations. The company's HQ and Technology Centre is at Waterside Court, Newport, and its registered office is at Bro Tathan, St Athan. Besides aerospace, it applies the same methods to rail, automotive, construction and insurance work.

## Products and solutions

- **Metrology and 3D laser scanning** - digital capture of aircraft structures and components.
- **Reverse engineering** - digital models and design data from scanned parts.
- **Damage assessment** - structural assessment and reporting for aircraft repair.
- **Portable X-ray radiography** - on-site inspection.
- **MRO technical support** - repair engineering support for MROs, airlines and OEMs, including AOG support.
- **Engineering consultancy** - structural verification and independent damage validation for other sectors.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Accreditations:** AS9100 and ISO 9001 (certified by BSI); validations with EASA, FAA, ANAC, CAAC and TCCA.
- **Memberships:** Defence Wales, Aerospace Wales, The 5% Club
