# IEC Telecom

> Satellite communications and managed connectivity provider serving maritime and land markets, including defence.

## What they do

IEC Telecom provides satellite communications and managed connectivity for businesses and mission-critical operations on land and at sea. Its site lists defence and civil government as separate land markets. It offers connectivity services, satellite terminals and managed network services.

## Products and solutions

- **Managed VSAT** - Managed satellite connectivity service.
- **Hybrid Connectivity** - Combined satellite and other network connectivity.
- **OptiShield** - Managed cybersecurity service.
- **Satellite terminals** - Maritime and land terminals and satellite phones.
