# Norsk Elektro Optikk AS (NEO)

> Oslo company developing and making electro-optical instruments, including HySpex hyperspectral cameras, lasers and LiDAR systems.

## What they do

Norsk Elektro Optikk AS was founded in 1985 by researchers from the Norwegian Defence Research Establishment. It combines research and development with manufacturing of electro-optical systems for industry, aerospace and defence. Its work covers hyperspectral imaging, lasers and LiDAR, and spectroscopy.

## Products and solutions

- **HySpex hyperspectral cameras** - VNIR and SWIR hyperspectral cameras for airborne, field and laboratory use
- **Space instruments** - Electro-optical instruments for space applications
