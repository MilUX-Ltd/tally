# Messenger BCR

> Building contractor specialising in heritage conservation and restoration, main contracting, passive fire protection, specialist roofing and structural repairs.

## What they do

Messenger BCR describes itself as a traditional building contractor offering a wide range of services connected to the built environment. Formed in April 2011, it is headquartered at the Collyweston Heritage Centre near Stamford and also has a regional office in Burntwood, Staffordshire. The company states it directly employs close to one hundred people. Its portfolio includes conservation and restoration work at historic buildings such as Leicester Cathedral, Stowe House and Royal Hospital Chelsea.

## Products and solutions

- **Main contracting** - building contracting services.
- **Insurance related repairs** - repair and recovery work.
- **Specialist conservation and restoration** - work on historic and listed buildings.
- **Passive fire protection** - fire protection services.
- **Collyweston and specialist roofing** - traditional and specialist roofing.
- **Specialist structural repairs** - structural repair division launched in 2025.
