# iS3

> Lincoln consultancy building business systems, automation and governed AI for SMEs, with a focus on audit-ready processes and evidence.

## What they do

iS3 Ltd states that it builds the systems behind an audit-ready business: fixing processes, bringing data into one system, automating repetitive work and adding AI with governance controls. It describes its work as making systems work, making them safe through governance, compliance and risk controls, and making them last through training, adoption and continuous improvement. It serves manufacturing and engineering firms, solicitors and accountants, and trades and contractors. The company states that it works from Lincoln.

## Products and solutions

- **Governance-led AI systems** - AI implementation with controls, accountability and an AI governance check.
- **Business operating system** - joining customer, stock, order and operational records into one system.
- **Automation and integration** - connecting systems and automating manual processes.
- **Software review** - fixed-price production readiness review for software built with AI.
- **Strategic leadership and workshops** - strategy, training and continuous improvement support.
