# Matchtech

> Engineering, technology and STEM recruitment brand of Gattaca plc, with defence and aerospace recruitment teams.

## What they do

Matchtech is described by Gattaca plc as its flagship recruitment brand and STEM skills specialist, and Gattaca plc states it now operates its recruitment brands under the Matchtech name. The Matchtech website states it offers permanent, contract and temporary recruitment, outsourced recruitment programmes and targeted talent campaigns. It states it works in defence and security sectors including aerospace defence, unmanned air systems, communication systems, land defence, naval defence, national security and public sector, and aerospace sectors including space. Matchtech Group (UK) Limited is registered at Solent Business Park, Fareham.

## Products and solutions

- **Recruitment** - contract, temporary, permanent and leadership search.
- **Outsourcing** - managed service programmes, recruitment process outsourcing and services procurement outsourcing.
- **Recruit, train, deploy** - targeted and inclusive recruitment campaigns.
