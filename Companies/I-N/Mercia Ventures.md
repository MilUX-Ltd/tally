# Mercia Ventures

> UK venture capital investor backing high-growth businesses at Series A and beyond, part of the Mercia Asset Management group.

## What they do

Mercia Ventures is a UK venture capital firm that invests in companies at Series A and beyond, providing up to 10 million pounds of initial capital for scale-up, international expansion and growth. It focuses on AI and software, consumer, deep tech and life sciences, and states it is open to businesses outside these areas. It states that it has invested over 2.5 billion pounds across 2,660 UK businesses, with a strong presence outside London. Mercia Ventures is a trading name of investment funds managed by Mercia Fund Management Limited and Mercia Regional Ventures Limited, subsidiaries of Mercia Asset Management PLC (company number 09223445), whose registered office is in Henley-in-Arden.

## Products and solutions

- **Venture capital investment** - Series A and later investment of up to 10 million pounds initial capital.
- **Platform** - an opt-in founder support programme with a founder community, events, workshops and partner introductions.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Accreditations:** Its fund management subsidiaries are authorised and regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority.
- **Memberships:** Greater Lincolnshire RDSC
