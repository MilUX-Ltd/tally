# Manpower UK

> Recruitment and workforce solutions company, part of ManpowerGroup, with a branch in Bridgend.

## What they do

Manpower UK provides temporary and permanent recruitment, flexible staffing, onsite workforce management and workforce solutions to employers across sectors including engineering, logistics, driving and customer service. It is part of ManpowerGroup, whose UK brands also include Brook Street and Experis, and it operates a national branch network. Its Welsh branch is in Bridgend; the company's registered office is in Uxbridge. Its programmes include an Academy of Advanced Manufacturing that trains military veterans as automation technicians.

## Products and solutions

- **Recruitment** - temporary, temp-to-permanent and permanent recruitment through local branches.
- **Workforce solutions** - flexible staffing, onsite management and end-to-end workforce programmes for enterprise clients.
- **Specialist talent** - recruitment in skills areas such as engineering, green technologies and supply chain.
- **MyPath** - training and career development programme for Manpower associates.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Accreditations:** ManpowerGroup holds the Defence Employer Recognition Scheme Gold award (since 2017).
- **Memberships:** Defence Wales
