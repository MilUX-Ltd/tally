# KDC Projects

> Engineering services company at Bournemouth Airport providing systems, safety, design, structural analysis and test engineering to aerospace and defence programmes.

## What they do

KDC Projects Limited states that it has over 160 engineers supporting complex engineering programmes across aerospace and defence. It provides systems-led engineering with specialist capability in safety engineering, design engineering, structural analysis and test engineering. The company states it works through embedded engineers in customer teams, managed work packages, urgent programme support and specialist technical input. Its published case studies include air-to-air refuelling pod avionic software and design work, an A220 test rig and submarine design certification and legislative compliance.

## Products and solutions

- **Systems engineering** - requirements capture and management through the development lifecycle.
- **Safety engineering** - safety compliance across critical systems.
- **Design engineering** - design of manufacturable engineering solutions.
- **Structural analysis** - evidence-based structural analysis to optimise designs.
- **Test engineering** - certification-ready test engineering across the verification lifecycle, including test rig design, commissioning and operation.
- **Managed work packages** - ownership of defined engineering scopes with agreed outputs.
