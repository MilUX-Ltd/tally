# Lincoln Science and Innovation Park

> Lincoln science park providing laboratory, office and conference space for science and technology businesses and university researchers.

## What they do

Lincoln Science and Innovation Park was founded in 2012 by joint venture partners Lincolnshire Co-op and the University of Lincoln, on the site of the former Ruston Bucyrus factory in central Lincoln. It states it provides a science park environment for science and technology businesses and university researchers, with buildings including the Boole Technology Centre, Alchemy Building, Think Tank, Gravity Building and Joseph Banks Laboratories. It also offers meeting and conference facilities, design and build opportunities for occupiers and a business angels programme.

## Products and solutions

- **Laboratory and office space** - buildings including the Boole Technology Centre and Joseph Banks Laboratories.
- **Meetings and conferencing** - conference and meeting facilities.
- **Business angels** - a programme connecting angel investors with growth businesses.
