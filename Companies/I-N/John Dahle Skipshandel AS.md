# John Dahle Skipshandel AS

> Supplier of lifting, rigging, lashing and maritime equipment, with a defence industry offering for military load securing and lifting.

## What they do

John Dahle (JDS) sells lifting, rigging, lashing, fall-protection and maritime products, and offers rental, inspection and repair of lifting equipment. Its site has a defence industry section covering lashing and recovery, lifting equipment, and software and consulting. It reports an agreement signed with Forsvarsmateriell.

## Products and solutions

- **Military lashing and recovery** - Load securing and recovery equipment for defence use.
- **Lifting equipment** - Lifting and rigging products, with inspection and repair.
