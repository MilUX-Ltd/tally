# Jotne Connect

> Jotne Connect makes standards-based product data management software for aeronautics, space, defence and the built environment.

## What they do

Jotne Connect develops software for exchanging, sharing and archiving product and engineering data using open standards such as ISO 10303. Its site has a defence services page covering data interoperability, and it cites involvement in the F-35 programme. It is based at Fornebu.

## Products and solutions

- **EXPRESS Data Manager** - Data management platform based on STEP and PLCS standards
- **EDMtruePLM** - Product model server for storing and accessing product data over its lifetime
- **EDMopenSimDM** - Simulation data management collaboration server
