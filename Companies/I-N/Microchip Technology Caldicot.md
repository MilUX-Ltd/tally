# Microchip Technology Caldicot

> South Wales advanced packaging site of US semiconductor company Microchip Technology, designing and manufacturing miniaturised high-reliability modules for harsh-environment applications.

## What they do

Microchip Technology Caldicot Limited is the UK subsidiary that runs Microchip Technology's Advanced Packaging Services site at Castlegate Business Park, Caldicot, Monmouthshire. The site designs, prototypes and manufactures miniaturised modules for high-reliability and harsh-environment applications, with aerospace and medical devices as its main markets, and has provided microelectronic services since 1983. It was formerly Microsemi Semiconductor Limited and took its current name after Microchip acquired Microsemi. The site is a partner in CSconnected, the UK Research and Innovation funded compound semiconductor cluster in South Wales.

## Products and solutions

- **Advanced packaging services** - design, rapid prototyping and manufacture of miniaturised electronic modules.
- **High-reliability modules** - for high temperature, high power and other harsh environments in aerospace, medical and telecommunications uses.
- **Panel moulding** - large-area panel moulding services for high-reliability products.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Accreditations:** ISO 9001:2015 (Microchip Technology Caldicot site). The site states it follows MIL-STD-883, ISO 13485 and AS9100 guidelines where appropriate.
- **Memberships:** Defence Wales; CSconnected partner.
