# Norse Concepts AS

> Consultant, product developer and distributor serving law enforcement, armed forces and first responders in Europe.

## What they do

Norse Concepts offers help with sales, public procurement, production and design. It develops new products for customers using local manufacturers, and distributes products from professional vendors to the European market. Its stated main focus is law enforcement, armed forces, fire departments and first responders.
