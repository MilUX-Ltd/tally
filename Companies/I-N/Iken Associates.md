# Iken Associates

> Boutique environmental and sustainability consultancy advising on climate and nature risk, supply chain resilience and environmental compliance.

## What they do

Iken Associates describes itself as a boutique consultancy providing advisory and delivery services across environmental and sustainability disciplines. Established in 2001, it states it has delivered projects across environment, sustainability, nature, health and climate for businesses, public sector organisations, governments and NGOs. Its practices include climate and nature risk management for corporate reporting, resilient supply chains, aviation and maritime, environmental compliance, and finance, risk and resilience.

## Products and solutions

- **Climate and nature risk management** - gap analysis and double materiality assessment for sustainability disclosures.
- **Resilient supply chains** - advice on diversified, sustainable and resilient supply chains.
- **Aviation and maritime** - sustainability and resilience for ports, airports, transport and logistics.
- **Environmental compliance** - environmental compliance support.
- **Finance, risk and resilience** - identifying risks and opportunities and implementing change.
