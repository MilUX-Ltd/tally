# Nicomatic Norway

> Nicomatic Norway, in Asker, offers connectors and cable technology for demanding applications including defence.

## What they do

Nicomatic Norway is the Norwegian arm of a connector maker and works with Accurate Nordic. Its site covers connectors, cable manufacture, enclosures and electronics for demanding uses, including defence and military, and tactical communications fibre.
