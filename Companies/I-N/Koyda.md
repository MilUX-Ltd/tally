# Koyda

> Surrey architectural metalwork and structural steel company designing, fabricating and installing balustrades, balconies, staircases and steelwork.

## What they do

Koyda Ltd is an architectural metalwork and structural steel company based at Jesmor Farm in Lingfield, Surrey, incorporated in 2005. It designs, fabricates and installs balustrades, balconies, staircases, handrails, plant screens and structural steelwork for main contractors, builders, developers, design teams and homeowners across London and the South East. The company states that it provides in-house 3D design and drawings, including design information for Gateway 2 on higher-risk buildings under the Building Safety Act. It states that it holds Constructionline Silver and is working towards ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and BS EN 1090 certification.

## Products and solutions

- **Balustrades and handrails** - glass and metal balustrades and handrails.
- **Balconies** - including aluminium balconies.
- **Staircases** - feature and functional stairs.
- **Structural steel** - design, fabrication and installation of steelwork.
- **Plant screens** - rooftop plant screening.
- **Design and engineering** - in-house 3D modelling and Gateway 2 design information.
