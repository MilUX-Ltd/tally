# LA International

> Stoke-on-Trent digital and technology resourcing company supplying security-cleared talent to defence, national security and government clients.

## What they do

LA International Computer Consultants Ltd is a digital and technology recruitment and project solutions company based in Stoke-on-Trent. The company states it supplies security-cleared and non-cleared contract and permanent talent, recruitment process outsourcing and outcome-based project delivery. It states it holds Enhanced Government Security Accreditation and runs an in-house security vetting division that can verify and transfer existing clearances and sponsor new ones. It states it serves sectors including aerospace, defence, central government and health.

## Products and solutions

- **Contract recruitment** - flexible security-cleared and non-cleared professionals.
- **Permanent recruitment** - permanent hires in digital and technology disciplines.
- **Recruitment process outsourcing** - managed recruitment partnerships.
- **Project solutions and delivery** - outcome-based delivery teams.
- **Security vetting** - verification, transfer and sponsorship of security clearances.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Accreditations:** ISO 9001; ISO 27001; ISO 20000; Cyber Essentials Plus.
- **Other:** Enhanced Government Security Accreditation.
