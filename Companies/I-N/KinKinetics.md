# KinKinetics

> Early-stage Welsh research company developing wearable, AI-based systems that monitor stress signals and support decision-making under pressure.

## What they do

KinKinetics is a neuroadaptive AI research company based in Cardiff. Its prototype system reads physiological stress signals such as skin conductance and heart-rate variability from a wearable, estimates the user's cognitive state in real time and gives visual or haptic feedback. The company states its near-term focus is healthcare and research, with high-stakes operators, space missions and defence as later applications. It describes the system as a research prototype, not a medical device.

## Products and solutions

- **Neuroadaptive monitoring prototype** - a sense, decide and respond loop combining wearable sensing, machine learning and feedback.
- **Decision support** - real-time guidance based on physiological data and predictive modelling for high-pressure decisions.
- **Emotional support systems** - haptic and AI-based support for people in isolated and confined environments.
