# Magload

> Hampshire designer and seller of belt-worn reloading accessories and firearm upgrade components, and a registered firearms dealer.

## What they do

Magload Ltd states that it designs and manufactures belt-worn accessories and firearm upgrade components intended to improve reload speed and performance, for uses it describes as ranging from competition shooting to the front line. Its online shop sells shotgun, rifle and pistol loaders, magwells, magazine extensions, holsters, belts and parts for a wide range of firearm makes, with dealers in the UK and overseas and worldwide shipping. The company states it is also a UK Registered Firearms Dealer offering firearms sales, gunsmithing, repair and storage services in Hampshire, and that it offers discounts to police, military and emergency services personnel.

## Products and solutions

- **Shotgun loaders and magwells** - speed loaders, belt systems and magwells for competition and tactical shotguns.
- **Rifle and pistol accessories** - magazine pouches, magwells, muzzle brakes, AR-15 and AR-22 parts.
- **Belts and belt technology** - belt-worn carriage systems.
- **Firearms dealer services** - sales of firearms and controlled components, gunsmithing, repair and gun storage.
