# NFM Group

> NFM Group makes personal protective equipment for armed forces, law enforcement and first responders.

## What they do

NFM Group specialises in protective equipment for the military and law enforcement, with over 90 engineers. Its site also describes it leading European Defence Fund projects on protective equipment standards.

## Products and solutions

- **Hjelm** - Helmet product line
- **Skjold** - Protection product line
