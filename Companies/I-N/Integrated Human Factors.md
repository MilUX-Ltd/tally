# Integrated Human Factors

> Edinburgh human factors consultancy providing consulting, software, wearable technology and training, with aerospace and defence experience.

## What they do

Integrated Human Factors (IHF), operating as IHF Limited, states it delivers human factors consulting, software, technology and training. The company states that aviation and aerospace are its heritage, that it has a track record of human systems integration in the aerospace and defence sector, and that it is a preferred supplier to the UK Ministry of Defence and the UK Civil Aviation Authority. It states it also works in rail, maritime, nuclear, oil and gas, healthcare, finance and other sectors, and that it is a registered consultancy of the Chartered Institute of Ergonomics and Human Factors.

## Products and solutions

- **Human factors consultancy** - ergonomics assessments, safety critical task analysis, incident investigation and human factors integration in user interface and user experience design.
- **Human factors software (IHF SaaS)** - digital permit to work, incident investigation (HF-AIR) and safety critical task analysis (TASC) software.
- **BaselineNC** - a workplace fatigue monitoring wearable.
- **Haitch-F maturity assessment** - a human factors maturity assessment.
- **Training and eLearning** - human factors training, including aviation and aerospace training.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Memberships:** Chartered Institute of Ergonomics and Human Factors (registered consultancy).
