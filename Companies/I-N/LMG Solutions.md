# LMG Solutions

> Engineering services company providing numerical control (NC) part programs, CAD modelling and jig and fixture design for manufacturers, from a base in Bridgend.

## What they do

LMG Solutions describes itself as a provider of NC part programs and engineering services. Its work covers NC part programming, program optimisation, part verification, post processing, 2D and 3D CAD modelling, and jig and fixture design. It lists military, aerospace, automotive, medical, oil and gas, and space among the sectors it serves. The company operates from an office at the Sony UK Technology Centre on Pencoed Technology Park, Bridgend, and has a second site in Leighton Buzzard, Bedfordshire.

## Products and solutions

- **NC part programming** - creation of numerical control programs for machining.
- **Program optimisation and part verification** - optimisation and verification of NC programs.
- **Post processing** - post processor work for machine tools.
- **CAD modelling** - 2D and 3D modelling.
- **Jig and fixture design** - design of tooling for manufacture.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Memberships:** Defence Wales
