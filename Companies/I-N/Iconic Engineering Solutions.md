# Iconic Engineering Solutions

> Grantham CNC precision machining and engineering design company serving motorsport, defence, agriculture, medical, industrial and aerospace customers.

## What they do

Iconic Engineering Solutions Ltd states that it was founded in 2013 to provide engineering services to other businesses, moved into its own unit in 2020 and expanded to a 5,000 square foot workshop at The Old Malthouse in Grantham in 2021. It provides CNC milling and turning, design and analysis, 3D scanning, 3D printing and metal finishing, and states that its quality processes are certified to ISO 9001. It lists motorsport, defence, agriculture, medical, industrial and aerospace among the industries it serves, with customers across Lincolnshire, the East Midlands and the wider UK.

## Products and solutions

- **CNC machining** - precision milling, turning and multi-axis machining.
- **Design and analysis** - CAD design and mechanical engineering analysis.
- **3D scanning and 3D printing** - scanning and additive manufacturing services.
- **Quality and inspection** - CMM and vision measurement inspection.
- **Metal finishing** - finishing services for machined parts.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Memberships:** Greater Lincolnshire RDSC
