# NatWest

> UK bank providing personal, premier, business, and corporate and institutional banking.

## What they do

National Westminster Bank plc, trading as NatWest, is a UK bank with its registered office at 250 Bishopsgate, London. It is authorised by the Prudential Regulation Authority and regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority and the Prudential Regulation Authority, and its website presents it as part of NatWest Group. It provides bank accounts, savings, mortgages, loans, credit cards and insurance to personal customers, and business banking, financing, payment services and growth support to businesses, alongside services for corporates and institutions.

## Products and solutions

- **Business banking** - business accounts, lending and financing for growth.
- **Payments** - card payment services including Tyl and Tap to Pay.
- **Business support** - the NatWest Accelerator programme, business guides and events.
- **Corporate and institutional banking** - services for larger corporates and institutions.
- **Personal banking** - current accounts, savings, mortgages, loans and credit cards.
