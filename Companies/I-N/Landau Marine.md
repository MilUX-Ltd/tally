# Landau Marine

> Southampton marine engineering company refitting, repairing and maintaining leisure, commercial, historic and defence vessels.

## What they do

Landau Marine, the trading name of Landau UK Ltd, delivers marine engineering across the leisure, commercial, historic and OEM sectors, covering refit, refurbishment, repair, composite manufacturing and OEM production support. The company states it has worked with all arms of the UK military as well as police and Border Force, providing fleet maintenance and servicing, refits, upgrades and repairs, and vessel life extension. Its head office is at Landau Supermarine Quay in Woolston, Southampton, and it also operates from Marchwood Military Port and Swanwick Marina. It also runs a boat haulage and logistics service.

## Products and solutions

- **Refit and repair** - refit, refurbishment and repair of RIBs, patrol boats and commercial vessels up to 24m.
- **Defence and government fleet support** - planned maintenance schedules, turnkey solutions and vessel life extension.
- **Marine engineering services** - mechanical and electrical survey and testing, welding and fabrication, composite and gel coat repairs, coding and MCA compliance.
- **OEM and manufacturing support** - composite manufacturing and production support for boat builders.
- **Landau Haulage** - transport and logistics of vessels.
