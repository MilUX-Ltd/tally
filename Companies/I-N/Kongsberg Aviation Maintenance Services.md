# Kongsberg Aviation Maintenance Services

> Maintenance, repair, overhaul and upgrade provider for military aircraft, helicopters, engines and avionics, based at Kjeller.

## What they do

Kongsberg Aviation Maintenance Services carries out maintenance, modification, repair and upgrade of aircraft, helicopters, engines and components. Its work includes support for the Norwegian F-16 fleet and Bell 412 helicopters. It is part of the Kongsberg defence and aerospace business area.
