# Inexorable

> UK developer of autonomy and orchestration technology for controlling multiple uncrewed vehicles in defence missions.

## What they do

Inexorable Ltd develops autonomous control technology for uncrewed vehicles (UxV) for defence, with the stated aim of allowing one operator to direct many assets on complex missions. It describes a shift from command-based control to collaborative autonomy and orchestration, with decentralised control intended to tolerate communications or node loss. The company states its team has experience in autonomous systems, AI and machine learning, and previously founded a company acquired by Chemring in 2021.

## Products and solutions

- **Autonomy and orchestration technology** - real-time autonomous control of multiple UxV assets across tasks and missions.
- **Inexorable Labs** - a service to deliver complex missions jointly with clients.
