# Ingencium

> UK consultancy offering strategy, procurement and maritime advice to defence, government and maritime clients.

## What they do

Ingencium describes itself as a defence and maritime consultancy working with the Ministry of Defence, prime contractors, SMEs and collaborative enterprise programmes. The company states that its advice is commercially grounded, led by a chartered accountant with finance director experience in manufacturing and defence businesses. It works across five practice areas covering strategy, digital services, maritime concept design, clean maritime and defence procurement. Ingencium Ltd was incorporated in April 2026.

## Products and solutions

- **Strategy and transformation** - operating model design, business cases, programme governance and financial planning.
- **Digital services** - digital strategy, technology procurement advice, legacy modernisation and supplier management.
- **Maritime concept design** - early-stage concept development, requirements articulation and commercial viability assessment for maritime platforms.
- **Clean maritime** - decarbonisation strategy, alternative fuels and propulsion, and regulatory compliance advice.
- **Defence procurement and bid support** - ITT responses, teaming strategy, pricing strategy and framework navigation.
