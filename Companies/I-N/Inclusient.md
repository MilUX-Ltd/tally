# Inclusient

> Human factors and occupational psychology consultancy working on the human and technology interface in defence, security and high-risk environments.

## What they do

Inclusient states that it applies human factors and occupational psychology expertise to help organisations optimise performance at the human and technology interface, with a focus on defence, security and high-risk environments. Its areas of expertise are human factors, technology-enhanced learning, and emerging technology and people, covering artificial intelligence, robotics and immersive systems. The company states it has led applied defence research including emotional-sensing trials, personalised learning studies, digital literacy frameworks, decision-support tools and gamified, adaptive and immersive submariner training.

## Products and solutions

- **Human factors** - human factors analysis and advice for people and technology systems.
- **Technology-enhanced learning** - design of individual and team training solutions, including immersive and adaptive methods.
- **Emerging technology and people** - human-machine teaming and people-focused strategy for AI, robotics and immersive systems.
- **Research and innovation leadership** - applied research and experimentation to de-risk future capabilities.
