# MicroLink Devices UK

> Cardiff company making lightweight, flexible gallium arsenide solar cells, solar sheets and high specific energy battery packs for high-altitude aircraft, UAVs and space.

## What they do

MicroLink Devices UK Ltd is the UK operation of MicroLink Devices, which pioneered the scale-up of the epitaxial lift-off (ELO) process for gallium arsenide solar cells. Based at Cardiff Gate Business Park, Cardiff, it supplies ultra-light, flexible multi-junction solar cells and custom solar sheets used on high-altitude long-endurance aircraft, smaller solar-powered UAVs and spacecraft. It also designs and manufactures high specific energy battery packs and integrates complete solar-electric power systems. The company states that its products serve aerospace and defence applications.

## Products and solutions

- **Flexible solar sheets** - custom solar sheets for high-altitude aircraft and UAVs.
- **IMM solar cells** - ultra-thin, flexible inverted metamorphic multi-junction solar cells for space and aerial platforms.
- **Battery packs** - custom high specific energy battery packs for extended UAV endurance.
- **Solar-electric power systems** - integrated solar, power management and storage systems.
- **Engineering services** - contract development of solar cells, solar sheets and battery integration.
