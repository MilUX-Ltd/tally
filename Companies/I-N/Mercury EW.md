# Mercury EW

> Lincolnshire veteran-owned provider of electromagnetic warfare and CEMA training, consultancy and defence training management.

## What they do

Mercury EW Ltd describes itself as a veteran-owned service provider to the defence industry, specialising in electromagnetic warfare (EW) and cyber and electromagnetic activities (CEMA) training, consultancy and advice, and defence training management services. The company was incorporated in April 2009 and states it has collaborated with 33 prime contractors and delivered training and consultancy in 24 countries. Its course portfolio includes land electromagnetic warfare, COMINT and ELINT technical analysis, CEMA awareness, counter-uncrewed aerial systems and mission data engineering. It also runs a cyber academy and provides field service representatives.

## Products and solutions

- **Defence training** - EW and CEMA courses with theoretical and scenario-based training.
- **Outsourced training partner** - training teams provided to support customers' training needs.
- **Consultancy and advice** - impartial EW and CEMA advice.
- **Field service representatives** - domain experts available for global deployment.
- **Defence training management** - services across the Defence Systems Approach to Training.
- **Cyber academy** - cyber training, including a schools programme.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships
