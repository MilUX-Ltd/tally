# Incendia

> Canadian developer of an autonomous multi-sensor hazard detection and alerting system for defence, civil safety and critical infrastructure.

## What they do

Incendia describes itself as a Canadian company developing technology for fire safety, founded in 2020. Its main product, the Pentagon, is a modular, autonomous multi-sensor unit that detects fire, gas, smoke, flooding, extreme temperatures and other hazards and issues localised alerts without needing a network. The company states that the system is designed for dual use and is aimed at defence sites such as military camps, as well as civil safety and critical infrastructure. It reports six patents filed.

## Products and solutions

- **The Pentagon** - autonomous multi-sensor detection and alerting unit that operates without network infrastructure.
- **Defence applications** - protection for military camps, bases and critical buildings.
- **Public and private sector deployments** - protection for communities, residential buildings and commercial operations.
