# Luftmateriell AS

> Luftmateriell supplies avionics and aviation equipment from its base at Torp airport, and has supplied the Norwegian armed forces for over 50 years.

## What they do

Luftmateriell AS was founded in 1940 and has been a supplier to the Norwegian armed forces for more than 50 years. It has civil and military aviation experience and represents partner brands in avionics and aircraft equipment. It is part of Skytec.
