# Masteel

> Lichfield specialist steel supplier of armour steel plate and profiles for defence, alongside pressure vessel, high-strength and stainless steels.

## What they do

Masteel supplies specialist steel plate, profiles and sections from its UK stockholding, with profiling, testing and dished-end services. For defence it supplies armour steel plate and profiles, including steel to DEF STAN 95-24, the standard that replaced MVEE 816. Its other markets include offshore, energy, shipbuilding, construction and engineering.

## Products and solutions

- **Armour steel plate and profiles** - armour plate, including DEF STAN 95-24 grades, cut to profile.
- **Specialist steels** - pressure vessel, chrome moly, high-strength low-alloy, weathering, heat-resistant and stainless steels.
- **Services** - profiling, testing, dished ends and worldwide shipping from UK stock.
