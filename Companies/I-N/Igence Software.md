# Igence Software

> East Grinstead software engineering company developing mission-critical software and AI for defence, transport, process control and autonomous systems.

## What they do

Igence Software Ltd states that it engineers mission-critical software and AI solutions for defence, transport, process control and autonomous environments. Its published services are team augmentation, systems integration, SC-cleared engineers, and applied AI and autonomy. The company is registered in England and Wales and operates from Scandia-Hus Business Park near East Grinstead. It was incorporated in 1999 as Igence Limited and adopted its current name in 2008.

## Products and solutions

- **Team augmentation** - software engineers placed into customer teams.
- **Systems integration** - integration of mission-critical software systems.
- **SC-cleared engineers** - security-cleared engineering staff for defence work.
- **Applied AI and autonomy** - AI and autonomy software for autonomous environments.
