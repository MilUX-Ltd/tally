# myprintpod

> Hampshire 3D printing service for rapid prototyping and low-volume manufacturing using recycled materials.

## What they do

MyPrintPod is a trading name of Augmented Solutions Limited, based in Southwick near Fareham, Hampshire. It provides a UK 3D printing service for rapid prototyping, design for additive manufacturing and low-volume production for start-ups, SMEs, researchers and innovators. The company states that it uses recycled materials and calibrated systems with per-part carbon dioxide equivalent tracking, and offers material recovery from old parts.

## Products and solutions

- **Co-creation lab** - rapid prototyping, CAD modelling and design for additive manufacturing.
- **Manufacturing as a service** - single units to small batch production.
- **Circular manufacturing** - recycled material inputs and end-of-life material recovery.
