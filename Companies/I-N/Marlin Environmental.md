# Marlin Environmental

> Portsmouth-based environmental and engineering services provider for maritime, defence and commercial clients.

## What they do

Marlin Environmental provides engineering, maintenance, technical cleaning and waste management services, with its head office in Farlington, Portsmouth. The company states it serves maritime, defence and commercial customers, works with the Ministry of Defence and major defence contractors, and has supported more than 24 Royal Navy vessels. It describes its offer as end-to-end support from engineering through to waste disposal, and also provides safety training.

## Products and solutions

- **Engineering and maintenance** - pumps and valves, fuel systems, vacuum waste systems and ballast water treatment systems.
- **Specialist technical cleaning** - technical and vent cleaning.
- **Waste management and drainage** - waste handling and disposal services.
- **Confined space support** - Authorised Persons and confined space rescue teams.
- **Safety training** - training delivered as an IOSH approved training provider.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Accreditations:** Cyber Essentials; JOSCAR; Achilles; IOSH approved training provider.
- **Memberships:** South Central RDSC
