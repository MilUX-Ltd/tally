# i2 Group

> Cambridge-based provider of link analysis and visual intelligence software, including i2 Analyst's Notebook, part of Harris Computer.

## What they do

i2 Group provides intelligence analysis software used to create and share intelligence, including i2 Analyst's Notebook and iBase. The company states it has worked with analysts and investigators for over 30 years, was part of IBM's security portfolio for more than a decade, and became part of Harris Computer, an operating company of Constellation Software Inc., in January 2022. It serves defence, law enforcement, corrections, national security, government and financial services customers, and states it is a partner to the US Department of Defense, the UK Ministry of Defence and NATO forces. Its UK office is in Cambridge.

## Products and solutions

- **i2 Analyst's Notebook** - link analysis and visual intelligence software, offered in Core, Collaborate, Connect and Pro tiers.
- **iBase** - intelligence database and analysis application.
- **Analysis Hub and Analysis Studio** - collaborative intelligence analysis products.
- **i2 add-ons** - Overwatch, Insights, Explore and TextChart.
- **Services** - professional services, data connectors and training.
