# JDR Products

> Nuneaton manufacturer of aluminium tube components, fabrications and sub-assemblies, including components for aerospace and defence.

## What they do

JDR Products Limited states it has manufactured aluminium tube in Britain since 1980. It supplies individual components, part assemblies and full sub-assemblies, with services covering forming and fabrication, cutting and machining, assembly and finishing, plastic mouldings and castings, and design and engineering support. It serves sectors including aerospace and defence, agriculture, medical equipment, construction, sports and leisure, architecture and contract furniture, and states that it is JOSCAR-accredited for aerospace and defence supply chains. Its in-house brands include TruDomes and RapidGB rapidly deployable shelters.

## Products and solutions

- **Forming and fabrication** - CNC tube bending, tube end forming and welding in aluminium and steel.
- **Cutting and machining** - tube cutting, laser cutting and multi-hole drilling.
- **Assembly and finishing** - assemblies with polishing, anodising and powder coating.
- **Plastic mouldings and castings** - moulded and cast parts.
- **Design and engineering support** - support from prototype to production.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Accreditations:** ISO 9001; ISO 14001; JOSCAR.
- **Memberships:** South Central RDSC; Made in Britain.
