# M5tec

> Newton Aycliffe engineering company providing design, embedded engineers and turnkey engineered solutions to high assurance industries.

## What they do

M5tec Limited is an engineering company at Aycliffe Business Park in County Durham, incorporated in 2014. The company states it provides engineered solutions to high assurance industries, with expertise in remotely operated engineered solutions for hazardous environments. Its stated sectors are nuclear, fusion energy and subsea, with offshore and renewables work also described.

## Products and solutions

- **Engineering design and consultancy** - mechanical design and analysis for high assurance applications.
- **Embedded engineering resource** - engineers working within client teams.
- **Turnkey services** - design through to delivery, using an on-site workshop and manufacturing partners.
- **Remote handling solutions** - remotely operated equipment for hazardous environments.
