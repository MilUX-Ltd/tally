# Lincoln College

> Further and higher education college in Lincoln, part of the Lincoln College Group with Newark College and the Air & Space Institute.

## What they do

Lincoln College is a further education college based on Monks Road in Lincoln. It offers courses for school leavers, adults, apprentices and international students, and runs the Lincoln College University Centre for degree-level study. The Lincoln College Group also includes Newark College and the Air & Space Institute in Newark, which offers further and higher education. The college runs commercial training settings, including a restaurant, a salon and an arts venue, where students train in live environments.

## Products and solutions

- **Further education** - Level 1 to Level 3 courses for school leavers and adults.
- **Apprenticeships** - apprenticeship training.
- **Lincoln College University Centre** - higher education and career-focused degree courses.
- **Air & Space Institute** - further and higher education provision at its Newark site.
- **Online learning** - online courses.
