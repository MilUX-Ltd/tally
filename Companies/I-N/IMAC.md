# IMAC

> Coventry engineering contractor providing mechanical, electrical, instrumentation, control and automation, security, civil and fabrication services.

## What they do

Installation Maintenance and Controls Limited (IMAC) provides MEICA (mechanical, electrical, instrumentation, control and automation), security, civil engineering and fabrication services, with turnkey delivery of new builds and refurbishments. Its sectors include water and wastewater, nuclear, industrial, manufacturing, transport and maritime, utilities, and custodial, judicial and defence. The company states that it provides physical security and building services to UK government departments and agencies to protect key buildings and critical infrastructure. It is based at Binley Business Park in Coventry.

## Products and solutions

- **MEICA services** - mechanical, electrical, instrumentation, control and automation installation.
- **Security** - physical security, surveillance, access control and alarm systems.
- **Civil engineering** - site development, infrastructure and structural engineering, and project management.
- **Fabrication** - precision fabrication.
- **Design and consultancy** - bespoke design solutions.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Accreditations:** ISO 9001; ISO 14001; ISO 27001; ISO 45001; Cyber Essentials; NICEIC; CompEx; Constructionline; SSAIB; SSIP (shown on its website).
- **Memberships:** South Central RDSC
