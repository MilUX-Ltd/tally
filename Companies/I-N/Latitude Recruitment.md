# Latitude Recruitment

> Hampshire recruitment agency specialising in engineering and manufacturing roles.

## What they do

Latitude Recruitment Ltd describes itself as a specialist engineering and manufacturing recruitment agency established in 2019, placing staff across Hampshire, Dorset and West Sussex. It recruits for permanent, temporary and contract roles, including skilled manufacturing trades such as assemblers, mechanical fitters, welders and machinists, and engineering and quality positions. It is based at Hedge End Business Centre near Southampton and also covers the Portsmouth area.

## Products and solutions

- **Permanent recruitment** - engineering, manufacturing and quality roles.
- **Temporary and contract recruitment** - skilled trades and manufacturing staff.
