# Netcompany UK

> UK arm of Netcompany Group, delivering IT consultancy, cloud migration and digital platforms for government, including a defence and resilience platform called VERA.

## What they do

Netcompany UK Limited is the UK company of Netcompany Group A/S, an international IT services business. The company states that it works with UK public sector customers including the NHS, National Highways and the Ministry of Defence. Public procurement notices record Netcompany UK supporting the migration of Ministry of Defence applications to MODCloud, and a hosting-environment support contract for the P-8A Delivery Team running from May 2025 to January 2027.

## Products and solutions

- **VERA** - defence and resilience platform that the company states provides orchestration across military and civil domains, situational awareness and a digital twin of the defence and critical national infrastructure, built partly on the company's Pulse platform.
- **Cloud migration and hosting support** - migration of applications to MODCloud and application patching and monitoring support for Azure-based hosting.
- **Digital platforms for government** - citizen-facing and critical infrastructure systems for UK public sector customers.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Frameworks:** G-Cloud 15 (VERA listed on the Digital Marketplace)
