# LESS AS

> Develops and supplies stretchers, evacuation and emergency preparedness equipment, including CBRNE and triage products.

## What they do

LESS AS develops and supplies stretchers, evacuation equipment and emergency preparedness systems, plus hypothermia protection, triage materials and decontamination equipment. The company says its products were developed with input from the Norwegian Armed Forces and Civil Defence.

## Products and solutions

- **LESS PRO** - Stretcher made of recycled plastic.
- **ROFI Rapid Rescue** - Treatment unit.
- **LESS Redningsenhet** - Trailer convertible to a casualty collection point.
