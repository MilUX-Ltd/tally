# Made Tech

> London digital, data and technology consultancy for UK public sector organisations, including defence and space.

## What they do

Made Tech Limited is a digital, data and technology services company and the operating subsidiary of Made Tech Group plc, which is quoted on the London Stock Exchange AIM market. The company states it helps public sector organisations modernise technology and accelerate digital delivery, and that it serves central government, health, local government, housing, national security and public safety, defence and space, transport and education. For defence and space clients it states it provides data platforms, secure AI and machine learning pipelines, cyber security support and legacy modernisation, and that 90% of its delivery team hold Security Check clearance.

## Products and solutions

- **Data and AI** - data engineering, data platforms and machine learning.
- **Digital transformation** - digital service delivery and legacy transformation.
- **Cloud and engineering** - cloud engineering and software delivery.
- **Managed services** - support of live digital and data services, applications and cloud infrastructure.
- **User-centred design** - service design and research.
- **Made Tech Housing** - specialist products for social housing providers.
