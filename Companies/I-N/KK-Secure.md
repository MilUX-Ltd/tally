# KK-Secure

> Lincoln cyber security firm providing 24/7 monitoring, managed detection and response, incident response and threat intelligence.

## What they do

KK-Secure describes itself as a UK cyber operations and intelligence firm founded in 2016, providing 24/7 monitoring, managed detection and response, incident response, threat intelligence and compliance services. It operates a Cyber Intelligence Centre at the Alchemy Building on Lincoln Science and Innovation Park, opened in 2020. It offers CyberShadows, an AI-enabled endpoint threat intelligence platform for endpoints, IoT and OT. The company states it has military intelligence experience, has signed the Armed Forces Covenant and holds the Employer Recognition Scheme Gold Award.

## Products and solutions

- **Managed detection and response** - 24/7 monitoring and response from its UK Cyber Intelligence Centre.
- **CyberShadows** - AI-enabled, logless endpoint threat intelligence platform for endpoints, IoT and OT.
- **Incident response** - containment and recovery services.
- **Advisory, GRC and testing** - ISO 27001 and NIS2 compliance guidance, risk management and security testing.
- **Supply chain risk** - monitoring of partners for vulnerabilities.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Accreditations:** ISO 27001 (certified 2023); Cyber Essentials. Armed Forces Covenant Employer Recognition Scheme Gold Award (2022).
- **Memberships:** Greater Lincolnshire RDSC
