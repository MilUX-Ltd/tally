# James Cowper Kreston

> Thames Valley firm of accountants and business advisers with a defence and aerospace sector practice.

## What they do

James Cowper Kreston is the trading name of James Cowper LLP, a firm of accountants and business advisers founded in 1922, with offices in London, Newbury, Oxford, Reading and Southampton. Its services cover accounts and business advisory, audit and assurance, corporate finance, tax, payroll, outsourcing and restructuring. It lists defence and aerospace as a sector specialism and states that it advises defence and aerospace suppliers, engineering and advanced manufacturing businesses and technology companies on tax and innovation reliefs, funding, financial controls and growth. The firm is a member of the Kreston network.

## Products and solutions

- **Audit and assurance** - statutory audit and assurance services.
- **Tax** - business, employment, international and indirect tax, private client tax, and R&D and Patent Box relief claims.
- **Corporate finance** - support for funding, acquisitions, investment and exit.
- **Accounts and business advisory** - accounts preparation, financial controls and growth planning.
- **Outsourcing and payroll** - outsourced finance and payroll services.
- **Restructuring and insolvency** - turnaround, restructuring and insolvency services.
