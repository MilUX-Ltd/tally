# M Group

> UK and Ireland infrastructure services group serving energy, water, communications, transport, and defence, industrial and public sector markets.

## What they do

M Group describes itself as an infrastructure services provider for organisations in the UK and Ireland, working across energy, water, technology and communications, transport, and defence, industrial and public sector markets. Its transport market includes rail and aviation, and defence is listed as a market within its defence, industrial and public sector business. The group states it has more than 14,000 people and over 300 locations. This page covers the group as a whole, including its rail and aviation business.

## Products and solutions

- **Capital projects** - delivery of infrastructure projects.
- **Repair and maintenance** - maintenance of operational infrastructure.
- **Digital solutions and data insights** - digital and data services for infrastructure owners.
- **Technical services and decarbonisation** - technical and energy transition services.
- **Cyber** - cyber services listed among its capabilities.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships
