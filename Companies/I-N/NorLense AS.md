# NorLense AS

> NorLense makes oil spill recovery equipment and high pressure inflatable tents for military, humanitarian and emergency use.

## What they do

NorLense AS, founded in 1975, makes oil spill recovery systems and inflatable shelters. Its SWIFT tent was developed with the Norwegian Army and is used in military operations.

## Products and solutions

- **SWIFT Tent** - Rapid modular inflatable tent system
