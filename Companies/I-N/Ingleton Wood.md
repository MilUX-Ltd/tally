# Ingleton Wood

> Multidisciplinary property and construction consultancy with offices across the East of England, London, Oxford and Nottingham, working on defence and secure estates.

## What they do

Ingleton Wood is a multidisciplinary property and construction consultancy providing architecture, engineering, surveying, planning and project management across the project lifecycle. The practice states that its security-cleared teams support defence and secure estates through frameworks, direct appointments and Tier 1 supply chains, with experience of technical facilities, single living accommodation, explosives and fuel installations, and estate decarbonisation. It traces its history to the 1930s and reports more than 250 employees from 2023. It has offices in Billericay, Cambridge, Colchester, London, Norwich, Nottingham and Oxford.

## Products and solutions

- **Architecture and interior design** - new build, refurbishment and masterplanning.
- **Building services engineering** - mechanical and electrical design.
- **Civil and structural engineering** - structural and civil design.
- **Building surveying and quantity surveying** - surveys and cost management.
- **Project management and CDM** - project management, CDM, building safety and health and safety services.
- **Climate and sustainability** - decarbonisation and low-carbon design.
