# Lindum Group

> Lincoln-based, employee and family-owned construction and property group working across Lincolnshire, Yorkshire and the East Midlands.

## What they do

Lindum Group Limited is a construction and property company founded in Lincoln in 1956, with offices in North Hykeham (Lincoln), York and Peterborough. It states that it has been family and employee-owned since 1994, with 500 of its 600 employees holding shares. Its services include construction, maintenance, roofing and cladding, joint ventures and land, plant hire, waste recycling, new homes, business space and bespoke joinery. The company is appointed to public sector construction frameworks including SCAPE and Pagabo.

## Products and solutions

- **Construction** - new build, refurbishment and design and build projects.
- **Maintenance** - building maintenance services.
- **Roofing and cladding** - roofing and cladding contracting.
- **Plant hire and fleet maintenance** - plant hire services.
- **Waste recycling and skip hire** - through Lindum Waste.
- **New homes and business space** - housebuilding and commercial space to let.
- **Bespoke joinery** - through Lindum Joinery.
