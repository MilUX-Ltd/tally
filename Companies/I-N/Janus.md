# Janus

> Norwegian maker of merino wool clothing, supplying base layers to the Norwegian Armed Forces and other customers.

## What they do

Janus manufactures merino wool base layers and other wool clothing in Norway. Its site announces a multi-year contract to supply base layers to Forsvarsmateriell, the procurement body for the Norwegian Armed Forces.

## Products and solutions

- **Merino wool base layers** - Underwear and mid-layers, including for armed forces.
