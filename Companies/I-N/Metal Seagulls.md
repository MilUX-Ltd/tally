# Metal Seagulls

> South West Wales light aviation company representing aero engine, propeller and aircraft kit manufacturers and supplying parts and engineering support.

## What they do

Metal Seagulls Ltd describes itself as a UK company in non-Part 21 aviation. It represents ULPower aero engines, DUC Helices propellers, Zenair aircraft kits and Fafali aircraft for the UK, Ireland, Iceland, the Falkland Islands and Africa, and supplies specialist parts and services internationally through an online shop. It states that it works with several OEMs on powerplant and energy source solutions and has hands-on experience in light aircraft build, maintenance, adaptation and operation. The company states it is based in South West Wales.

## Products and solutions

- **ULPower aero engines** - distribution of engines, spares and repairs.
- **DUC Helices propellers** - propeller supply.
- **Zenair aircraft kits** - two-seat STOL and cruising airframe kits.
- **Powerplant solutions** - work with OEMs on powerplant and energy source developments.
