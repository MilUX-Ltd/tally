# My Sales Agent

> Fareham marine brand sales agency representing safety and clothing brands to UK trade buyers, including military buyers.

## What they do

My Sales Agent Limited is a UK marine brand sales agency representing seven brands across leisure marine, commercial marine and professional markets, selling to trade accounts only. It states it provides sales representation, technical product advice, visits and training, and one point of contact for its brands. Its professional market includes the public sector, military, training organisations and marine search and rescue.

## Products and solutions

- **Brand representation** - UK trade sales for Musto, Helly Hansen, sMRT, Eflare, Mullion, Ursuit and Shock WBV.
- **Technical advice** - product selection advice for craft, crews and customers.
- **Market development** - support for brands entering new UK and international territories.
