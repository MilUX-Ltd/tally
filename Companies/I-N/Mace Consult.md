# Mace Consult

> International programme and project management consultancy, carved out of Mace Group in 2026 with majority investment from Goldman Sachs Alternatives.

## What they do

Mace Consult is a programme and project delivery consultancy that operates under the Mace brand. It launched as a standalone company on 5 March 2026, after Goldman Sachs Alternatives completed a majority private equity investment and its carve-out from Mace Group; the business retained the Mace brand. The company states it has more than 5,500 professionals across six continents and generated close to US$1 billion in revenue in 2025. The Mace website lists defence and national security among its markets, with defence, cyber and space work for government and industry clients.

## Products and solutions

- **Programme and project management** - delivery of programmes and projects on time, on budget and on scope.
- **Defence and national security** - consultancy for defence and national security infrastructure and programmes.
