# IN2

> High Wycombe-registered strategic communications and behavioural change consultancy providing information environment analysis.

## What they do

IN2 describes itself as a research-led strategic communications and behavioural change company, providing information environment analysis and cognitive advantage for organisations operating in complex or rapidly changing geopolitical, security, economic and reputational environments. It lists governments and defence and security organisations among the sectors it supports, alongside academia, development organisations and commercial enterprises. Its stated areas of work include campaign design, behavioural insight, research and analysis, countering disinformation, media production, translation in more than 20 languages, and training; it lists offices in London, Warsaw, Kyiv, Baghdad, Erbil, Beirut, Delhi, Dubai and Nairobi.

## Products and solutions

- **Omnicat** - IN2's AI tool for multilingual sentiment analysis of audience feedback, including regional Arabic dialects.
- **IN2 MENA Pulse** - a subscription decision-intelligence briefing.
