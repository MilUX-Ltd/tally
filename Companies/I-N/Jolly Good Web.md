# Jolly Good Web

> Lincoln web and digital agency building Webflow websites and providing digital marketing, brand design and support, including for defence organisations and suppliers.

## What they do

Jolly Good Web is a web and digital agency based in Lincoln, established in 2020, that builds websites and web apps on Webflow and provides SEO, pay-per-click and channel marketing, brand and UX design, and ongoing support. It states it is a Webflow Premium Partner and a Google Partner and has a team of ten. Its defence and security sector page states that it works with military organisations, service charities and associations, and suppliers to defence, that its founder is a Royal Air Force veteran, and that it is an Armed Forces Covenant signatory and web partner to the Greater Lincolnshire defence and security cluster.

## Products and solutions

- **Website projects** - Webflow websites and web apps.
- **Digital marketing** - SEO, PPC and channel marketing, including campaigns aimed at defence procurement audiences.
- **Brand, print and UX design** - brand identity, UI, pitch decks and exhibition graphics.
- **Ongoing support** - website maintenance, security and continued marketing support.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Accreditations:** Armed Forces Covenant signatory; Webflow Premium Partner; Google Partner.
- **Memberships:** Greater Lincolnshire RDSC
