# Insphere

> Bristol developer of metrology sensing and robot control software that improves the accuracy of industrial robots in manufacturing.

## What they do

Insphere develops metrology sensor technology and software for intelligent automation in smart factories, aimed at making robotic manufacturing more accurate and flexible. Its applications include robot machining, additive manufacturing, drilling, assembly and precision welding. The company lists defence among its target industries, alongside aerospace, automotive, composites and nuclear, and states that its sensing technology can make robotic solutions more flexible for low-volume, frequently changing defence products. It is based at the Bristol and Bath Science Park.

## Products and solutions

- **IONA** - robot tracking and guidance system.
- **ORA** - robot control software.
- **Renishaw RCS series** - robot diagnostics, calibration and probing.
- **Calibration and consultancy** - robot performance services.
