# Noyce Insurance Solutions

> Port Solent commercial insurance broker serving businesses in the South of England.

## What they do

Noyce Insurance Solutions Ltd is a commercial insurance broker authorised and regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority. The company states that it specialises in providing tailored insurance protection for businesses in the South of England with complex insurance needs, with access to major insurance markets including Lloyd's. It covers sectors including aviation, construction, digital, engineering, manufacturing, marine, professionals and transport. Its office is at Port Solent in Portsmouth.

## Products and solutions

- **Asset protection** - cover for business assets, including goods in transit.
- **Cyber protection** - cyber insurance.
- **Employee protection** - cover relating to staff.
- **Legal liability protection** - liability insurance.
- **Motor vehicle protection** - motor cover.
- **Revenue protection** - cover to protect business revenue.
