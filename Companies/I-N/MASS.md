# MASS

> Cohort plc company providing electronic warfare and CEMA services, synthetic test and evaluation, secure software, exercise delivery and training for defence and government.

## What they do

MASS describes itself as a technology company specialising in highly secure software, services and training for defence, government and business, and has been part of Cohort plc since 2006. Its defence work covers electronic warfare and cyber and electromagnetic activities (CEMA) support and training, mission data management, countermeasures development, mission planning, and synthetic test and evaluation. It also delivers exercise support for operational headquarters, software development, IT managed services, a lessons support service and Cyber Essentials certification as an IASME certification body. Its head office is in St Neots, with offices in Lincoln, where it runs a training academy, and Chippenham.

## Products and solutions

- **EW and CEMA services** - mission data management, countermeasures development, mission planning and expert manpower provision.
- **EW and CEMA training** - DSAT-compliant electronic warfare training courses.
- **Synthetic test and evaluation** - multi-domain synthetic testing using digital twin technology.
- **Exercise delivery** - operational HQ exercise support, strategic analysis and decision-making exercises, and individual and team training.
- **Software development and IT managed services** - secure software, data services and IT service delivery.
- **Cyber Essentials** - certification services as an IASME registered certification body.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Accreditations:** ISO 9001:2015; ISO 27001:2022; JOSCAR; Cyber Essentials; Defence Employer Recognition Scheme Silver award.
- **Memberships:** Greater Lincolnshire RDSC; Tech Talent Charter signatory; Armed Forces Covenant signatory.
