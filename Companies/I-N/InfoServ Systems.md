# InfoServ Systems

> Brockworth software engineering company building secure, mission-critical systems for national security and defence.

## What they do

InfoServ Systems delivers secure software systems across the national security and defence sectors, with a stated mission of keeping clients at the forefront of signal processing and analysis. Its engineering work covers ultra-low-latency C, C++ and Rust development, including moving legacy C++ codebases to Rust, researching new capabilities and optimising existing systems. It also runs a partner network serving defence, security and commercial customers.
