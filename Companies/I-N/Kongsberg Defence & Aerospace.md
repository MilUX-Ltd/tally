# Kongsberg Defence & Aerospace

> Develops defence, aerospace, maritime and surveillance systems including air defence, missiles, naval and space systems.

## What they do

Kongsberg Defence & Aerospace develops and supplies defence and security systems to armed forces. Its work covers air defence, missiles, maritime systems, uncrewed vessels, and space-based communication, navigation and surveillance.

## Products and solutions

- **NASAMS** - Integrated air defence system.
- **HUGIN** - Family of undersea vehicles for monitoring subsea infrastructure.
- **Vanguard** - Standardised vessels for maritime monitoring, protection and security.
- **Space for Defence** - Space-based communication, navigation and surveillance.
