# LGR Technologies

> Defence technology company building modular tactical sensor systems for perimeter detection and situational awareness.

## What they do

LGR Technologies builds rugged, modular sensor systems that detect events and alert operators, who make the final decision. The company is running pilot programmes with defence and security organisations. Its systems can be bought, rented or trialled.

## Products and solutions

- **MLTS** - Mobile Lightweight Tactical Sensor for perimeter defence.
- **Munin** - Concept linking sensor alerts to drone thermal imagery, in development.
