# Kry10

> Formally verified security products for defence and critical national infrastructure, built on the seL4 microkernel; UK office in Gloucester.

## What they do

Kry10 builds high-assurance security products on Kry10 OS (KOS), an operating system based on the formally verified seL4 microkernel, which rules out whole classes of vulnerability by mathematical proof. Its products include guard appliances that protect in-service operational technology without modifying or re-certifying it, and secure management of AI agents and workloads on sensitive systems. Kry10 UK Ltd is the UK arm of a company founded in New Zealand, with teams in the USA and Australia; in 2026 ARIA funded it to deliver CNI Guardian under the Safeguarded AI programme.

## Products and solutions

- **CNI Guardian and OT Network Guard** - inline guard appliances giving provable protection in front of certified operational technology.
- **Trusted Workload Management** - isolated, evidenced management of AI agents and workloads on sensitive systems, including a private AI product and a secure sandbox.
- **KOS SDK** - the seL4-based operating system for organisations building their own high-assurance devices.
