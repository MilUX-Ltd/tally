# Kry10

> Formally verified security products for defence and critical national infrastructure, built on the seL4 microkernel; US-owned, with a UK office in Gloucester.

## What they do

Kry10 builds high-assurance security products on Kry10 OS (KOS), an operating system based on the formally verified seL4 microkernel, which rules out whole classes of vulnerability by mathematical proof. Its products include OT Network Guard, a guard appliance that protects in-service operational technology without modifying or re-certifying it, and secure management of AI agents and workloads on sensitive systems. Kry10 is US-owned. It was founded in New Zealand in 2020 and has teams in the USA, Australia and the UK, where Kry10 UK Ltd was incorporated in 2025. In 2026 ARIA funded its CNI Guardian research project under the Safeguarded AI programme.

## Products and solutions

- **OT Network Guard** - an inline guard appliance giving provable protection in front of certified operational technology.
- **Trusted Workload Management** - isolated, evidenced management of AI agents and workloads on sensitive systems, including a private AI product and a secure sandbox.
- **KOS SDK** - the seL4-based operating system for organisations building their own high-assurance devices.
