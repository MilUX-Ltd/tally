# KSAT - Kongsberg Satellite Services

> Satellite ground network and Earth observation services, including maritime surveillance and security.

## What they do

KSAT provides ground network services for satellite data acquisition and operations, and Earth observation services. These include satellite imagery and maritime surveillance and security, covering maritime situational awareness and tracking vessels from space.

## Products and solutions

- **KSATmax** - Data acquisition and TT&C solutions.
- **KSATlite** - Ground network for small satellites and constellations.
- **Maritime Surveillance & Security** - Maritime situational awareness from satellite data.
