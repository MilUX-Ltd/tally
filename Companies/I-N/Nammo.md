# Nammo

> Norway-headquartered aerospace and defence company making specialty ammunition and rocket motors.

## What they do

Nammo is an international aerospace and defence company headquartered in Norway. It makes specialty ammunition and rocket motors for governments. Its product areas are propulsion, ammunition, grenades, warheads and energetics, shoulder fired systems, and space.

## Products and solutions

- **Propulsion** - Rocket motors.
- **Ammunition** - Specialty ammunition.
- **Grenades, warheads and energetics** - Energetic products.
- **Shoulder fired systems** - Shoulder-launched weapon systems.
- **Space** - Space propulsion products.
