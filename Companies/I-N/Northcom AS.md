# Northcom AS

> Supplier of critical communication solutions, including radio, infrastructure and antennas, to emergency services, the armed forces and industry.

## What they do

Northcom describes itself as a total supplier of critical communication solutions. Its markets are emergency services, the Armed Forces, oil and gas, and energy and shipping. It supplies radio systems, accessories, infrastructure, antennas and SD-WAN solutions.

## Products and solutions

- **Radio** - TETRA, DMR, analogue, marine, satellite and LTE radio.
- **Infrastructure** - Base stations and accessories.
- **Antennas** - Antennas, filters and cabling.
- **SD-WAN** - Network connectivity solutions.
