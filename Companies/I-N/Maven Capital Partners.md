# Maven Capital Partners

> UK private equity and property investment manager backing SMEs through buyouts, growth capital, VCTs and regional funds, with an office in Reading.

## What they do

Maven Capital Partners describes itself as a private equity and property manager with over 120 executives across a UK office network, providing equity funding of up to 20 million pounds in a single transaction for management buyouts and growth capital. It states that it was formed in 2009 through a management buyout of the private equity business of Aberdeen Asset Management and was acquired by Mattioli Woods Limited in 2021. It manages venture capital trusts, EIS products, private investor co-investment and regional growth funds. Its Reading office invests across Reading, Oxford and the south of England, and the firm cites defence among the growth sectors of the Thames Valley region.

## Products and solutions

- **Private equity** - management buyouts and growth capital for SMEs.
- **Regional growth funds** - debt and equity funds including Midlands Engine Investment Fund II, Northern Powerhouse Investment Fund II, South West Investment Fund and South East Investment Fund mandates.
- **Venture capital trusts and EIS** - tax-efficient investment products for retail investors.
- **Maven Investor Partners** - deal-by-deal private equity and property co-investment.
- **Property investment** - property investment opportunities for investors.
