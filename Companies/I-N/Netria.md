# Netria

> Lincoln developer of a privacy-first AI product suite covering private AI deployment, cyber and supplier risk, and secure chat.

## What they do

Netria describes itself as a privacy-first private AI platform that brings together secure AI, cyber risk, compliance and private deployment in one product suite. Its flagship, Netria Signal, is described as a risk engine connecting cyber, human, governance, AI and supplier risk. The company states that its services can run on hybrid, Netria-operated or self-hosted private nodes with end-to-end encryption, and that customer data is not used for model training. It targets regulated sectors including finance, healthcare, legal and government, and is based at the Sparkhouse in Lincoln.

## Products and solutions

- **Netria Signal** - risk engine for cyber, human, governance, AI and supplier risk.
- **Netria Private AI** - dedicated private deployments for teams with sovereignty requirements.
- **Netria Code** - software discovery and assurance on an in-infrastructure appliance or a Netria node.
- **Netria SAR Shield** - support for subject access request workflows with privacy controls.
- **Netria Chat** - end-to-end encrypted AI assistance.
- **Development services** - delivery support for secure AI implementation.
