# MarineGuard

> Southampton maritime and infrastructure security company designing and integrating detection, surveillance and response systems around its own C2 platform.

## What they do

MarineGuard Systems Ltd designs, engineers and integrates security systems for ports and terminals, refineries and industrial sites, commercial shipping and offshore assets, and superyachts. The company states it was founded in 1998 and builds its own command and control (C2) software in house, bringing radar, AIS, sonar, cameras, alarms and drone tracks onto a single map. Its systems combine long range radar, thermal cameras, diver detection sonar, RF and radar drone detection and disruption, and access control. The company states its systems are engineered to defence standards including SAPIENT (BSI Flex 335).

## Products and solutions

- **C2 platform** - in-house command and control software presenting all sensors on one map.
- **Perimeter surveillance** - long range radar, thermal cameras and AIS.
- **Diver detection** - 360 degree diver detection sonar beneath the waterline.
- **Drone defence** - RF and radar drone detection and disruption.
- **Access control** - biometric, card, Bluetooth and keypad access control.
- **Lifecycle services** - survey and risk assessment, design, installation, training and maintenance.
