# Meritus Talent

> Cardiff-based recruitment agency for the aerospace, defence and space sectors, specialising in security-cleared candidates.

## What they do

Meritus Talent is a specialist recruitment agency supplying permanent, contract and executive hires to aerospace, defence and space organisations. It states that it maintains an off-market database of security-cleared candidates and has consultants with experience of government clearance processes. The company was incorporated in 2024, has its head office at Anchor Court in Cardiff, and trades as a brand of the Recolution Group.

## Products and solutions

- **Permanent and contract recruitment** - for aerospace, defence and space employers in the UK and USA.
- **Executive search** - senior hires in the sector.
- **Talent solutions** - recruitment process outsourcing, talent-on-demand and embedded growth partner services.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Accreditations:** ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and ISO 27001, stated as held across the Recolution Group (October 2026).
- **Memberships:** Defence Wales; Aerospace Wales Forum directory listing.
