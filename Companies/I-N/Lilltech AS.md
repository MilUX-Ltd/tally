# Lilltech AS

> Family-owned supplier of CBRNe, detection, EOD, forensic and tactical equipment to defence, police and security customers in Norway and Sweden.

## What they do

Lilltech has sold equipment in Norway and Sweden since 1994. Its customers include defence, police, prison authorities and security organisations. The range covers CBRNe, detection, EOD and counter-IED, forensics, tactical equipment and protection.

## Products and solutions

- **CBRNe** - Protective suits and detection equipment.
- **EOD/CIED** - Explosive ordnance disposal and counter-IED equipment.
- **Detection** - Sensors and detection equipment.
