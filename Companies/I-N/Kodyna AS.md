# Kodyna AS

> Kodyna is a Kongsberg-based engineering company developing drivetrain, electric motor and actuator systems, including an ammunition handling system.

## What they do

Kodyna takes on multi-disciplinary development work for demanding customers. Its site reports a series manufacturing contract from Kongsberg Defence for an ammunition system. It also names customers in automotive, maritime, space and other sectors.

## Products and solutions

- **Ammo system** - Ammunition handling system produced in series for Kongsberg Defence
