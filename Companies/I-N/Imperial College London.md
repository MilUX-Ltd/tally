# Imperial College London

> Research university based at South Kensington, London, focused on science, engineering, medicine and business.

## What they do

Imperial College London is a university with its main campus at South Kensington in London, teaching and researching across science, engineering, medicine and business. It works with industry through its business engagement services and its consultancy arm, Imperial Consultants. The college's Institute for Security Science and Technology, which carried out security and resilience research, education and innovation, closed in January 2026 according to the college's website.

## Products and solutions

- **Research and education** - research and degree programmes across science, engineering, medicine and business.
- **Imperial Consultants** - consultancy access to the college's academic expertise and facilities.
- **Imperial for business** - routes for companies to work with the college.
