# MN Consultants

> Basingstoke manufacturing consultancy covering Lean, quality, OEE, ERP and automation.

## What they do

MN Consultants describes itself as a UK manufacturing consultancy based in Basingstoke, Hampshire, serving manufacturers across England and Wales and led by a chartered engineer. It runs operations assessments to find recoverable cost and capacity, then provides Lean, quality and overall equipment effectiveness specialists, and delivers ERP, MRP and automation projects. Sectors it lists include aerospace and defence supply, automotive, healthcare and NHS supply, electronics and plastics.

## Products and solutions

- **Manufacturing operations assessment** - floor-level diagnostic of cost and capacity losses.
- **Lean and OEE improvement** - standard work, flow and equipment effectiveness programmes.
- **Quality and cost reduction** - FMEA, error-proofing and waste removal.
- **ERP and MRP implementation** - system selection, design, delivery and rescue of stalled projects.
- **Automation and robotics** - cobots, test rigs and automated assembly.
- **Interim operations leadership** - senior operations support for transitions and acquisitions.
