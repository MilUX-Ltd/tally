# Metris Leadership

> Brockhampton consultancy for leadership development, team performance and culture design in high-growth businesses.

## What they do

Metris is a specialist consultancy working with high-growth businesses, investors and executive teams on leadership, teamwork and culture. It embeds with a client to design and deliver an individual programme covering leadership capacity, teamwork skills and cultural alignment. The company states that its roots are in the ethos of UK Special Forces and in other sectors that solve novel problems in complex, high-consequence environments.
