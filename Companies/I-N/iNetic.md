# iNetic

> Andover designer and manufacturer of electric motors for automotive, industrial, aerospace and exploration applications.

## What they do

iNetic Limited designs and manufactures electric motors at its site on the East Portway Industrial Estate in Andover. The company states that it began in 1997 as S&S Winding, a stator manufacturer, took the iNetic identity after merging with an engine design business in 2008, and merged with Moto Optima in 2022. It offers custom motor design, simulation, rapid prototyping, testing and built-to-print production, with product lines for traction, motion, aviation and exploration (oil and gas). The company states that it is AS9100 certified and works to the standards of the aerospace, defence and security industries.

## Products and solutions

- **iNetic Traction** - electric drivetrain products including the iEV and rEV motor ranges and a motor drive.
- **iNetic Motion** - servo motors for automation and industrial use.
- **iNetic Aviation** - permanent magnet alternators, stepper motors, frameless servo motors and custom brushless DC motors for aerospace applications.
- **iNetic Exploration** - motors for oil and gas exploration.
- **Engineering services** - custom motor design, design and simulation, rapid prototyping and testing, and built-to-print manufacturing with optional IP transfer.
