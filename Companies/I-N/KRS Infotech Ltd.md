# KRS Infotech Ltd

> UK technology consultancy and resource partner supplying specialist engineering and enterprise transformation capability to defence programmes.

## What they do

KRS Infotech is a UK technology consultancy and resource partner, established in 2013, supplying specialist engineering and enterprise transformation capability to defence primes, technology vendors and critical national infrastructure programmes. The company states it operates as a tier-1 subcontractor on UK defence programmes, delivering both embedded specialist resource and managed, outcome-based delivery against defined statements of work. It works from two sites: an office in Colchester, Essex, and a registered office in Wimbledon, London, giving it a presence in the East of England defence cluster alongside London coverage.

Note on identity: several unrelated companies trade under the "KRS Infotech" name, including an India-based CRM software firm. Company number 08546527 is the definitive identifier for this UK entity.

## Products and solutions

- **SAP and ERP transformation** - ECC to S/4HANA migration from readiness assessment through to post-migration support, greenfield and brownfield systems integration, SAP EWM, ABAP development, quality assurance reviews and licensing advisory.
- **Test and quality assurance** - programme test management, and functional, performance, security and usability testing, manual and automated, with agile and continuous integration adoption support.
- **Infrastructure and platform engineering** - Linux server build, patching, hardening and application support in secure environments.
- **DevOps and automation** - CI/CD pipeline design, build automation and toolchain integration.
- **Software design and engineering** - bespoke development and solution design.
- **Enterprise architecture and IT strategy** - legacy-to-cloud transition, IT strategy consulting, software portfolio analysis and rationalisation.
- **Systems integration, cyber security and data analysis** - integration, security and data capability for defence and critical infrastructure clients.
- **Quantum computing** - listed by the company as a service capability.
- **Software and systems training** - technical training for software and engineering teams.

## Delivery models

- Embedded specialist resource under client direction.
- Managed, outcome-based delivery against statements of work and payment-by-performance milestones.
- Backfill and surge resourcing for cleared programme roles.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Frameworks:** JOSCAR (Joint Supply Chain Accreditation Register), registered.
- **Memberships:** ADS Group.
- **Security:** the company states SC-cleared engineering resource is available, and that it sponsors, renews and upgrades clearances to meet programme requirements.
