# Norwegian Aviation & Defense Group AS

> Sola company providing Bell helicopters to Norway and Iceland for offshore, utility and military missions.

## What they do

Norwegian Aviation and Defense Group represents Bell Helicopter in Norway and Iceland. It offers helicopters for offshore, utility, military and mapping work, including the Bell 412 and Bell 525. It states that it is recognised as an industrial cooperation partner by the Norwegian Ministry of Defence.

## Products and solutions

- **Bell 525** - Helicopter offered for troop transport and other mission profiles
- **Bell 412** - Multi-mission helicopter in service with the Norwegian Air Force
