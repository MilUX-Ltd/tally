# Magellan Aerospace UK

> UK subsidiary of Canadian aerospace manufacturer Magellan Aerospace, machining aerostructure components for Airbus wings at Llay, Wrexham.

## What they do

Magellan Aerospace (UK) Limited is the UK business of Magellan Aerospace Corporation, a Canadian designer and manufacturer of aeroengine and aerostructure assemblies listed on the Toronto Stock Exchange. The UK company was incorporated in 2003 and its registered office is at the Structures Building, Rackery Lane, Llay, Wrexham. The Welsh operation uses high-speed 3-, 4- and 5-axis long-bed machining centres to make major aerostructure components for Airbus, with surface treatments and assembly supporting component manufacture. Magellan states that its Wrexham facility is its long-bed machining centre for Airbus single-aisle wing components.

## Products and solutions

- **Aerostructure components** - spars, wing ribs and skin reinforcements for Airbus wing programmes.
- **Long-bed machining** - high-speed 5-axis machining of large aluminium and hard metal parts.
- **Surface treatment and assembly** - treatments and assembly of complex aerostructure components.
