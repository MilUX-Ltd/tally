# The Armour Group

> Louth-based group of companies providing cyber security, compliance and penetration testing, investigations and airborne imaging services.

## What they do

The Armour Group describes itself as a group of four specialist businesses covering cyber security, technology and intelligence, based in Louth, Lincolnshire. Network Armour provides security architecture, cloud protection and managed services; Digital Armour provides cyber advice, certification and penetration testing; Dark Armour provides investigations, digital intelligence and protection; and Airborne Armour provides airborne capability, aerial imaging and intelligence. The holding company was registered in 2019 as The Armour Group of Companies Limited and changed its name in September 2025.

## Products and solutions

- **Network Armour** - security architecture, cloud protection and managed security services.
- **Digital Armour** - cyber security advice, certification support and penetration testing.
- **Dark Armour** - investigations, digital intelligence and protection.
- **Airborne Armour** - aerial imaging and airborne intelligence services.
