# Trizo

> Bridgwater contract electronics manufacturer of PCB assemblies and box builds for defence, aerospace and space.

## What they do

Trizo is a UK contract electronics manufacturer providing high-reliability PCB assembly, surface-mount assembly, prototyping, testing and full box-build integration. It serves defence and security, aviation and space, automotive, nuclear and energy, medical and commercial customers, from prototype to full-scale production, with AS9100-accredited processes and a traceable supply chain.

## Products and solutions

- **PCB assembly** - surface-mount and through-hole assembly, including multi-layer and high-density boards.
- **Prototyping and testing** - prototype builds, inspection including automated X-ray, and test.
- **Box build** - full system integration and assembly.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Accreditations:** AS9100.
