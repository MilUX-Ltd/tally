# SensiPhi

> University of Southampton spin-out developing high-resolution distributed acoustic sensing systems that turn optical fibres into dense sensor arrays.

## What they do

SensiPhi describes itself as a deep-tech start-up spun out from the University of Southampton in 2024. It develops High-Resolution Distributed Acoustic Sensing (HR-DAS) systems that use specially designed optical fibre and an interrogator to measure strain and vibration at over 10,000 points along a single fibre. The company states its technologies apply to civil engineering, aerospace, oil and gas, seismology and defence, including perimeter and border security monitoring. It offers equipment rental, system integration and monitoring services alongside its products.

## Products and solutions

- **HR-DAS** - high-resolution distributed acoustic sensing interrogator with centimetre-scale spatial resolution.
- **xDAS** - conventional distributed acoustic sensing for long-range monitoring, including perimeter security, structural health monitoring and seismology.
- **ULEB fibre** - ultra-low-loss enhanced backreflecting optical fibre for distributed acoustic sensing.
- **Services** - interrogator rental, system integration and commissioning, data gathering and full-service monitoring.
