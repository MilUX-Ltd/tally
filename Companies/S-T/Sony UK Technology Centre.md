# Sony UK Technology Centre

> Sony manufacturing and service centre at Pencoed, near Bridgend, producing broadcast and professional equipment and offering contract electronics manufacturing to third-party customers.

## What they do

Sony UK Technology Centre (Sony UK TEC) is a Sony manufacturing, service and repair centre at Pencoed Technology Park, near Bridgend, in a 30,000 m² building opened in 1993. Its core operation is the manufacture of high-end HD and 4K broadcast and professional equipment, and it states it is the only facility outside East Asia producing high definition camera units for Sony. It also offers contract electronics manufacturing for third-party customers, covering design for manufacture and test, prototyping, new product introduction, surface mount and through-hole assembly, testing and supply chain management. The site also provides professional equipment repairs and hosts a business incubation centre with office space for smaller companies.

## Products and solutions

- **Contract electronics manufacturing** - prototyping, new product introduction, SMT and through-hole PCB assembly, conformal coating, potting and X-ray inspection.
- **Test and compliance** - bespoke test equipment design, EMC testing, safety testing, drop and vibration testing, functional and in-circuit testing.
- **Sony broadcast manufacturing** - manufacture of HD and 4K broadcast and professional camera equipment.
- **Product service and repairs** - professional equipment repairs, on-location support and refurbished broadcast equipment.
- **Office rental** - business incubation centre space on the Pencoed site.
