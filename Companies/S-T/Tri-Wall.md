# Tri-Wall

> Industrial packaging manufacturer headquartered in Monmouth, supplying multi-material packaging, packaging design, testing and fulfilment services from seven UK sites.

## What they do

Tri-Wall is a packaging provider offering off-the-shelf and made-to-measure multi-material packaging, with services covering design and development, manufacturing, testing, fulfilment, stock management and storage, timber treatment and on-site packing. Its UK headquarters is at Wonastow Road, Monmouth, which houses its corrugator, specialises in industrial corrugated board and has a UKAS-accredited in-house test facility; it also has a site at Penllergaer, Swansea, and five sites in England. Tri-Wall states it was the first manufacturer to bring triple-wall corrugated board to the UK and Europe, and that it is part of the global Tri-Wall network of more than 170 sites and 16,000 employees. The Monmouth site supplies sectors including aerospace, automotive, retail, manufacturing and engineering.

## Products and solutions

- **Corrugated and multi-material packaging** - heavy-duty triple-wall corrugated board and other materials, standard and made to measure.
- **UN dangerous goods packaging** - UN-certified packaging for dangerous goods, including lithium-ion batteries.
- **Packaging testing** - UKAS-accredited in-house testing including compression, drop, vibration and climatic tests.
- **Packaging design and development** - bespoke packaging design.
- **Fulfilment, storage and on-site packing** - packaging fulfilment, stock management and storage, and packing at customer sites.
