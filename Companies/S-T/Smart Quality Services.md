# Smart Quality Services

> Bournemouth ISO management system consultancy also supporting Cyber Essentials and Defence Cyber Certification.

## What they do

Smart Quality Services Ltd is an independent consultancy that helps organisations build and maintain ISO management systems, working mainly with ISO 9001, 14001, 45001 and 27001. It states it does not sell certification and helps clients choose an independent UKAS-accredited certification body. It also supports Cyber Essentials, Cyber Essentials Plus and Defence Cyber Certification, delivered with CyberSmart, and lists defence and aerospace among its sectors.

## Products and solutions

- **ISO consultancy** - fixed-fee gap analysis, management system development, internal audits, management review and Stage 1 audit support.
- **Ongoing support** - maintenance support to keep certification in place.
- **Cyber certification** - Cyber Essentials, Cyber Essentials Plus and Defence Cyber Certification support.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Accreditations:** Cyber Essentials.
- **Memberships:** South Central RDSC; Make UK; Hampshire Chamber of Commerce; FAC Aerospace Defence Space; South West RDSC
