# SeaBot Maritime

> Portsmouth maritime consultancy and learning provider offering courses in maritime autonomy, remote operations and uncrewed survey.

## What they do

SeaBot Maritime Ltd describes itself as a specialist maritime consultancy and learning solutions provider that creates and delivers learning through digital and experiential solutions. Its courses cover maritime autonomy and remote operations and the use of uncrewed aerial and surface vehicles for survey. The company provides information on training funding for Armed Forces personnel and states it is a Career Transition Partnership preferred supplier and an Armed Forces Covenant signatory.

## Products and solutions

- **Maritime Autonomy and Remote Operations** - training course.
- **UAV Surveyor** - training course on uncrewed aerial vehicle survey.
- **USV Surveyor** - training course on uncrewed surface vessel survey.
- **SeaLearn** - online knowledge hub and training portal.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Accreditations:** Career Transition Partnership preferred supplier; Armed Forces Covenant signatory.
- **Memberships:** Maritime UK Solent (Launchpad partner); South Central RDSC
