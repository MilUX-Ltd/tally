# Thornton-Firkin

> National construction consultancy practice with offices in Birmingham, Chester, Lincoln, London and Newcastle.

## What they do

Thornton-Firkin describes itself as a national practice offering professional construction consultancy services to clients across all sectors of industry and commerce. It operates as Thornton-Firkin LLP, with offices in Birmingham, Chester, Lincoln, London and Newcastle upon Tyne. Its Lincoln office is at Deepdale Enterprise Park, Nettleham.
