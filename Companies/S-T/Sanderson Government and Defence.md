# Sanderson Government and Defence

> Bristol-based specialist in talent and delivery services for UK government, defence, national security and their critical suppliers.

## What they do

Sanderson Government and Defence Limited is the government and defence business of the Sanderson recruitment group, with its head office in Bristol. The company states it provides talent solutions for the UK government sector and its critical supply chains, with about 20 years of experience in the sector, and that it hires security-cleared talent at SC and DV level. Its stated sectors are UK government, defence, national security and critical suppliers.

## Products and solutions

- **Staffing solutions** - permanent, contract and interim talent in data and AI, design, cyber security, technology and portfolio, programme and project management.
- **Executive search** - senior leaders, interim specialists and advisers.
- **Recruitment process outsourcing** - full or project-based outsourced recruitment.
- **Managed service programmes** - single-framework management of permanent and contingent talent, with a security-clearance pipeline.
- **SGD Applied** - consulting and delivery division that takes responsibility for outcomes.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Accreditations:** ISO 9001, ISO 14001, ISO 45001, ISO/IEC 27001, Cyber Essentials Plus
- **Other:** Armed Forces Covenant signatory; Government Commercial Agency supplier
