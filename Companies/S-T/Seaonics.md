# Seaonics

> Ålesund maker of cranes, winches, gangways and launch and recovery systems, with a Navy and Coast Guard market segment.

## What they do

Seaonics supplies electric lifting and handling equipment for vessels, including cranes, gangways, winches and launch and recovery systems. Its market segments are offshore energy, ocean harvesting, research and exploration, and navy and coast guard. Its site includes material on seabed defence and the monitoring of subsea infrastructure.
