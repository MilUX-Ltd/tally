# Svemko

> Norwegian maker of silencers and muzzle brakes for hunters, sport shooters, military and law enforcement, with a dedicated defence division.

## What they do

Svemko produces titanium silencers, muzzle brakes and accessories. Its dedicated Svemko Defence division serves military and law enforcement users and has worked with the Norwegian Armed Forces on suppressors. Flash performance has been measured by the Norwegian Defence Research Establishment.

## Products and solutions

- **Silencers** - Titanium suppressors for rifles
- **Muzzle brakes** - Recoil reduction for rifles
- **Quick-Mount** - Quick attachment system
