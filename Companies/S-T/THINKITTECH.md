# THINKITTECH

> Daresbury software company building material traceability and systems integration software, including AMSynchro for metal powders in additive manufacturing.

## What they do

THINKITTECH is a UK software company based at Sci-Tech Daresbury that builds software for material traceability, systems integration and condition monitoring. It links data held in different systems, such as shipments, lab results and inventory movements, into a single material history. Its stated applications include metal additive manufacturing, biotech and biobanks, the nuclear sector, and space, aerospace and defence. The company does not publish a site in Wales; it is listed as a member of Defence Wales.

## Products and solutions

- **AMSynchro** - software for metal powders that brings together powder specifications, lab results, operations and inventory, with material genealogy views.
- **In-transit condition monitoring** - review of humidity, oxygen, pressure and temperature recorded during transport.
- **Systems integration** - automated retrieval and linking of records from existing systems.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Memberships:** Defence Wales. The company states its work has been supported by the European Space Agency (ESA Kick-Start activity) and the UK Space Agency.
