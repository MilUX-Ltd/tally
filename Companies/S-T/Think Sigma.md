# Think Sigma

> Hampshire training and consultancy company focused on commercial and contract management skills.

## What they do

Think Sigma Ltd delivers training and consultancy aimed at improving commercial awareness, decision making and value from contracts. The company states that its team brings experience from commercial, contracting and leadership roles. Its training courses are built around the client organisation, and its consultancy work focuses on identifying constraints, building capability and improving performance. It also lists apprenticeships among its services.

## Products and solutions

- **Commercial awareness training** - practical courses in commercial and contract management.
- **Business consultancy** - hands-on support to build commercial capability and improve performance.
