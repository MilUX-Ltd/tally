# Skarv Technologies AS

> Trondheim maker of autonomous underwater vehicles for optical and acoustic seabed survey, including mine countermeasures.

## What they do

Skarv Technologies builds the CORAX series of autonomous underwater vehicles for high-resolution optical and acoustic seafloor mapping. The vehicle detects, images and classifies objects on the seabed, supporting survey work alongside mine countermeasures and threat identification.

## Products and solutions

- **CORAX 600** - Opto-acoustic survey autonomous underwater vehicle
