# Sterling Thermal Technology

> Aylesbury designer and manufacturer of bespoke heat exchangers for defence, marine, power generation, renewables and industrial markets.

## What they do

Sterling Thermal Technology (Sterling TT) designs and manufactures bespoke and standard heat exchangers at its head office and manufacturing facility in Aylesbury, and repairs, replaces and refurbishes heat exchangers including those of other designs. The company states that it was established in 1904 and has previously traded under names including HEI, Thermo Engineers, Britannia Heat Transfer, Thame Energy Services and Britannia Heatex. It states that it has supplied heat exchange technology to the defence sector for more than 50 years, working with the Royal Navy and allied navies on ships and submarines, including coolers for the Type 26 frigates, and supplies exact replacement or upgraded parts to keep older equipment in service.

## Products and solutions

- **Compact fin heat exchangers** - air coolers, charge air coolers, condensers, hydrogen coolers and transformer coolers.
- **Single tube heat exchangers** - air cooled condensers, air blast coolers, air preheaters, economisers and rotating machine coolers.
- **Shell and tube heat exchangers** - compact shell and tube units, reboilers and transformer oil water coolers.
- **Aftermarket and service support** - repair, replacement, refurbishment and test, inspection and report services.
