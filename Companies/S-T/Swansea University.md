# Swansea University

> Research university with security, defence and resilience research spanning advanced materials and manufacturing, cyber security, communications and navigation, and human performance.

## What they do

Swansea University is a research university with two campuses, Singleton Park and the Bay Campus on Fabian Way, where its engineering and computer science activity is based. Its security, defence and resilience research is organised in four themes: advanced materials, manufacturing and engineering; human performance, health and resilience; cyber security and digital resilience; and communications, connectivity and navigation, underpinned by expertise in artificial intelligence, modelling and the social sciences. The university states that much of this research is dual-use, with civilian, industrial and public-sector as well as defence applications. It is a founding member of the Defence Universities Alliance and has been an Armed Forces Covenant signatory since 2023.

## Products and solutions

- **Security, defence and resilience research** - research programme across the four themes above, with academia, industry and government partners.
- **Computational Foundry** - computer science and mathematics building on the Bay Campus.
- **MSc Cyber Security** - postgraduate degree certified by the National Cyber Security Centre.
- **College of Engineering research** - materials, manufacturing and coatings research, including the IMPACT institute, on the Bay Campus.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Accreditations:** NCSC-certified MSc Cyber Security.
- **Memberships:** Defence Wales; Defence Universities Alliance (founding member); Armed Forces Covenant signatory.
