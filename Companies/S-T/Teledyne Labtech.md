# Teledyne Labtech

> Teledyne business in Presteigne, Powys, that manufactures, assembles and tests complex RF and microwave printed circuit boards for space, defence and medical applications.

## What they do

Teledyne Labtech manufactures, assembles and tests complex RF and microwave printed circuit boards and assemblies, built to design-for-manufacture principles, for use in satellite payloads, defence electronics and medical devices. The business operates from Broadaxe Business Park in Presteigne, Powys, and states over four decades of experience in the industry. Labtech was founded in 1984 and became part of Teledyne Technologies in 2010 through Teledyne's acquisition of Intelek plc. Teledyne has invested in PTFE laminate processing equipment at the Presteigne site, which serves defence and aerospace markets.

## Products and solutions

- **RF and microwave PCBs** - manufacture of complex RF and microwave printed circuit boards, including thin PTFE substrates.
- **PCB assemblies** - design-for-manufacture complex PCB assemblies.
- **Test** - testing of RF and microwave assemblies.
