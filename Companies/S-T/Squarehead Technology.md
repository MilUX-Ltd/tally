# Squarehead Technology

> Norwegian maker of passive acoustic microphone-array sensors for drone detection and artillery fire detection, as well as industrial monitoring.

## What they do

Squarehead Technology develops microphone arrays that passively detect threats and anomalies in the sound image. Its defence application areas are drone detection and acoustic counter rocket, artillery and mortar detection. It also serves industrial and maritime markets.

## Products and solutions

- **Drone detection** - Passive acoustic detection of drones
- **Acoustic C-RAM** - Directional acoustic arrays to detect rocket, artillery and mortar fire
