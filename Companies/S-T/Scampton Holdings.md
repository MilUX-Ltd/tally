# Scampton Holdings

> Development company proposing the regeneration of the former RAF Scampton site in Lincolnshire for aerospace, defence, education and heritage uses.

## What they do

Scampton Holdings Limited states that it is the preferred development partner of West Lindsey District Council for the former RAF Scampton site, having won a public tender process in 2023. Its stated vision is to redevelop the site as a hub for commercial aerospace, defence, education and heritage activity, keeping Scampton as a licensed and operational airfield and creating local jobs and educational pathways. The company states that its team brings large-scale project, construction, aerospace, defence and airfield operation experience, supported by an advisory board.

## Products and solutions

- **RAF Scampton redevelopment** - proposed phased, masterplanned regeneration of the former RAF Scampton site covering commercial, aerospace, defence, education and heritage uses.
