# Time on Target

> Wiltshire defence consultancy and supplier helping companies bring products and technology to frontline military users.

## What they do

Time on Target is a UK defence consultancy and supplier whose stated aim is to help innovative companies get technology to frontline military users. It offers bid support, market engagement, sales and marketing strategy, field-driven product development, product sourcing and innovation scouting. It also supplies product lines including clothing and footwear, personal protection and technology, and sells Nordic and alpine skis direct to military units. The company was incorporated in 2025 and is registered in Bulford, Wiltshire.

## Products and solutions

- **Bid support** - support for defence bids.
- **Market engagement** - market engagement and end-user fusion.
- **Product development and sourcing** - field-driven product development, product sourcing and routes to market.
- **Product supply** - clothing, footwear, personal protection and technology, and skis for military units.
