# Seacht Group

> Ringwood-based energy and engineering group providing design, fabrication, pressure vessel manufacture, civils and site services.

## What they do

Seacht Group describes itself as a UK energy and engineering group offering turnkey solutions for the power sector. Through its group companies it provides design, fabrication, pressure vessel manufacturing, civils and onsite services, and it states it works in natural gas networks, security and renewable energy. The holding company, Seacht Holdings Limited, is registered at ICM House in Ringwood, Hampshire.

## Products and solutions

- **Saith** - EPC design services for energy and petrochemicals.
- **Paul & Loughran** - pressure vessels, heat exchangers and process skids, based in Consett.
- **BLE Engineering** - fabrication skids and pipework, based in Southampton.
- **VII Services** - civils, installation and maintenance for energy and utilities.
- **Corrosion Protection Services** - cathodic protection for oil, gas, marine and petrochemical sectors.
- **Parkside Safety Services** - CDM and health and safety consultancy.
