# Scanmatic AS

> Arendal company supplying instrumentation and control systems, with defence systems for NATO and allied navies including sonar test and anti-torpedo countermeasures.

## What they do

Scanmatic AS has delivered instrumentation, control systems and signal processing since 1971. Its defence segment covers hydroacoustic test, calibration and anti-submarine warfare training systems, torpedo countermeasures for submarines and surface ships, multi-positioning for calibration, and radar monitoring and surveillance systems. Other segments include renewables, environment, traffic systems and offshore.

## Products and solutions

- **TORDEC** - Anti-torpedo countermeasure for surface vessels
- **SONDEC** - Sonar decoy and torpedo countermeasure
- **SONCAT** - Sonar calibration and training buoy
- **SONTAS** - Portable sonar tester for calibration at site
- **Sonar3** - Core technology for underwater acoustics and sonar systems
