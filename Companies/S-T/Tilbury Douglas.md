# Tilbury Douglas

> UK building, fit-out, engineering and infrastructure contractor with a defence sector practice.

## What they do

Tilbury Douglas is a UK building, fit-out, engineering and infrastructure company providing design-and-build, project management and construction services, with mechanical, electrical and plumbing engineering delivered through Tilbury Douglas Engineering Limited. Its sectors include health, education, water, defence, justice and civic and civil government. The company states it has supported the Ministry of Defence with construction services for over 20 years, is a framework partner to the Defence Infrastructure Organisation through the CCS construction framework, and is delivering the joint MOD and NHS Integrated Care Centre at Catterick Garrison. Formerly part of Interserve, it readopted the Tilbury Douglas name in 2021 and reported turnover of over 600 million pounds for 2025.

## Products and solutions

- **Regional building** - new build, refurbishment, design and build, and build-only projects.
- **Fit-out** - Cat A and Cat B fit-out and refurbishment.
- **Engineering** - mechanical, electrical and plumbing services as principal contractor and specialist supply chain partner.
- **Infrastructure** - critical infrastructure projects.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Frameworks:** Defence Infrastructure Organisation framework partner through the CCS construction framework.
- **Accreditations:** Investors in Diversity Standard; Disability Confident Committed; Defence Employer Recognition Scheme Gold award; Armed Forces Covenant signatory.
- **Memberships:** Build UK; Greater Lincolnshire RDSC
