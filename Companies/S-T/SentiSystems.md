# SentiSystems

> Trondheim company developing GNSS-denied navigation and sensor fusion for ships, drones and vehicles.

## What they do

SentiSystems is an NTNU spin-off that develops sensor-agnostic navigation software and hardware for positioning when satellite navigation is unavailable or jammed. It cites deployments across maritime, defence and industrial sectors. The SentiHUB board synchronises timing across inertial, visual, lidar and radar sensors.

## Products and solutions

- **SentiNAV** - Sensor fusion navigation solution for GNSS-denied positioning
- **SentiHUB** - Hardware board for precise sensor time synchronisation
