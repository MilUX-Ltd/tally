# Shenton Group

> Andover provider of standby generators, UPS and combined heat and power systems, with rental, maintenance and 24/7 emergency support.

## What they do

Shenton Group supplies, installs and maintains standby and prime power generators, uninterruptible power supplies, control panels and switchgear, and combined heat and power systems. The company states it has been delivering power solutions since 1982 and supports public services, government departments, national infrastructure and high security sites, with a Government and MOD sector among those it serves. It states that its first major Ministry of Defence contract was during the Gulf conflict in 1989. Services include generator rental, maintenance contracts, remote monitoring, fuel services, load bank testing, design and consultancy. The company describes itself as family run; the trading entity named on its accreditations is Shenton Global Limited.

## Products and solutions

- **Standby and prime generators** - supply, installation and integration of generator systems.
- **Uninterruptible power supplies** - UPS systems and maintenance contracts.
- **Control panels and switchgear** - including ATS panels.
- **Combined heat and power** - CHP systems; the company states it is sole UK distributor for TEDOM.
- **Generator rental and Power Call** - temporary and emergency power, with a 24/7 call-out service.
- **Maintenance and monitoring** - generator, UPS, CHP and HV/LV switchgear maintenance, remote monitoring, fuel polishing, load bank testing and refuelling.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Accreditations:** ISO 9001; ISO 14001; ISO 45001; Cyber Essentials; Gas Safe registered engineers; UVDB Achilles; Constructionline; CHAS; SafeContractor; HMRC registered dealer in controlled oil.
- **Memberships:** South Central RDSC
