# TINEX AS

> System integrator and commercial agency supplying defence and security equipment to the Norwegian market for foreign manufacturers.

## What they do

TINEX is a privately owned company founded in 1991 that helps foreign manufacturers enter the Norwegian market. In defence and security it integrates and delivers electronic warfare, force protection, sensor, EOD and power supply equipment to defence, police and other security authorities. It also offers engineering, systems integration, service and maintenance, testing and training.

## Products and solutions

- **Defence & Security** - Electronic warfare, force protection, sensors, EOD and power supply
- **Systems integration** - Design, integration, service and maintenance
