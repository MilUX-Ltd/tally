# SMI Interconnect

> UK manufacturer of wiring looms, cable harnesses and electrical assemblies, part of the SMI cable systems group in Andover.

## What they do

SMI Interconnect (Systems Manufacturing and Interconnect) is a specialist manufacturer of wiring looms, cable harnesses and electrical assemblies for OEMs, operating as a distinct offering within the wider SMI group, which manufactures pressure moulded cable systems from Andover, Hampshire. It states it supports work from prototype and small batch to volume production, with build-to-print and panel wiring, and that all manufacturing is carried out in the UK. It lists marine, land and defence, communications, aerospace, energy and industrial among the sectors it serves.

## Products and solutions

- **Wiring looms** - low to high complexity looms.
- **Cable harnesses** - harness assemblies built to customer specifications.
- **Build-to-print** - manufacture to customer drawings.
- **Prototype and production** - new product introduction, small batch and volume production.
- **Panel wiring** - panel wiring and electrical assemblies.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Accreditations:** ISO 9001, ISO 14001, ISO 45001, Cyber Essentials Plus and JOSCAR (company-stated). Production staff trained to IPC soldering and crimping standards.
- **Memberships:** South Central RDSC
