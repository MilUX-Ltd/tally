# SMS Aero

> Aviation safety training and consultancy provider based at RAF Cranwell in Lincolnshire.

## What they do

SMS Aero is the trading name of Affinity Capital Works Limited. It describes itself as a provider of aviation safety training and consultancy for regulatory authorities, operators and maintenance organisations worldwide. Its areas of expertise include safety management systems, regulatory compliance, risk management, human factors, root cause analysis and technical assurance. The company states it has operated for nearly 20 years, and its registered office is at Hangar 29, RAF Cranwell.

## Products and solutions

- **Core competence and continuation courses** - aviation safety training.
- **Management system courses** - safety management system training.
- **Regulatory awareness courses** - regulatory training.
- **Assured Competency Programme** - structured competence programmes tailored to each organisation.
- **Consultancy** - support in safety, compliance, technical assurance and risk management.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Memberships:** Greater Lincolnshire RDSC; Royal Aeronautical Society.
