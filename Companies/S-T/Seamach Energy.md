# Seamach Energy

> UK engineering firm developing patented floating offshore wind turbine technology and working across wave, tidal and fusion energy.

## What they do

Seamach Energy, the trading name of Seamach Ltd, describes itself as a UK engineering firm specialising in renewable energy, with expertise in high energy electro-mechanical and multiphysics design, including nuclear fusion. Its work covers wind, wave and tidal energy, floating structures and fusion energy. The company states it has designed and patented next generation wind turbine technologies targeting systems of 30 to 50MW, including a floating offshore wind turbine and platform, with patents granted in the UK, USA, China and Europe. It also develops onshore and urban turbines with modular electrical generators.

## Products and solutions

- **Floating offshore wind turbine** - patented 50MW-class floating turbine and platform architecture.
- **Onshore and urban turbines** - wind turbine designs with modular electrical generators.
- **Engineering design** - high energy electro-mechanical and multiphysics design, including fusion energy work.
