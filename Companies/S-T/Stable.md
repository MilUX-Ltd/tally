# Stable

> Arendal company making precision stabilisation platforms for moving vessels and vehicles, with defence, maritime, health and yacht markets.

## What they do

Stable builds stabilised platforms that reduce the effect of motion on people, sensors and systems. Its defence sector page covers stabilised launch and recovery surfaces for UAVs and stabilisation of radars, antennas, cameras and sensor payloads on moving vessels. It also serves maritime, healthcare and yacht markets.

## Products and solutions

- **UAV launch and recovery platform** - Stabilised take-off and landing surface at sea
- **Sensor and payload stabilisation** - For radars, antennas, LiDARs and cameras
