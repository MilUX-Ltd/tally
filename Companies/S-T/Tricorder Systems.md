# Tricorder Systems

> London start-up building deployable field medical hardware and command software for forward medical units and field hospitals.

## What they do

Tricorder Systems manufactures hardware for forward-deployed medical units, from light surgical teams to complete field hospitals, and ships it with ClinicalOS, command and control software that tracks the patient journey, consumables, power and equipment. Its pods are built to deploy to forward operating bases and run fully offline, for sovereign defence procurement. The company was incorporated in 2026 and is backed by Founders Factory.

## Products and solutions

- **Deployable medical hardware** - pods and equipment for forward medical units, from light surgical teams to turnkey field hospitals.
- **ClinicalOS** - command and control for field medical operations: patient journey, supply chain, power and devices in one view, running offline.
- **Continual Learning** - a platform that carries lessons from each deployment into the next.
