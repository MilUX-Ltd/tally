# Solent University

> Southampton university with a practical focus, including the Warsash Maritime School for maritime and offshore education and training.

## What they do

Solent University is a university based in Southampton, previously known as Southampton Solent University. It describes its focus as the practical side of higher education, giving students connections, experience and confidence for their careers, and offers apprenticeships, consultancy, facilities and research collaboration to businesses. Its Warsash Maritime School, at a campus in Warsash, states it has delivered maritime education, training and consultancy for over 70 years and offers over 150 accredited deck, engineering, interior, maritime and offshore safety courses. Warsash runs officer cadet training programmes and ship simulation and ship handling facilities.

## Products and solutions

- **Higher education** - undergraduate, postgraduate and research degrees.
- **Apprenticeships** - degree and higher apprenticeships for employers.
- **Warsash Maritime School** - deck, engineering, electro-technical, STCW safety and security, and offshore training.
- **Maritime simulation** - bridge, engine room and liquid cargo simulators, and a ship handling training centre.
- **Business services** - consultancy, facilities hire and student recruitment for employers.
