# Space Solar

> Harwell-based company developing space-based solar power and wireless power beaming.

## What they do

Space Solar states that it is developing and commercialising space-based solar power, using large, modular solar arrays assembled in orbit and wireless power transmission to deliver energy to Earth. Its head office is in the Atlas Building on the Harwell campus in Oxfordshire, and its website is operated by Space Solar Group Holdings Ltd. The company states that its technology has defence and security applications, including resilient space operations, and that its OSPREY Builder capability is intended for autonomous in-space assembly and repair of large structures. It was selected for the 2026 cohort of the NATO DIANA Challenge Programme.

## Products and solutions

- **Space-based solar power** - large lightweight solar arrays in orbit providing continuous power.
- **Wireless power transmission** - RF power beaming, including a 360 degree transmission demonstration.
- **OSPREY Builder** - autonomous in-space assembly and repair of large modular structures.
- **Power beaming for high altitude platforms** - RF power beaming research for enduring stratospheric flight.
