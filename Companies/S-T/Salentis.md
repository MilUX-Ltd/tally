# Salentis

> Farnham bid and proposal consultancy supporting capture, proposal writing, reviews and design.

## What they do

Salentis is a bid and proposal consultancy that works with clients to produce compliant proposals through a structured, systematic approach, from capture through to submission. Its services cover pre-bid capture strategy and competitor analysis, managed Red Team quality reviews, writers, designers and subject matter experts for client teams, and support for entering new procurement markets. Its published case studies include defence work, among them a Defence Operational Training Capability bid, and it states Armed Forces Covenant and Make UK Defence membership.

## Products and solutions

- **Pre-bid and capture** - capture strategy, black hat and blue team reviews, competitor analysis.
- **Quality reviews** - managed Red Team reviews with structured feedback.
- **Client team support** - proposal writers, graphic designers, template designers and procurement portal expertise.
- **Training** - proposal training alongside its structured proposal system.
