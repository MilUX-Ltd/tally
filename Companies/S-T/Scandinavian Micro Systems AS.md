# Scandinavian Micro Systems AS

> Norwegian maker of marine navigation instruments such as heading displays and optical bearing devices, selling to navies and coast guards among others.

## What they do

Scandinavian Micro Systems supplies ship navigation instruments and converters, including digital heading and rate-of-turn displays and optical bearing devices. Its site lists a Navy and Coast Guard market alongside commercial ships, yachts and cruise ships. It also offers vessel integration, support and spares.

## Products and solutions

- **Digital Heading and ROT Displays** - Ship heading and rate of turn
- **Optical Bearing Device** - Position fix independent of GPS
- **NMEA to Step/Synchro Converter** - Replaces old gyrocompass interfaces
