# Spectrum Technologies

> Bridgend manufacturer of UV laser wire marking, laser wire stripping and wire processing systems for aerospace, defence and other high-reliability industries.

## What they do

Spectrum Technologies designs, manufactures and supplies laser wire marking, laser wire stripping, measure-and-cut, wire handling and inkjet marking systems, along with sample processing and contract manufacturing services. It was established in 1989 as a subsidiary of British Aerospace to commercialise UV laser wire marking, became independent through a management buyout in 1994, and opened its Bridgend factory on Western Avenue in 2000. Its first wire marker was used on Tornado aircraft harnesses, and it serves aviation, defence, space, medical, rail, automotive and telecoms customers, reporting over 1,300 systems installed in 60 countries. It also has US operations near Fort Worth, Texas, and a sales office in Shanghai.

## Products and solutions

- **Laser wire markers** - Nova 50-100i, Nova 800i and Nova 1000 series UV laser wire marking systems.
- **Laser wire strippers** - Sienna 200, 300 and 500 series and Sienna Rapier.
- **Measure and cut systems** - Nova Mini-Max and Mini-Max+.
- **Wire handling and automation** - dereelers, wire auto select and load units, rereelers, coiling and labelling.
- **Inkjet wire markers** - NovaJet.
- **Peripheral systems** - CMS2 contrast measurement system, Nova Lift and air cleaning systems.
