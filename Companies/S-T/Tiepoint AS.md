# Tiepoint AS

> Operational drone services, maritime surveillance and AI-based data analysis for environmental, emergency response and defence-relevant missions.

## What they do

Tiepoint provides operational drone services for surveillance, inspection, mapping and emergency response. It also develops intelligence tools that combine edge processing, AI and data fusion for decision support. Its history lists total-defence deliveries and deliveries to the Norwegian Defence Estates Agency, and its intelligence work is described as defence relevant.

## Products and solutions

- **Drone services** - Operational drone operations for surveillance, inspection and mapping
- **TRONE** - Framework for compliant drone flight operations
- **Intelligence** - AI and data fusion for faster operational decisions
