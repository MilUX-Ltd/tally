# The Excellence Partnership

> Business improvement consultancy providing strategy, lean, quality, people development and coaching programmes.

## What they do

The Excellence Partnership Limited describes itself as a firm of improvement specialists that helps organisations improve performance through their people and processes. It states it uses improvement frameworks and work-based learning programmes, including a seven-step Excellence Programme covering diagnostics, coaching, people development, process improvement and improvement projects. Its team covers strategy and operations, kaizen and lean, change, quality, coaching, and supply chains and procurement, and its published case studies include precision engineering and electronics manufacturers.

## Products and solutions

- **The Excellence Programme** - seven-step improvement framework from diagnostic to improvement projects.
- **Coaching and people development** - work-based learning and coaching programmes.
- **Process improvement** - kaizen, lean and quality improvement.
- **Supply chain and procurement** - supply chain improvement support.
