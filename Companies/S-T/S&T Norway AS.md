# S&T Norway AS

> Oslo space technology and consulting company with a Defence and Security expertise area covering satellite ISR, resilient navigation and space situational awareness.

## What they do

S&T Norway works in space technology and high-tech consulting. Its Defence and Security area offers Earth observation solutions for intelligence, surveillance and reconnaissance, resilient satellite navigation for positioning, navigation and timing, and sensors for space situational awareness.

## Products and solutions

- **SMART Antenna** - Antenna product for resilient satellite navigation
