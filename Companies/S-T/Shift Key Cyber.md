# Shift Key Cyber

> Lincoln cyber security consultancy specialising in audit, risk, governance and compliance, including Defence Cyber Certification.

## What they do

Shift Key Cyber provides cyber security consulting services focused on audit, risk, governance and compliance for organisations of all sizes and sectors. The company states that it is an NCSC Assured Service Provider and an IASME certification body, and its services include Defence Cyber Certification, Cyber Essentials, IASME Cyber Assurance, supply chain audits and ISO 27001 implementation. It also offers virtual CISO and security manager services, incident management and business continuity planning. It was incorporated in 2021 and is registered at the Sparkhouse in Lincoln.

## Products and solutions

- **Defence Cyber Certification** - assessment and certification support for defence suppliers.
- **Certification** - Cyber Essentials and Cyber Essentials Plus, IASME Cyber Assurance and Secure Innovation Security Review Scheme.
- **Audit and implementation** - ISO 27001 and ISO 9001 implementation, internal audits and supply chain audits.
- **Advisory** - cyber resilience audit, Cyber Advisor, risk consultancy, virtual CISO and security manager as a service.
- **Resilience** - incident management and business continuity planning.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Accreditations:** Cyber Essentials Plus; NCSC Assured Service Provider; IASME certification body; The Cyber Scheme Accredited Company.
- **Memberships:** ADS; Greater Lincolnshire RDSC
