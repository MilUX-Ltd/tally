# SM Group (Europe)

> Plymouth value-added distributor of marine electronics to installers, boat builders, commercial, military and government customers.

## What they do

SM Group (Europe) Ltd (SMG) describes itself as the largest value-added distributor of marine electronics in the UK and Ireland, established in 1972. It supplies installers, chandleries, marinas, boat builders and commercial customers, and states that its customers include military and government clients. Brands it lists include Shakespeare Antennas, Actisense, Maretron, GME and Veratron.

## Products and solutions

- **Marine electronics distribution** - value-added distribution of marine electronics, antennas, networking and power products.
