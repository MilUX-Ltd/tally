# Stryvo AS

> Stryn mechanical manufacturer offering machining, welding and assembly, with defence listed among the industries it serves.

## What they do

Stryvo is a mechanical manufacturing company in Stryn. It offers design and engineering, CNC and manual machining, welding, gear production, assembly, surface treatment, testing and documentation. Its homepage lists defence among the industries it serves, with a dedicated sector page. It also sells its own products such as a seawater filter and a trash rack cleaner.

## Products and solutions

- **Sjøvannsfilter** - Seawater filter
- **Grindrensker** - Trash rack cleaner
