# Stable Resources

> Cardiff technology company supplying freelance technical resources and workplace technology built on Microsoft products, and an ADS Group member.

## What they do

Stable Resources Limited is based at Stable Studios in Pontcanna, Cardiff, and states it has more than 20 years of experience. It supplies freelance technical resources to Microsoft UK and other organisations, builds workplace technology solutions on Microsoft products and offers skills-based training. Its ADS Group member profile lists it under professional services and describes it as a Microsoft preferred partner.

## Products and solutions

- **Resources** - technical resourcing, including freelance resources.
- **Solutions** - workplace technology built on Microsoft products.
- **Learning** - technical and skills-based training.
- **Sgript** - AI-powered workspace that turns audio, notes and supporting content into completed forms.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Memberships:** ADS Group
- **Other:** Microsoft Solutions Partner
