# Sherborne Sensors

> Basingstoke designer and manufacturer of precision accelerometers, inclinometers and load cells, part of Nova Metrix.

## What they do

Sherborne Sensors designs and manufactures high-precision accelerometers, inclinometers and load cells for industries including aerospace, civil engineering, metal processing, mining and tunnelling, and transport. The company states its origins lie in Schaevitz Engineering, which began UK manufacture of inertial products in 1974; former employees acquired that business in 2002 to form Sherborne Sensors Limited, which added force transducer lines from Maywood Instruments in 2007 and became part of Nova Metrix in 2012. In aerospace it describes uses in flight control, inertial navigation and guidance, thrust measurement and test. Its headquarters is in Basingstoke, Hampshire, with a sales office in the USA.

## Products and solutions

- **Accelerometers** - servo and MEMS accelerometers for linear acceleration measurement.
- **Inclinometers** - servo, digital, wireless and MEMS inclinometers.
- **Load cells** - compression, shear and tension load cells and force transducers.
- **Custom solutions** - custom-built sensors around its inertial sensor elements.
- **Services** - calibration and repairs, training, and consulting and engineering.
