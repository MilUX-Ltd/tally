# TG Simulation

> Lincoln designer and builder of flight simulators and simulator parts, with a multi-crew training school.

## What they do

TG Simulation Ltd states that it designs, builds and services flight simulators and simulator parts, and has done so since 2013. It offers full-service simulator design and build, including a King Air B200 full flight simulator build, and simulator software including SimToolKitPro. It runs a multi-crew cooperation school using a Boeing 737-800 FNPT II and offers airline assessment preparation and simulator experiences. The company states it completed an A320neo conversion in Newark for the Air & Space Institute.

## Products and solutions

- **Simulator design and build** - concept to finished flight simulators and parts.
- **MCC School** - multi-crew training on a Boeing 737-800 FNPT II.
- **Simulator software** - SimToolKitPro and tailored simulator software.
