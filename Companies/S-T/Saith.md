# Saith

> Ringwood engineering consultancy for energy and utility networks and part of Seacht Group.

## What they do

Saith Limited is an engineering consultancy for energy and utility networks, based in Ringwood, Hampshire. It states it helps major utilities transition safely, efficiently and sustainably towards net zero, with work across energy networks, infrastructure design and technical assurance. Seacht Group lists Saith as its group company for EPC design services for energy and petrochemicals.

## Products and solutions

- **Energy networks** - engineering consultancy for energy and utility networks.
- **Infrastructure design** - EPC design services for energy and petrochemicals.
- **Technical assurance** - technical assurance for utility clients.
