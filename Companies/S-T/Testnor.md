# Testnor

> Tromsø company testing the resilience of navigation and other critical systems against real-world electronic warfare interference in the Arctic.

## What they do

Testnor captures real-world jamming and spoofing signals during open-air Arctic tests and replays them in the lab. It describes defence, industry and government as its customers and is backed by the NATO DIANA programme. It is certified to ISO 9001, 14001 and 27001.

## Products and solutions

- **Capture, playback, accelerate** - Field capture and lab replay of interference signals
