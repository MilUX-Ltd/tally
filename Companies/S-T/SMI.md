# SMI

> Andover designer and manufacturer of pressure moulded cable systems, connectors and hull penetrators for naval, aerospace and energy platforms.

## What they do

Scientific Management International (SMI) designs and manufactures fit and forget cable systems for platforms operating in hostile environments, including naval ships, submarines, aircraft, offshore power generation and transport. The company states that it is the preferred supplier of through-hull penetrators and connectors to the Royal Navy's nuclear submarine and surface fleets, and reports over 20,000 submarine installations. It holds Ministry of Defence Capability Approval to Defence Standard 08-171. SMI has its UK office in Andover, Hampshire, with offices in France, Canada and Australia.

## Products and solutions

- **Cable systems** - pressure moulded cable systems, subsea cable systems and aircraft cable harnesses.
- **Connectors** - subsea connectors, aircraft cable connectors and GISMA connectors.
- **Penetrators** - external hull penetrators, pressure hull glands, multi gland penetrators and connectorised penetrators.
- **TelemetriX** - cable and connector product line for defence platforms.
- **Engineering services** - in-house design, engineering and overmoulding technology.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Accreditations:** ISO 9001:2015; ISO 45001; MOD Capability Approval to Def Stan 08-171; MOD Type Approval; Cyber Essentials; Cyber Essentials Plus; JOSCAR.
- **Memberships:** ADS; Make UK Defence; Global Underwater Hub; South Central RDSC
