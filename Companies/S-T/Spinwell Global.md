# Spinwell Global

> Recruitment business supplying contract and permanent professionals, including in defence and security, to public sector and private clients.

## What they do

Spinwell Global Limited is a recruitment business with a UK office and a Singapore office. Its website lists sectors including engineering and construction (covering defence and security, aviation and aerospace, nuclear, rail and energy), change and transformation, financial services, technology and healthcare. Its published vacancies are mainly with central government, local authorities and councils, alongside some private sector roles.

## Products and solutions

- **Contract and permanent recruitment** - roles across public and private sector clients.
- **Leadership roles** - senior appointments.
