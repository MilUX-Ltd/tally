# The Good Penguin

> Newport-based embedded software consultancy specialising in embedded Linux, low-level software and device security.

## What they do

The Good Penguin is an embedded software consultancy founded in 2019 and based at Langstone, Newport. Its UK-based embedded Linux consultants provide engineering services covering hardware enablement, embedded distribution development, kernel and low-level development, security hardening and performance optimisation, typically on ARM-based platforms. It works to fixed-price or time-and-materials scopes from its own offices, or embeds engineers in customer teams for longer engagements.

## Products and solutions

- **Hardware enablement** - bootloader and OS porting, board bring-up and board support package development.
- **Distribution development** - embedded filesystems (Yocto, Buildroot, PTXdist), firmware update, secure boot and software integration.
- **Security** - vulnerability management, kernel and distribution maintenance, security hardening and secure boot.
- **Low-level and kernel development** - bare-metal and microcontroller firmware, driver development, Linux porting, backporting and upstreaming.
- **Optimisation** - boot time, power consumption and performance optimisation.
- **Application software** - embedded application software, libraries and porting.
