# TECOSIM UK

> Basildon-based computer-aided engineering company providing simulation, analysis, CAD and engineering staff services.

## What they do

TECOSIM UK is a computer-aided engineering company and part of the international TECOSIM group. It provides finite element analysis, computational fluid dynamics, thermal, multi-physics, multi-body and NVH simulation, optimisation, CAD and consultancy, and supplies engineers on demand. It lists automotive, energy, off-highway and agriculture, and product engineering among its sectors. Its UK head office is at Seax Court, Basildon.

## Products and solutions

- **Structural simulation** - finite element analysis and dynamic simulation.
- **Fluid and thermal simulation** - CFD and 1D thermal simulation.
- **Multi-physics and NVH** - coupled simulation, multi-body simulation and noise, vibration and harshness analysis.
- **CAD and consultancy** - design and engineering consultancy.
- **Engineers on demand** - supply of simulation specialists and CAD designers.
