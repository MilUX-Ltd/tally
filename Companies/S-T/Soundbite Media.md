# Soundbite Media

> Marketing communications business providing PR, media training, messaging and content for SMEs, the public sector and thought leaders.

## What they do

Soundbite Media provides marketing communications, including PR, media training, messaging and content, for SMEs, agencies, public sector teams and thought leaders. Its services include PR and content delivery, broadcast coaching for business leaders, mentoring and ongoing communications support, and it sells downloadable PR and marketing toolkits and a monthly marketing membership. Its business profile on Enterprise Nation gives its location as Basingstoke.

## Products and solutions

- **PR and content** - PR and content delivery, including acting as a delivery partner for marketing support.
- **Broadcast coaching** - media and broadcast coaching for business leaders and projects.
- **Public sector communications** - accessible, community-focused communications for local government and public sector teams.
- **The Comms Kit** - downloadable PR and marketing toolkits.
- **Mentoring and membership** - one-to-one mentoring and a monthly marketing membership.
