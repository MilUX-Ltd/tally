# SC Architecture

> Chandler's Ford RIBA chartered architecture practice working on commercial, MOD, modular and private residential projects.

## What they do

SC Architecture is a RIBA chartered architectural practice based in Chandler's Ford near Southampton, trading as SC Architecture since 2011 and previously the architectural arm of Stephens Cox Associates. The practice states that it provides the full range of architectural services for refurbishment and new build projects for commercial, public and private residential clients across England and Wales. It lists specialisms in the care and accessible living sector, option studies for government and commercial clients, and the procurement and installation of modular buildings. Its portfolio includes a Commercial and MOD project category.

## Products and solutions

- **Architectural design** - refurbishment and new build design for commercial, public and residential clients.
- **Option studies** - studies for government and commercial clients.
- **Modular buildings** - procurement and installation of modular build solutions.
- **Design team coordination** - assembling structural, services and cost consultants for full design solutions.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Accreditations:** RIBA chartered practice; quality assured to ISO 9001.
- **Memberships:** South Central RDSC
