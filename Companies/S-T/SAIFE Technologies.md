# SAIFE Technologies

> Trowbridge developer of sCAiPe, a runtime that checks the authority for consequential actions taken by AI, autonomous and conventional systems.

## What they do

SAIFE Technologies is developing sCAiPe Constitutional Runtime, a patent-pending layer that sits between an intended action and its execution and checks that the exact action is authorised before it takes effect, keeping evidence of what followed. Its principle is that capability is not authority: an AI agent, machine or person able to act is not thereby allowed to. The company lists defence among its application areas, including governance of AI and autonomous systems, command and control and critical national infrastructure; the technology is at production-candidate stage and not yet in live defence use.

## Products and solutions

- **sCAiPe Constitutional Runtime** - independent authorisation of consequential actions at the point of effect, with evidence for assurance.
- **MySAiFe** - an application of the same approach to personal records and continuity.
