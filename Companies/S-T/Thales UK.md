# Thales UK

> UK business of the French Thales group, working in defence, aerospace, space and cyber security, with a cyber and digital campus at Ebbw Vale in Wales.

## What they do

Thales UK Limited is the UK operating company of the Thales group, with its registered office at Green Park, Reading. Thales states it employs about 7,800 people at 16 UK sites from the South West of England to Glasgow, working across defence, cyber security and digital identity, and space. In Wales, Thales states it supports 1,100 jobs centred on its Ebbw Vale campus, built on the former steelworks site, and has about 30 staff at Aberporth working on uncrewed aerial vehicles. Ebbw Vale hosts the National Digital Exploitation Centre, delivered with the Welsh Government and the University of South Wales, the ResilientWorks living laboratory and the UK arm of Thales's Operational Technology Competence Centre.

## Products and solutions

- **National Digital Exploitation Centre (Ebbw Vale)** - cyber and digital development and education centre opened in 2019 with the Welsh Government and the University of South Wales.
- **ResilientWorks (Ebbw Vale)** - living laboratory with research laboratories, a test track and a model street for testing autonomous vehicle and power systems.
- **Operational technology cyber security** - Cyber Resilience Lab with smart energy grid and gas distribution test benches, and a UK cyber range.
- **Uncrewed aerial systems (Aberporth)** - UAV activity at the West Wales site.
- **Wider UK capabilities** - defence electronics, optronics, maritime systems, space and digital identity from sites including Crawley, Reading, Bristol, Templecombe, Cheadle, Belfast and Glasgow.
