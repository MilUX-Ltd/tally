# The Thrive Team

> West Sussex executive search, coaching and workplace training firm focused on the defence, engineering and advanced technology sectors.

## What they do

The Thrive Team describes itself as a specialist headhunter and performance coaching business for the defence, engineering and advanced technology sectors. The company states that it searches for senior leaders and technical specialists, including engineers in robotics, UAVs, software and systems engineering, and supports placements with transitional coaching. It also provides leadership coaching and workplace training. It is based in Selsey, West Sussex, and works with clients across the UK and Europe.

## Products and solutions

- **Executive search** - search for senior leaders in defence and engineering businesses.
- **Engineering and defence search** - technical specialists for R&D, robotics, UAV, software and systems engineering roles.
- **Corporate and interim search** - commercial, HR, finance, procurement and interim or fractional roles.
- **Coaching** - leadership, transitional, career and wellbeing coaching.
- **Training** - management and leadership, mental health, neurodiversity and psychological safety training.
