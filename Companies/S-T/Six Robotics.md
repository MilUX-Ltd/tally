# Six Robotics

> Oslo company building swarm autonomy software that lets unmanned systems operate together for NATO and allied forces.

## What they do

Six Robotics develops collaborative autonomy so that groups of unmanned systems act as one. Its stated mission is to give NATO and its allies an advantage in unmanned operations and to strengthen defence.
