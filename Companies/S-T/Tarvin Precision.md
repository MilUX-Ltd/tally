# Tarvin Precision

> Flintshire subcontract precision engineering company providing CNC machining and assembly for aerospace, defence, marine and scientific customers.

## What they do

Tarvin Precision is a subcontract precision engineering business at Hawarden Business Park, Flintshire, specialising in low-volume, high-variety niche engineering. It provides CNC milling, turning and 5-axis machining, together with sub and full assembly and reverse engineering. For defence work it states that parts are made at a secure, monitored facility in North Wales, with ERP-based traceability, first article reports and certificates of conformity. Its sectors include aerospace, defence, marine, scientific and medical, automotive and mining.

## Products and solutions

- **CNC machining** - milling, turning and 5-axis machining of complex parts, prototypes and batches.
- **Assembly** - sub-assembly, full assembly and clean assembly.
- **Reverse engineering** - recreation of parts from existing components.
- **Defence components** - precision machined parts for defence systems, including naval and sonar applications.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Accreditations:** AS9100 (certificate valid March 2026 to March 2029); ISO 9001; JOSCAR registered; Cyber Essentials Plus.
- **Memberships:** Defence Wales; Aerospace Wales Forum directory listing.
