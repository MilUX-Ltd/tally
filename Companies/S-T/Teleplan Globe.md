# Teleplan Globe

> Norwegian software company making battle management, intelligence management and emergency coordination systems for defence, police and emergency services.

## What they do

Teleplan Globe develops situational awareness and decision support software for defence forces, law enforcement and civil preparedness. Its products include a battle management system, a dismounted soldier system and intelligence management. It describes itself as a strategic partner to the Norwegian Armed Forces and serves NATO countries.

## Products and solutions

- **FACNAV Battle Management System** - Battle management software
- **FACNAV Dismounted Soldier System** - Soldier-level situational awareness
- **ICMT** - Intelligence management
- **NORA** - Emergency coordination for police
