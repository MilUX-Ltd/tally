# Seiche

> Devon specialist in underwater noise and marine mammal monitoring, supplying passive acoustic monitoring equipment, measurement services and training.

## What they do

Seiche Ltd describes itself as a UK specialist in underwater noise and marine mammal monitoring, providing passive acoustic monitoring (PAM) equipment and environmental services to offshore industries including oil and gas, renewables, construction and marine science. The company states it works with naval institutes and offers products for sonar applications, including an amplifier for capacitive sonar transducers and a digital thin line array. It is part of Seiche Water Technology Group, which also includes Seiche Training, AutoNaut uncrewed surface vessels and Ashridge Engineering, and operates from Bradworthy, Devon.

## Products and solutions

- **Passive acoustic monitoring systems** - towed, vertical and shore-cabled PAM, wireless and satellite remote PAM, and acoustic recorders.
- **Acoustic sensors** - hydrophones and the Hydraq QQ1000.
- **Platforms** - real-time sound measurement buoys, modular buoy systems and AutoNaut uncrewed surface vessels for PAM.
- **Scalare amplifier** - power amplifier designed to drive capacitive sonar transducers.
- **Digital thin line array** - towed array system.
- **Modelling, measurement and mitigation** - underwater noise services for offshore projects.
- **Training** - courses including marine mammal observation and underwater acoustics and sonar systems.
