# Triangula

> AI software that detects and positions gunshots using mobile phones as acoustic sensors, for defence, border control and urban security.

## What they do

Triangula is a gunshot positioning system that uses three or more mobile phones or watches running its Scout app as sensors. It reports location, weapon type, time and direction and can be integrated with command and control systems. Its site lists defence, base defence, force protection and border control among use cases, and says it was developed with support from FFI and the Norwegian Armed Forces.

## Products and solutions

- **Triangula gunshot positioning** - Detects and positions gunshots from small firearms
- **Triangula Scout app** - Turns phones into acoustic sensors
