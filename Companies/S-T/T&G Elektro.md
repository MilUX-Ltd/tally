# T&G Elektro

> Høvik manufacturer and distributor of cables, harnesses and connectors for defence, aerospace, telecommunications and energy.

## What they do

T&G Elektro makes and distributes cables, cable harnesses, connectors and fiber optic assemblies. Its defence and security page covers military-grade connectors and harnesses for vehicles, aircraft and missiles. It is certified to EN9100, ISO 9001 and ISO 14001.

## Products and solutions

- **Harness and cable** - Cable assemblies and harnesses
- **Connectors** - Including MIL-spec connectors
- **Fiber optics** - Fiber optic components and boards
