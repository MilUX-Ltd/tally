# Syncrolift

> Supplier of shiplifts, transfer systems and docking solutions for shipyards, including submarine and naval installations.

## What they do

Syncrolift supplies shiplift and transfer systems that lift vessels out of the water and move them on land. Its site reports a shiplift system for submarines for the Norwegian Navy, a submarine transfer system for thyssenkrupp and a US Navy sponsored study of its FastDocking system. It also supplies control systems, service and consulting on docking facilities.

## Products and solutions

- **Shiplift Systems** - Lift vessels out of the water for maintenance and repair
- **Transfer Systems** - Move ships on land between work areas
- **FastDocking** - Docking system covered by a US Navy sponsored study
- **Service Solutions** - Service for shiplift installations
