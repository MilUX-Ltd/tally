# Tektora

> Corsham technology and IT recruitment firm placing contract and permanent staff across the UK.

## What they do

Tektora describes itself as specialists in technology and IT recruitment, recruiting across the full spectrum of the tech industry. It provides contract and permanent recruitment across the UK, and a Plug-In and Play talent acquisition model for venture-backed start-ups that are scaling.
