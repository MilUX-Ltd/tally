# Strategic Edge Consulting

> Leamington Spa consultancy providing strategy, technology leadership and cyber advisory for defence, national security and commercial clients.

## What they do

Strategic Edge Consulting provides strategic advisory, technology leadership and decision support for organisations working in defence, security and other high-consequence environments. Its services include strategic leadership, digital transformation, fractional C-suite support, technical advisory, go-to-market strategy, and cyber security advisory and penetration testing. It also publishes briefings on cyber risk, defence delivery, digital transformation and governance.
