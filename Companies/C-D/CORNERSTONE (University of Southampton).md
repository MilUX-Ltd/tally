# CORNERSTONE (University of Southampton)

> Open-source silicon photonics rapid prototyping foundry at the University of Southampton Optoelectronics Research Centre.

## What they do

CORNERSTONE describes itself as an open source, licence-free silicon photonics rapid prototyping foundry, based at the Optoelectronics Research Centre of the University of Southampton. It offers seven photonics technology platforms for applications including telecoms, sensing, LiDAR and quantum. Services include multi-project wafer fabrication, bespoke fabrication batches, design consultancy, training courses and testing. The website shows UKRI, EPSRC and Innovate UK among its partners and funders.

## Products and solutions

- **Multi-project wafer runs** - fabrication across several silicon photonics platforms using deep-UV projection lithography.
- **Bespoke fabrication** - custom fabrication batches with flexible tape-out dates.
- **Design consultancy** - photonic integrated circuit design support.
- **Training** - courses including a PIC Bootcamp.
- **Testing** - chip-level, lightwave component analyser and wafer-scale testing.
