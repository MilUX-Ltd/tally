# Dacon

> Supplier of inspection, measurement, rescue and case equipment, with a dedicated defence solutions area.

## What they do

Dacon supplies inspection and NDT equipment, rescue equipment, lighting and protective cases to several sectors. It has a dedicated page of solutions for the defence sector including gun barrel measurement, borescopes and custom equipment cases.

## Products and solutions

- **Gun Barrel Measurement** - Barrel inspection and measurement equipment
- **Dacon Rescue Basket** - Maritime rescue equipment
