# Defendo AS

> Supplier of operational equipment, protective gear and clothing to the Norwegian Armed Forces, police and rescue services.

## What they do

Defendo supplies mission-specific equipment, clothing and protective gear. It states it is an official supplier of field equipment to the Norwegian Armed Forces. Other customers include police, rescue and fire services, shooting clubs and businesses. It also offers custom-branded apparel and printing and embroidery services.

## Products and solutions

- **Riot and protective equipment** - Riot control and protective gear
- **Ballistic plate carriers and vests** - Body protection
- **Field and load-bearing gear** - Field equipment, footwear and outdoor clothing
- **Unmanned ground vehicles** - Listed among its product range
