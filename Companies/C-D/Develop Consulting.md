# Develop Consulting

> Coventry-based Lean and operational improvement consultancy working in manufacturing, healthcare, aerospace and maintenance, repair and overhaul.

## What they do

Develop Consulting is a UK Lean and operational excellence consultancy that focuses on people and process. It works alongside client teams on Lean implementation, process optimisation, productivity improvement and workforce training. Its stated sectors include healthcare, manufacturing, construction, aerospace, rail, airport operations, logistics, maritime and MRO. The firm is based in Coventry and does not publish a site in Wales; it joined Aerospace Wales in November 2025.

## Products and solutions

- **Lean implementation and process improvement** - hands-on operational improvement programmes.
- **MRO transformation** - improvement support for maintenance, repair and overhaul operations.
- **Workforce training** - capability building, including the Click2Learn online learning product.
- **Demand and capacity optimisation** - planning support, used widely in healthcare.
