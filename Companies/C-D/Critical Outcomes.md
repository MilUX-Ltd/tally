# Critical Outcomes

> Farnham independent advisory firm on defence industrial capability for defence companies, investors and government in the UK and US.

## What they do

Critical Outcomes Ltd describes itself as an independent advisory firm serving defence companies, investors and government in the UK and US, established in 2011. It states it provides feasibility and early design studies for new defence manufacturing plant, site finding and development for hazardous and secure facilities including planning, utilities and explosives licensing, and market entry, capture and bid strategy for UK MOD and US Department of Defense programmes. The firm states that most of its work is confidential and that its principals are security cleared.

## Products and solutions

- **Feasibility studies** - feasibility and early design studies for new defence manufacturing plant.
- **Sites** - finding and developing sites for hazardous and secure facilities, including explosives licensing.
- **Strategy and bids** - market entry, capture and bid strategy for UK MOD and US Department of Defense programmes.
