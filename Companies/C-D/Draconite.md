# Draconite

> Scottish developer of a bioengineered, heat-activated thermal protection material for land, sea, air and space applications.

## What they do

Draconite is a thermal protection material developed by Fuller Three Limited, a Scottish company. The company describes it as a bioengineered, fermentation-derived material that strengthens when exposed to heat, protecting against threats above 2,000 degrees Celsius while keeping the protected side within 60 degrees of ambient, and states that it is RF-transparent and flexible at cryogenic temperatures. It lists applications across land, sea, air and space, including battery thermal runaway, naval systems, hypersonics, directed energy, drones and spacecraft re-entry. The company states that it has two UK patents pending and active evaluations across defence, space, air and land.

## Products and solutions

- **Draconite thermal protection material** - conformable, heat-activated material for extreme thermal protection.
- **Draconite design engine** - computational mission-to-material design tool, stated as in development.
