# Casper Defence

> Defence division of the Casper Group providing port agency, husbandry and logistics support to naval vessels at UK ports.

## What they do

Casper Defence provides dedicated marine and logistics services to the defence industry as part of the Casper Group of maritime services companies, which states its origins date back to 1872. The company states that it supports naval vessels at commercial ports and bases around the UK, working with Royal Navy liaison officers, port authorities and service providers. Its Defence Hub co-ordinates logistical support and UK port calls through a single point of contact available around the clock. It is based at Tees Wharf in Middlesbrough.

## Products and solutions

- **Defence Hub** - 24/7 single point of contact co-ordinating UK port calls and logistical support.
- **Logistic solutions** - logistical support to naval vessels around the UK, with group port agency, logistics and customs services.
- **Agency services** - port and husbandry services to naval vessels at UK commercial ports and bases.
