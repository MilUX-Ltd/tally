# Comrod Communication AS

> Designs and supplies antenna systems, masts and power equipment for tactical, naval and submarine use.

## What they do

Comrod designs and supplies antenna systems, masts and power equipment for communications and sensor payloads. Its product lines include tactical antennas and masts, naval antennas and systems, submarine antennas, mission and control systems, and power supplies and battery chargers.

## Products and solutions

- **Tactical antennas** - Field antenna systems.
- **Tactical masts** - Motorised telescopic and tripod sectional masts.
- **Naval antennas and systems** - Antenna systems for naval platforms.
- **Submarine antennas and systems** - Antennas for submarines.
- **Power supplies and battery chargers** - Power equipment for demanding use.
