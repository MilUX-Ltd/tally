# Charcroft Electronics

> Mid Wales specialist distributor and UK manufacturer of high-reliability electronic components for defence, aerospace, rail, audio and industrial markets.

## What they do

Charcroft Electronics Ltd is a specialist distributor of passive, interconnect, power, magnetics, optoelectronic and electromechanical components, and a manufacturer of professional passive components. Founded in 1974, it operates from Dol-y-Coed in Llanwrtyd Wells, Powys, and from Llay Industrial Estate near Wrexham, where its Industrial Capacitors Wrexham (ICW) business makes film capacitors. The company states it supplies components that meet military and NATO specifications to defence contractors and supports long-term defence programmes with obsolescence management. It reports exporting Welsh-made products to 35 countries.

## Products and solutions

- **Component distribution** - franchised and approved distribution of resistors, capacitors, magnetics, RF and microwave, electromechanical, power conversion, opto and sensor, and discrete semiconductor products.
- **Defence components** - COTS, MIL-SPEC, BS/CECC, DSCC and MIL-PRF components, with NATO Stock Number part identification.
- **Capacitor manufacturing** - custom and high-voltage metallised film capacitors made by ICW in Wrexham, plus silver mica and legacy ceramic capacitors.
- **Precision resistors** - VPG-approved Precision Centre for Bulk Metal Foil resistors, with custom passive assemblies and audio resistors.
- **Obsolescence solutions** - life-cycle planning and sourcing for long-running programmes.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Accreditations:** BS EN 9120:2018 (AS 9120); ISO 9001:2015; IECQ approved distributor; JOSCAR registered supplier; Cyber Essentials.
- **Memberships:** Defence Wales, International Institute of Obsolescence Management
