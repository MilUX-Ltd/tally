# ConMec

> Supplies connectors, mechanics and cable assemblies for professional electronics, including the defence market.

## What they do

ConMec supplies components and cable systems for professional electronics, as project work and series production. Its site states that much of its business is in broadcasting, telecom, automation, offshore, maritime, aviation and the defence market.

## Products and solutions

- **Connectors** - Contacts and connectors.
- **Cable assemblies** - Cable assembly (kabelkonfeksjon).
- **Mechanics** - Mechanical components and enclosures.
