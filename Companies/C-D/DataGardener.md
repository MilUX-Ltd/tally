# DataGardener

> Hampshire company running a UK company intelligence platform with data on over 17 million UK companies.

## What they do

DataGardener Solutions Limited operates a UK company intelligence platform that it states holds data on over 17 million UK companies, drawn from public records including Companies House. It offers intelligence products for business development, lending, procurement, government procurement, mergers and acquisitions, property and international trade, together with a company data API, CRM integrations and an MCP server for AI agents. The company was incorporated in 2020 and its registered office is in Eastleigh, Hampshire.

## Products and solutions

- **Business intelligence platform** - search, prospecting, monitoring and market research across UK company data.
- **Procurement intelligence** - supplier and government procurement intelligence.
- **Lending and credit risk intelligence** - credit risk assessment and lending intelligence.
- **M&A and property intelligence** - acquisition target research and property and land intelligence.
- **Company Data API and integrations** - API access, Salesforce and HubSpot integrations, and an MCP server for AI agents.
