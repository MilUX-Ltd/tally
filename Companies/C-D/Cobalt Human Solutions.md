# Cobalt Human Solutions

> Lincoln-based human factors consultancy offering crew resource management, human factors, team performance and wellbeing training.

## What they do

Cobalt Human Solutions describes itself as a human-focused business consultancy concerned with how individuals and teams work together to improve operational performance. It offers crew resource management training for teamwork and communication in high-pressure environments, human factors guidance on designing systems and processes around people, team and organisational performance training, and mental health and wellbeing support. The company states it works in critical environments where human factors matter.

## Products and solutions

- **Crew resource management** - teamwork and communication training for high-pressure environments.
- **Human factors** - guidance on designing systems and processes around people.
- **Team and organisational performance** - training in collaboration, leadership and strategic alignment.
- **Mental health and wellbeing** - tailored support and training.
