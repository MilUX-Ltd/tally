# Coptrz

> Leeds distributor and integrator of uncrewed aircraft systems, payloads and training for defence and security users.

## What they do

Coptrz supplies and integrates uncrewed aircraft systems for defence and security users, covering intelligence, surveillance and reconnaissance platforms, payloads, access technology, tactical mapping and long-range systems from more than 50 technology suppliers. It provides UK-based drone training, including defence courses, with maintenance and support.

## Products and solutions

- **ISR and long-range UAS** - platforms and payloads from partner manufacturers.
- **Access technology and tactical awareness** - small indoor and tactical drones, and mapping solutions.
- **Train, maintain and sustain** - defence drone training courses, maintenance and support.
