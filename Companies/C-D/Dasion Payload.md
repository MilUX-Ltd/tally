# Dasion Payload

> Shenzhen-based supplier of UAV payloads, covering electro-optical and thermal gimbal cameras, laser rangefinders, LiDAR and radar, for ISR, border and coastal patrol, inspection and public safety work.

## What they do

Dasion Payload supplies camera and sensor payloads for unmanned aerial vehicles from a base in Bao'an District, Shenzhen. The company presents itself as a supplier and sourcing partner rather than a manufacturer, offering product sourcing and export support alongside OEM and ODM integration for UAV makers and system integrators. It states that its ISR payloads are intended for "defense and security operations", and lists border security, coastal patrol and critical infrastructure protection among its applications, alongside civil work such as power line and forest inspection, firefighting, traffic patrol and search and rescue.

The company notes that some of its gimbal cameras with infrared thermal imaging are subject to export control and require a Chinese Dual-Use Item Export Licence.

## Products and solutions

- **Electro-optical and infrared gimbal cameras** - stabilised EO, thermal and MWIR imaging payloads, including combined EO-LRF and EO-IR-LRF units.
- **Laser rangefinder payloads** - high-magnification imaging combined with laser ranging for distance-aware aerial observation and measurement.
- **LiDAR** - payloads for high-precision 3D mapping and terrain survey.
- **Radar and detectors** - payloads for detection, surveillance and remote sensing.
- **Survey cameras** - ortho, oblique, hyperspectral and multispectral payloads for aerial mapping and spectral analysis.
- **Utility payloads** - lighting, speaker, firefighting, logistics and sling systems for emergency response and aerial transport.
