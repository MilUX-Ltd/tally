# Camp Supply International

> Supplies container-based camp infrastructure such as kitchens, laundry and water units, including for NATO and military use.

## What they do

Camp Supply International supplies container-based solutions for camp infrastructure, built for extreme climates. Its site states it is a chosen supplier for NATO and that its kitchen solutions can be adapted to military requirements. Units include kitchen, laundry, sanitation, water purification, laboratory, workshop and accommodation containers.

## Products and solutions

- **Kitchen containers** - Two, three and four container kitchen versions.
- **Laundry containers** - Container-based laundry units.
- **Water purification** - Container-based water purification and storage.
- **Workshop containers** - Containerised workshop units.
