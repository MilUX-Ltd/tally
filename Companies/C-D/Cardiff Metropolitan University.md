# Cardiff Metropolitan University

> Cardiff university whose School of Technologies carries out satellite communications and aerospace research and teaches computer and cyber security and aviation.

## What they do

Cardiff Metropolitan University is a university based in Cardiff, with its Cardiff School of Technologies on the Llandaff Campus. The school researches satellite communications and aerospace technologies, including compact antennas for small satellites in low Earth orbit, and works with partners such as the Satellite Applications Catapult, the UK Metamaterials Network and Airbus Endeavr. It teaches computer security and advanced cyber security courses and runs an aviation degree in partnership with Cardiff Airport and Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University.

## Products and solutions

- **Space and satellite communications research** - design and prototyping of a compact, foldable reflectarray antenna for CubeSats using printed circuit board technology.
- **BSc Computer Security** - which the university states was the first bachelor's degree in Wales to receive provisional certification from the National Cyber Security Centre.
- **MSc Advanced Cyber Security** - postgraduate course aligned to NCSC recommendations and the CyBOK framework.
- **Aviation degree** - delivered with Cardiff Airport and Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University.
- **Industry collaboration** - Knowledge Transfer Partnerships and research consortia with industry.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Memberships:** Defence Wales; co-lead of the space and aviation challenge area of the UK Metamaterials Network (through a member of staff).
