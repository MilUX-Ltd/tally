# Cold Skills

> Supplies tactical equipment and operational support to military, authorities and professional users.

## What they do

Cold Skills supplies tactical equipment for military, authorities and professional users, in categories such as clothing, weapon accessories, mobility, survival, medical supplies, protection and command and communications. It also offers operational support including procurement assistance, custom solutions, training and development.
