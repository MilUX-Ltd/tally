# Cayes AS

> Nordic provider of secure mobile, encrypted voice and chat, and device management for the public sector.

## What they do

Cayes is a Nordic enterprise security provider. It offers secure digital workspace and unified endpoint management, encrypted voice and chat, and critical event management, and names defence among the Nordic government and agency customers it serves.

## Products and solutions

- **Encrypted Voice and chat** - Secure communications
- **Critical Event Management** - Alerting and incident communication
- **BlackBerry UEM** - Unified endpoint management
