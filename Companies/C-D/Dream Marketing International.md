# Dream Marketing International

> Berkshire company running mobile exhibition roadshows that take supplier technology displays to industrial sites.

## What they do

Dream Marketing International Ltd runs industry-specific roadshows using an exhibition trailer that holds up to 24 exhibition stands, taking supplier technology displays to industrial sites to meet engineering, production and design teams. The company states that it has run roadshows for over 20 years in the automotive, defence and aerospace, rail, medical device and oil and gas industries, and that it plans an annual Defence and Aerospace Tour. It also offers the vehicle for tailored roadshows, training, presentations and hospitality, for rent or purchase.

## Products and solutions

- **Industry roadshows** - shared exhibition tours to industrial sites, including a Defence and Aerospace Tour.
- **Tailored roadshows** - exclusive use of the exhibition vehicle for a single company's tour.
