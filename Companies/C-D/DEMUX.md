# DEMUX

> Bedfordshire supplier of forensic video and audio analysis, redaction and digital investigation software, hardware and training.

## What they do

Demux Video Services Limited, trading as DEMUX, supplies specialist software, hardware and training for digital investigations, redaction and forensic multimedia analysis. The company states that it was founded by a former police detective and expert witness, has more than 25 years of experience in video and CCTV evidence, and has supplied over 100 organisations. Its tools support recovery of video and data from CCTV, DVRs, phones and computers, analysis and enhancement of video and audio, redaction of personal information, media authentication and online content capture. It lists its clients as including solicitors, barristers, police forces, local authorities, security firms, regulators and government departments.

## Products and solutions

- **Forensic video and audio analysis** - A-DAPT video analysis software.
- **Video acquisition and CCTV recovery** - E-Capture Kit and Magnet WITNESS.
- **Media authentication** - Magnet VERIFY and MeaFuse.
- **Digital, cyber and web investigations** - EVIQUIRE web capture and Magnet AXIOM Cyber.
- **Redaction and data protection** - CaseGuard redaction software.
- **Investigative interviewing** - MeaConnexus.
- **Forensic hardware** - Logic Cube forensic hardware and Faraday bags.
- **Training** - courses and on-demand training, including CaseGuard training.
