# Capytech

> Cambridge-based e-learning company building bespoke courses, branded learning platforms and AI training, including defence training to DSAT.

## What they do

Capytech UK Ltd describes itself as a Cambridge-based e-learning company that builds custom e-learning, branded learning management platforms and AI training for UK organisations, drawing on 13 years of Capytech experience in training technology. For defence and aerospace customers it states it designs modules to the Defence Systems Approach to Training (DSAT) set out in JSP 822, and names defence industry companies among its clients. Its learning platform is hosted in the UK and the company states it is Cyber Essentials certified.

## Products and solutions

- **Bespoke e-learning** - custom compliance, onboarding and induction modules, built to SCORM and xAPI standards.
- **Branded learning platforms** - learning and training management systems hosted in the UK.
- **AI training** - courses on the use of AI.
- **Virtual training environments** - 3D virtual events and training spaces.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Accreditations:** Cyber Essentials.
- **Memberships:** Greater Lincolnshire RDSC
