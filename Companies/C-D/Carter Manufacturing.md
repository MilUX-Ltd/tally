# Carter Manufacturing

> Oxfordshire supplier of precision and custom bearings and aerospace bearing tooling for aerospace, defence, space and industrial markets.

## What they do

Carter Manufacturing Ltd, trading as Carter Bearings, describes itself as a global supplier of precision bearings and aerospace bearing tooling, based near Oxford, with a US operation, Carter Americas LLC. It supplies thin section, hybrid and ceramic, cryogenic, high temperature and custom bearings, and swaging, staking, proof load and other tools for aerospace bearing installation. The company states that it has supplied precision bearings to the defence industry for over 25 years and that it became an approved supplier to the European Space Agency in 2020. It also offers bearing engineering, inspection, testing, refurbishment and training services.

## Products and solutions

- **Precision bearings** - thin section, hybrid and ceramic, cryogenic, high temperature, motorsport and custom made-to-order bearings.
- **Aerospace bearing tools** - tri-roller swaging, staking, cutting, proof load and torque test tools, including tooling for Boeing bearings.
- **Bearing services** - application engineering, inspection, performance testing, reverse engineering, failure analysis and refurbishment.
- **Training** - aerospace bearing tool training and custom tool design.
