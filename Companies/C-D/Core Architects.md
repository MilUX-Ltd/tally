# Core Architects

> RIBA Chartered architectural practice in Lincoln and Nottingham working on commercial, industrial, education and residential projects.

## What they do

Core Architects is an RIBA Chartered Practice with offices in Lincoln and Nottingham, trading through Core Plus Architects Limited. The practice states it has more than 16 years as a practice and delivers commercial, industrial, education and residential projects for public and private sector clients. Its services run from concept design through planning and statutory approvals to technical delivery. Published projects include the Hub Building at the South Lincolnshire Food Enterprise Zone and work at Lincoln Science and Innovation Park.

## Products and solutions

- **Concept design** - brief definition, feasibility studies and design development.
- **Planning and statutory approvals** - planning strategy, submissions and liaison with authorities.
- **Technical delivery** - technical design and coordination through to construction.
