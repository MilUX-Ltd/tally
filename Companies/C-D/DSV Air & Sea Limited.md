# DSV Air & Sea Limited

> UK air and sea freight company of the Danish transport and logistics group DSV, providing freight forwarding, customs clearance and aerospace logistics.

## What they do

DSV Air & Sea Limited is the UK air and sea freight forwarding company of DSV, a Danish transport and logistics group with about 75,000 people in more than 80 countries. In the UK, DSV offers air freight, sea freight, road transport, project logistics, contract logistics and customs clearance. Its aerospace logistics offer covers production logistics, aircraft engine logistics, spare parts and aircraft-on-ground (AOG) support, outsize and project cargo, and hazardous goods handling, for aircraft and engine manufacturers, MRO companies and their suppliers. The company's registered office is at Parkeston, Harwich; DSV does not publish a Welsh site for this company.

## Products and solutions

- **Air and sea freight** - international freight forwarding by air and sea.
- **Aerospace logistics** - production logistics, engine logistics, spare parts and AOG support.
- **Project and outsize logistics** - air and ship chartering and project management for large cargo.
- **Customs clearance** - customs and documentation services.
- **Road and contract logistics** - domestic and European road transport and warehousing through the wider DSV group.
