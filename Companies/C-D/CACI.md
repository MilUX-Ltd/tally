# CACI

> UK data, technology and consulting company serving defence, national security, government and commercial clients.

## What they do

CACI Limited is a UK information technology and professional services company, founded in 1982, that describes itself as building solutions using data, technology and insight. The company states it serves sectors including defence, national security, the public sector, policing, retail and financial services. Its solution areas cover data and AI, transformation and software delivery, logistics and operations (including digital forensics), and technology services such as cloud engineering, network and infrastructure, and security and DevSecOps.

## Products and solutions

- **Data and AI** - business intelligence, location intelligence, predictive analytics and a secure AI platform.
- **Cloud and engineering** - cloud engineering, secure landing zones, digital twinning and managed secure services.
- **Security and DevSecOps** - network infrastructure and security services.
- **Digital forensics** - computer forensics, lab accreditation, proficiency testing and open-source intelligence as a service.
- **Software** - products including Certa, Cygnum, Osiris and Ask Aida.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Accreditations:** ISO 9001, ISO 14001, ISO 27001:2022, ISO 45001, Cyber Essentials Plus, JOSCAR (as listed in its ADS member entry).
- **Memberships:** ADS.
