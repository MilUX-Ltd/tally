# Cottam & Brookes Engineering

> Caerphilly precision engineering company making aerospace ground support equipment, engine tooling, precision parts and assemblies.

## What they do

Cottam & Brookes Engineering is a South Wales family business, established in 1989, operating a toolroom and machine shop at Trecenydd, Caerphilly. It designs and manufactures precision parts and complex assemblies for aerospace and other industries, with design and manufacture carried out within one facility. The company specialises in ground support equipment and engine tooling for maintenance, repair and overhaul of aero engines, and lists aerospace, automotive, oil and gas, medical and power generation among the industries it serves.

## Products and solutions

- **MRO engine tooling** - manufacture and technical support of ground support and engine tooling for engine families including CFM56, GE90, RB211, GP7200, CF6, CF34, GEnx and LM2500.
- **Reconditioning** - refurbishment of existing tooling to return it to service.
- **CNC milling and turning** - multi-axis machining of parts and assemblies up to 2.2 metres.
- **EDM erosion** - wire and spark erosion for small, fine-tolerance parts.
- **Fabrication** - MIG and TIG welding of mild steel, aluminium and stainless steel.
- **Design engineering, reverse engineering and inspection** - design to manufacture, and replacement of obsolete parts.
