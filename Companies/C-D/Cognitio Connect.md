# Cognitio Connect

> London placement agency helping people leaving the UK military intelligence community move into civilian careers.

## What they do

Cognitio Connect provides job placement support, mentoring and practical help for individuals transitioning from the UK's military intelligence community, and works with employers seeking their skills. Its services are built on what it calls The Intelligence Network, a group of professionals drawn from the intelligence community, through which it offers mentoring and access to roles not advertised on public job boards. It describes itself as a candidate-focused placement agency and states that it does not post client requirements on public websites.

## Products and solutions

- **Network** - The Intelligence Network of professionals from the intelligence community.
- **Mentoring** - guidance on translating specialist skills, security clearances and career paths.
- **Placement** - matching candidates with employers, with discretion about sensitive experience.
