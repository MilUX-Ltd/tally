# Copello

> Hampshire specialist STEM recruitment business for defence, engineering and technology, cyber security and aerospace.

## What they do

Copello describes itself as a specialist STEM talent provider for defence, engineering and technology, and cyber security, and also lists aviation and aerospace among its sectors. Founded in 2018, it operates from the Spectrum Building at Solent Business Park, Whiteley. It offers contingency recruitment, exclusive multi-role hiring, work package delivery and executive search. The company trades as Copello Global Ltd.

## Products and solutions

- **Copello Core** - contingency recruitment.
- **Copello Partner** - exclusive recruitment for hiring several people.
- **Copello Project** - work package delivery for clients packaging several roles.
- **Copello Search** - executive search.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Accreditations:** JOSCAR registered; Cyber Essentials certified. Armed Forces Covenant signatory.
- **Memberships:** South Central RDSC
