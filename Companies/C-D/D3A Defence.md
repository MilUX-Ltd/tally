# D3A Defence

> Veteran-owned Wiltshire company providing subject matter expertise and training solutions to military and uniformed services.

## What they do

D3A Defence Ltd is a veteran-owned and operated company, founded in 2018, that states it develops and delivers training solutions for military and uniformed services. The company states it acts as an intermediary between suppliers, manufacturers and end users, and offers integration of live and synthetic elements for geographically distributed training. It describes its training as JSP 822 DSAT compliant and includes training needs analysis.

## Products and solutions

- **SQEP** - subject matter expertise covering operational, tactical and strategic planning.
- **Training** - training from skills acquisition to advanced application, with training needs analysis.
- **Simulation** - hardware and software agnostic simulation support integrating with existing systems.
- **Applied research and technology** - military-informed research supporting defence suppliers' training solutions.
- **Bid support** - proposal preparation and presentation support.
- **Small team training tasks** - small-group exercises building decision-making and teamwork.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Accreditations:** ISO 27001:2022 and JOSCAR registration, as listed in its ADS member entry.
- **Memberships:** ADS.
