# Canny Comms

> Porton Down communications and marketing agency working with defence and security organisations.

## What they do

Canny Comms provides communications and marketing for defence and security, covering communications and PR, marketing and content, and training and coaching. It says it works with the MOD, primes, defence and security SMEs and government teams, and its published work includes branding, CGI and animation for uncrewed systems and electronic warfare products. It also runs the Canny Chats podcast.

## Products and solutions

- **Reputation Masterclass** - an annual CIPR-accredited conference.
- **Reputation Labs** - small-group, hands-on training through the year.
- **She's the Expert and Be the Expert** - one-day workshops.
- **Canny Experts** - six to twelve month training, coaching and mentoring programmes.
