# Centerprise International

> Basingstoke IT hardware manufacturer and managed services provider supplying defence, government, health, education and enterprise customers.

## What they do

Centerprise International states that it was founded in 1983, moved into UK PC manufacturing, and opened its Basingstoke headquarters in 1990, with further operations in South Wales including a site at Ystrad Mynach. It provides managed IT services, end user compute, cloud and hosting, business continuity, IT asset lifecycle and secure disposal services, and OEM manufacturing and logistics. For defence, it states that it has longstanding relationships with the British Army, the Royal Air Force and the Royal Navy, supplying deployable ICT, secure platforms for technical training and specialist hardware, and that it can be procured through Crown Commercial Service frameworks including Technology Products and Associated Services 2 (TePAS 2). Its brands include CiCloud, CiContinuity, CiLifecycle, Centiant, Ci-OEM, Boxx Tech, CompTech Solutions and Yoyotech.

## Products and solutions

- **Modern IT and managed services** - end user compute, helpdesk, networking, storage and migration services.
- **Cloud and hosting** - UK sovereign CiCloud, private AI, and disaster recovery and backup as a service.
- **Business continuity** - continuity services and consultancy through CiContinuity.
- **IT asset management** - secure IT asset disposal, on-site data destruction, hardware audits, refurbishment and device as a service through CiLifecycle.
- **OEM services** - prototyping, manufacturing, workshop repair, and logistics and supply chain management.
- **Defence and security** - deployable integrated ICT, virtualised infrastructure, training platforms and bespoke secure hardware.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Frameworks:** Crown Commercial Service, including Technology Products and Associated Services 2 (TePAS 2).
- **Accreditations:** ISO 9001; ISO 14001; ISO 27001; ISO 45001; ISO 10002; ISO 20000-1; ISO 22301; ISO 42001; BS 15713; ADISA Standard 8.0; Cyber Essentials Plus; JOSCAR.
- **Memberships:** South Central RDSC
