# Capital Options

> Credit broker arranging equipment finance, leasing and business loans for businesses, including aerospace and industrial equipment users.

## What they do

Capital Options Limited is an equipment finance company based in Shrewsbury that describes itself as a credit broker rather than a lender. It arranges business loans, equipment finance and leasing through a range of UK and international finance providers. The sectors it lists include IT, industrial equipment, aerospace, heavy equipment, shipping and marine, and transport. Its website does not list an office in Wales.

## Products and solutions

- **Equipment leasing** - lease purchase, hire purchase, lease rental, and sale and leaseback.
- **Refinancing** - funding raised against unencumbered assets.
- **Vehicle leasing** - contract hire, contract purchase and lease rental.
- **Commercial loans** - business loans, mainly where no asset security is available.
