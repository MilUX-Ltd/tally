# Dolphitech

> Maker of ultrasonic non-destructive testing equipment using matrix array imaging, used on defence composite structures among other sectors.

## What they do

Dolphitech makes ultrasonic non-destructive testing systems that use matrix imaging to find damage and defects in critical assets. Its defence page describes inspection of composite structures on aircraft, helicopters and drones, naval vessels and armoured vehicles, including in the field. It also offers calibration, maintenance and training.

## Products and solutions

- **Dolphicam2** - Latest generation ultrasonic NDT system
- **MAUT core units and matrix array transducers** - Core imaging hardware
- **Scanning tools, automation and software** - Accessories for inspection
