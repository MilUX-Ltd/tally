# Control Freaks

> Lincolnshire control panel manufacturer and industrial automation and electrical engineering specialist.

## What they do

Control Freaks Ltd is a control panel manufacturer and electrical engineering specialist providing industrial automation and control systems across the UK. Its services include electrical design, control panel manufacture, PLC and motion control programming, system integration, robotics, SCADA and MES visualisation, condition monitoring, and installation, servicing and upgrades. It works with Siemens, Allen-Bradley, Mitsubishi and Omron technology and lists food, manufacturing, process and recycling among its sectors. The company is based at Holbeach Technology Park in Lincolnshire.

## Products and solutions

- **Control panel manufacture** - bespoke control panel design, manufacture and assembly.
- **PLC and motion control programming** - PLC, motion control, SCADA and MES software.
- **Robotics and integration** - robotics, motion control and system integration.
- **Functional safety and compliance** - CE, ATEX and PUWER compliance support.
- **Electrical contracting and maintenance** - industrial networking, installation, servicing and upgrades.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Accreditations:** ISO 9001:2015; CompEx; Mitsubishi Electric Automation Systems Approved Solutions Partner.
- **Memberships:** Greater Lincolnshire RDSC
