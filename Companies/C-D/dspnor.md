# dspnor

> Bergen company making radar signal processing, tracking, simulation and distribution products, including a drone detection radar.

## What they do

dspnor makes radar signal converters, processors, trackers, simulators and display products, plus a low-SWaP Doppler radar for drone detection. Its products are used for air and sea surveillance, and it shows at defence exhibitions such as DSEI and Eurosatory.

## Products and solutions

- **Dronnur 2D Drone Detection Radar** - Low-SWaP Doppler radar for drone detection
- **ScanView** - Radar processing framework
- **ScanServer** - Radar processor and tracker
- **ScanFaker** - Digital radar simulator
