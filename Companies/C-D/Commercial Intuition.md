# Commercial Intuition

> Gloucester consultancy delivering project and programme management, PMO, change and Odoo system implementation.

## What they do

Commercial Intuition is a consultancy that states it helps organisations with strategy, systems and change. It offers project and programme management, P3M-aligned governance and PMO implementation, business process redesign and change adoption support, and the design and build of business systems including Odoo implementation and integration. Its published client feedback includes central government, local authority and housing association work, and it markets a ready-to-deploy PMO framework pack. It gives its address as Kestrel Court, Quedgeley, Gloucester.

## Products and solutions

- **Project and programme management** - agile delivery, P3M-aligned governance and PMO implementation.
- **PMO in a Box** - governance frameworks, reporting and dashboard templates, RAID and portfolio tooling.
- **System design and build** - Odoo implementation, customisation and integration with existing systems.
- **Change and transformation** - business process redesign, change readiness and benefits realisation.
- **Ongoing support** - post-go-live support and continuous improvement.
