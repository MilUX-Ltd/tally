# Cardiff University

> Research-intensive university in Cardiff with defence-relevant research in compound semiconductors, high frequency engineering and cyber security.

## What they do

Cardiff University is a research-intensive university based in Cardiff. Its defence-relevant research includes compound semiconductors, where it develops gallium nitride integrated circuit technology for RF communications and leads a doctoral training centre in compound semiconductor manufacturing, and high frequency engineering, where its Centre for High Frequency Engineering researches communications, sensors and materials for security, energy and health. Its Centre for Cyber Security Research is recognised by the National Cyber Security Centre as an Academic Centre of Excellence in Cyber Security Research. The university is a member of the Ministry of Defence-led Defence Universities Alliance and is part of the South Wales compound semiconductor cluster.

## Products and solutions

- **Centre for High Frequency Engineering** - research and microwave characterisation facilities for RF and microwave components, with measurement up to 67 GHz.
- **Compound semiconductor research** - gallium nitride integrated circuit research and a doctoral training centre in compound semiconductor manufacturing.
- **Centre for Cyber Security Research** - NCSC-recognised cyber security research centre.

## Frameworks, accreditations and memberships

- **Accreditations:** NCSC Academic Centre of Excellence in Cyber Security Research (renewed 2024); NCSC Academic Centre of Excellence in Cyber Security Education.
- **Memberships:** Defence Wales; Defence Universities Alliance.
