# D7 Advisers

> Hampshire-based boardroom advisory firm offering director and board advice, training and reviews, with sector advice to UK defence transport and logistics.

## What they do

D7 Advisers Ltd states that it improves director and board performance through advising, training, coaching and board effectiveness reviews, in person across Hampshire, Berkshire, Surrey and London and online across the UK. It works with private, public and not-for-profit organisations, and its approach is based on the seven statutory duties of a company director. The company lists sector advisory services covering strategic advice and support to UK defence transport and logistics, transport and highways, and the not-for-profit sector.

## Products and solutions

- **Board advising** - independent support to directors and boards.
- **Board training and coaching** - in-person and online boardroom training and director development.
- **Board effectiveness reviews** - limited, comprehensive or targeted reviews for SMEs.
- **Sector advisory** - strategic advice to UK defence transport and logistics, transport and highways, and not-for-profit organisations.
