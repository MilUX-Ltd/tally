# DK NEJET

> Trondheim company developing software-first autonomous drone systems for security and defence.

## What they do

DK NEJET develops mission-level control software and swarm intelligence for unmanned aircraft. Its site describes computer vision for guidance in GNSS-denied areas, jam-resistant data links and a common ground control layer for mixed fleets.
