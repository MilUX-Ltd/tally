# Clear Cut Cyber

> London cyber security consultancy providing threat modelling, risk management, compliance, cyber exercising and CISO services.

## What they do

Clear Cut Cyber provides tailored cyber security services covering threat modelling, risk management, strategy and implementation, compliance and assurance, cyber exercises and an outsourced CISO service. The company says it works with clients to understand what they value and needs protecting, and helps them build cyber defence capabilities across the organisation. It lists charitable organisations, the public sector, engineering and industrial firms, and commercial enterprises among the sectors it serves.

## Products and solutions

- **Threat Modelling**, **Risk Management**, **Strategy and Implementation**, **Compliance and Assurance** - advisory services.
- **Cyber Exercising** - exercises to test how organisations respond to security events.
- **CISO Service** - outsourced chief information security officer support.
