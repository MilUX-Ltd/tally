# Cre8tive Space

> Farnborough design and build company delivering secure facilities, offices and specialist technical environments for defence, aerospace and government clients.

## What they do

Cre8tive Space Limited describes itself as a UK specialist in the design, construction and fit-out of secure facilities for organisations in defence, aerospace, government, finance, technology and national security. The company states it delivers classified and non-classified environments, including Facility Security Clearance (formerly List X) facilities, secure offices, laboratories, communications rooms and data centres, from concept and security planning through to handover. It also provides office design and build and specialist shielding projects, and is based at Cody Technology Park, Farnborough.

## Products and solutions

- **Secure facilities** - design and build of Facility Security Clearance and restricted-access environments using NPSA guidance.
- **Specialist shielding** - RF, TEMPEST, electromagnetic, EMP, blast, radiation and acoustic protection.
- **Office design and build** - workplace design, construction and fit-out.
- **Specialist projects** - laboratories, control rooms, data centres and technical spaces, including CIDA-accredited cabling installations.
