# CXC

> Swindon consultancy providing programme and service management, integrated logistics support, security and specialist consultants.

## What they do

CXC, the trading name of Company X Consulting Ltd, describes itself as an SME consultancy with strong social values that provides consultancy specialists, outcomes and services. Its services include business analysis, business continuity, service integration and management, portfolio, programme and project management, integrated logistics support, commercial and procurement support, bid support and training development. Its security work covers information and cyber security and technical surveillance countermeasures, and it runs career transition support for service leavers and reservists.
