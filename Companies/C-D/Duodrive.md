# Duodrive

> Micro SME developing contra-rotating electric propulsion for marine vessels.

## What they do

Duodrive Limited describes itself as a start-up micro SME developing intellectual property in electric marine propulsion, for new build and retrofit applications intended to cut vessel carbon emissions towards zero-emission operation. It states that it leads an Innovate UK funded research and development project and is active within the net zero innovation cluster centred on Tees Valley. Its propulsion concepts are named Duodrivetrain and Motodrivetrain.

## Products and solutions

- **Duodrivetrain and Motodrivetrain** - contra-rotating electric propulsion concepts for marine vessels.
