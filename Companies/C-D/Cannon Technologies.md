# Cannon Technologies

> Hampshire designer and manufacturer of server racks, data cabinets, outdoor enclosures and modular data centres.

## What they do

Cannon Technologies Ltd designs and manufactures data cabinets and metal enclosure systems used in data networking, telecommunications, electronics and IT. The company states it was established in 1978 with the objective of developing activity for the defence and commercial sectors, and it makes both standard and custom designs at New Milton in Hampshire. Its range covers 19 inch server cabinets, aisle containment, integrated outdoor enclosures, power distribution, cooling and micro and modular data centres. It also offers high-security enclosures for protecting communications and surveillance equipment at critical infrastructure sites.

## Products and solutions

- **Server racks and cabinets** - SMART-X and EPIC-X 19 inch server cabinet ranges, frames and accessories.
- **Integrated outdoor enclosures** - indoor and outdoor racks and enclosures for communications equipment.
- **Modular and micro data centres** - modular data centre systems, including AI-ready micro data centres.
- **DualDefence** - fire-rated, security-rated double-walled protection enclosure for communications and electronic equipment.
- **Data centre infrastructure** - aisle containment, cooling, power distribution and installation and support services.
