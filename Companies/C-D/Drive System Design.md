# Drive System Design

> Leamington Spa engineering consultancy specialising in electrified propulsion and actuation systems, including for defence vehicles.

## What they do

Drive System Design Limited, also known as DSD, is an engineering consultancy based in Leamington Spa that is described in trade press as an electrified propulsion and actuation systems specialist. For defence it works with tracked and wheeled vehicle manufacturers and suppliers on subsystem design, analysis and validation through to powerpack integration. The company made its UK debut at DSEI in September 2023 and has a presence in the United States.

## Products and solutions

- **Defence vehicle propulsion** - subsystem design, analysis and validation, and powerpack integration.
- **Open Platform Inverter** - a power electronics product for developing motor control systems.
- **Transmission and driveline engineering** - hybrid and electric transmission design, control software and test facilities.
