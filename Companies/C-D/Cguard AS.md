# Cguard AS

> Kristiansund company developing and manufacturing ROV systems for maritime defence, mine detection and clearance.

## What they do

Cguard develops and manufactures ROV systems for maritime defence and infrastructure missions, and supplies military forces. Its systems cover reconnaissance, mine detection and mine clearance and disposal, and it takes part in the REPMUS exercise.

## Products and solutions

- **Black Mamba** - Portable reconnaissance ROV for detecting foreign objects
- **Yellow Tail** - ROV that detects and neutralises mines
