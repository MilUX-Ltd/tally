# ComPower AS

> Bergen-area supplier of UPS systems, aids to navigation and power electronics for ships and rigs.

## What they do

ComPower designs UPS systems, aids to navigation and power electronics for ships and offshore rigs. Its references state it has supplied UPS systems to most of the Norwegian Navy's vessels, including the Coast Guard, frigates and corvettes.

## Products and solutions

- **UPS systems** - Module-based UPS for ships and rigs
- **Aids to Navigation** - Navigation aid units for marine use
