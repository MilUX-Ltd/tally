# Carry Gently

> Aldershot provider of specialist transport, IT relocation and secure warehousing for high-value and delicate equipment.

## What they do

Carry Gently describes itself as an independent provider of specialist transport solutions and IT relocation services, with 40 years' experience in the specialist IT and technology transport sector. It moves IT hardware and media, medical, scientific and laboratory equipment, telecoms infrastructure, test systems and broadcast equipment using GPS-tracked vehicles with load compartment CCTV. It runs secure warehouses in Aldershot and Manchester and also offers worldwide freight forwarding and project management for complex moves.

## Products and solutions

- **Specialist transport** - purpose-built vehicles for high-value, delicate loads, with delivery to point of use.
- **Freight forwarding** - door-to-door air and sea freight for overseas moves.
- **Secure warehousing** - short and longer-term storage with CCTV.
- **Project management** - a dedicated project manager for major or complex moves.
