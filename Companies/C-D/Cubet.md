# Cubet

> Lincolnshire leadership coaching and business development consultancy.

## What they do

Cubet Ltd provides leadership coaching, training and development for businesses. Its services cover leadership and management, change management, personal development, team and behavioural development, and mindset and culture, delivered through coaching and tailored workshops. The company states that it uses DISC behavioural insights as an associate of DISC Simple. Its registered office is in Gainsborough, Lincolnshire.

## Products and solutions

- **Leadership and management** - coaching and training for leaders.
- **Change management** - support to plan and deliver change.
- **Team and behavioural development** - team workshops and DISC behavioural profiling.
- **Personal development** - tailored individual programmes.
